Home, auto, marine, aircraft and church upholstery.

If it has a seat, a cushion or a cover, we can strip it and do it again.

  • Sofas, dining seats, cushions and the other pieces you live with.

    Send: The whole piece from the front, a close-up of the worn part, and the width.

  • Boat cushions and cockpit seating.

    Send: Each cushion laid flat, a close-up of the cover, and how many there are.

  • Car seats and interiors, re-covered and restitched.

    Send: The seats from the open door, the damage up close, and the make and year.

  • Aircraft seating and cabin upholstery.

    Send: One seat front and side, the damage up close, and the seat count.

  • Pews and church seating, done as a set.

    Send: One pew front and side, its length, and how many pews.
