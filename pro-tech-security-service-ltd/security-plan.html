Where is Pro-Tech Security?

At #2 Farrington Road & Gregory Street, Nassau (P.O. Box CB-13083). The office is open Monday to Friday, 9 am to 5 pm.

Pro-Tech runs 24/7 central station monitoring, so a monitored alarm is watched around the clock, including weekends.
