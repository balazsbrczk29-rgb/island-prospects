Practical answers.

How fast can you build my site?
Simple web pages are normally ready within 24 hours of the order. Sites with several pages or complex features take a little longer, and we tell you when you order.
Can you take over and redesign the site I already have?
Yes. We can work hourly if the changes are small, or take over the site, redesign it and keep it up to date for you.
Can you register and host my domain?
Yes. If the domain is available we register it and host it, with a simple control panel you can use yourself.
How can I pay?
Cash (not by mail), USD or B$ cheques, bank-to-bank or direct deposit, and credit cards through PayPal.
What does it cost?
Every job is quoted for what you need. Sites over ten pages get a discount, and the first look at your search key phrases is free.
