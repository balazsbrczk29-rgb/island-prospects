1996

Two entrepreneurs in mid 1996: one took the graphics, the other the code.

Their first multi-page site, the Bahamas Vacation Guide, went to the top of the search engines. Businesses asked to be included, then asked for sites of their own.

The guide gave the studio its name: BVG stands for Bahamas Vacation Guide. It is still online, its pages signed "Designed and Maintained by BVG Design".
