01Since 1996

Two founders, one guide, thirty years online.

In mid-1996 two entrepreneurs split the job of getting online: one took the graphics, the other the code. Their first multi-page site, the Bahamas Vacation Guide, climbed to the top of the search engines.

Businesses started asking to be included and to have pages made for them. The studio got its name from the guide: BVG stands for Bahamas Vacation Guide.

02What we do

Six ways we put you online.

Personal service, and no account too small or too large. Every job is quoted for what you need.

  • 01

    From a single clear page to themed multi-page sites with contact areas, graphics and the detail your customers ask for. Complex builds with JavaScript and animation when the job needs it. Discounts on sites over ten pages, and more on sites in the hundreds.

  • 02

    Pages written and built so search engines understand who you are, what you sell and which island you are on. It is how the studio started.

  • 03

    For a site that already exists but nobody finds. It starts with a free first look at the key phrases you use now and whether they fit what you want.

  • 04

    We register your domain if it is available and host it, with a simple control panel anyone can use.

  • 05

    Hourly help for small changes, or we take over your existing site, redesign it and keep it up to date.

  • 06

    Campaigns beyond the web: print layouts and printed materials, video ads for YouTube, and disc production with printed labels.
