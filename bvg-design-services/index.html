1996

Two entrepreneurs, one afternoon in mid 1996.

One took the graphics, the other the code. A month later their multi-page Bahamas Vacation Guide went straight to the top of the search engines, ahead of the established Bahamas guides.

Businesses started asking to be included and for pages of their own. The guide gave the studio its name: BVG stands for Bahamas Vacation Guide. It is still online, still designed and maintained by BVG.
