02Since 1965

Sixty years below the surface.

"The exploration of the seas is not the private domain of the scientist, nor the industrialist, nor the military, nor the diving sportsman. Its pursuit belongs to all people." Al Tillman, UNEXSO, 1966.

Dive log

1965

  1. 1965

    UNEXSO opens

    One of the first organisations to offer recreational scuba diving in The Bahamas, and the prototype for the modern dive resort.

  2. 1968

    Lloyd Bridges visits

    Original staff Al Tillman, Dave Woodward and Jack McKenney with Lloyd Bridges.

  3. 1970

    Buzz Aldrin dives

    A few months after walking on the Moon. The same year, Peter Benchley completes his certification here and sees his first shark.

  4. 1971

    The Nemo

    A vertical submersible, used as an underwater elevator for non-divers and by the US Navy for testing.

  5. 1972

    The Hydrolab

    An underwater habitat installed a mile off Grand Bahama to study undersea ecology, biology, geology and medicine.

  6. 1975

    Ben's Cave

    Ben Rose dives the cave that carries his name.

  7. 1982

    Theo's Wreck

    Intentionally sunk as an artificial reef.

  8. 1987

    The Dolphin Experience

    The region's first, and a new shark dive the same year.

  9. 1991

    Shark Encounter course

    Ben Rose's groundbreaking course.

  10. Today

    Sanctuary Bay

    UNEXSO Lab for education and research, coral restoration with the Perry Institute for Marine Science, and the diving programme relaunching.

03UNEXSO Lab

Education, research and a reef to replant.

The UNEXSO Lab at Sanctuary Bay supports in-house research, education and training, with a focus on native wildlife. With the Perry Institute for Marine Science, UNEXSO plants coral domes to support reef restoration, and guests can see the work up close.
