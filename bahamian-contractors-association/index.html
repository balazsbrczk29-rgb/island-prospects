01 · What we do

Protect, educate, and win work for contractors.

01Advocacy

  • The official voice of the construction industry in The Bahamas.
  • At the table in Economic Partnership Agreement talks with the EU, Caricom and others.
  • Drove the Construction Contractors Act of 2016, and presses for its Board to be appointed.

02Education

  • Training seminars and certification programmes.
  • The Contractor Seminar Series, with international partners.
  • Support for standard testing and licensing, and awards for excellent work.

03Growth

  • Invitations to bid on government and private projects.
  • Local firms promoted to international companies coming to The Bahamas.
  • Monthly luncheons, events, industry reports and market insight.

02 · The Contractors Act

What your dues have built.

The BCA drove the Act that makes a Construction Contractors Board possible, and keeps pressing for the Board to be appointed.

  1. 1959

    The BCA is established

    The leading organisation advocating for construction professionals in The Bahamas.

  2. 2016

    Construction Contractors Act passed

    A significant achievement driven by the BCA, making the Construction Contractors Board, a key regulatory body, possible.

  3. Now

    The Board, still to be appointed

    The BCA urges the timely appointment of the Construction Contractors Board that the Act mandates.
