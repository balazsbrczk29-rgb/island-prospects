Good to know

Before you book.

Your rental is approved for use within a set boundary around Abaco: the Sea of Abaco from Green Turtle Cay down to Little Harbour, inside the outer cays. It keeps you in safe, familiar water, close to fuel, help and cell service. The Atlantic side is a no-go area. If you have a route in mind, ask and the team will confirm it is in range.
