02  Our story

One recipe, one shop, the same street.

  1. 2000George Bates, originally from the United Kingdom, opens a small rum cake shop in downtown Nassau with his Original recipe.
  2. 2014The shop's cake is recognised as the Official Rum Cake of The Bahamas.
  3. TodayStill in the same location it started, with master baker Simon Smith, a range of flavours, and cakes shipped from Nassau.
