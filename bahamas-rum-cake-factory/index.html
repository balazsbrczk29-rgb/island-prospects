One recipe, one downtown shop, since 2000.

George Bates came to Nassau from the UK and created the Original recipe in a shop downtown in 2000. The factory has baked in the same place ever since.

In 2014 it was recognised as the Official Rum Cake of The Bahamas. Every cake is marinated in Ole Nassau Bahamian Rum.
