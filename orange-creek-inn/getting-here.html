How to reach Orange Creek Inn, Cat Island

One short flight, then three and a half miles.

Most guests fly to Nassau and take a domestic flight on to Cat Island. From Florida there is a direct flight four days a week. Either way, the last stretch is a quiet road north from Arthur’s Town Airport.

What guests ask before they come.

Which airport is nearest?

Arthur's Town Airport, about 3.5 miles from the inn. A taxi is about $15, or we can collect you.

Do I need a car?

Cars and bicycles are both available and worth having for the beaches and settlements along the island. A car is about $80 a day and can be waiting at the airport. Bicycles are $10 a day.

Is the inn close to the beach?

It is directly across the road from a white-sand beach, for swimming, fishing or doing nothing.

Where do we eat?

Your studio has a stove-top kitchenette and the grocery store is on site. There are about six restaurants nearby serving Bahamian food, at $12 to $18 for an average meal.

Is the fishing good?

Some of the best bonefishing flats on Cat Island are close to the inn, and there is saltwater fishing too.

When is the best time to come?

Cat Island is a year-round island. December to April are the most popular months.

How do I pay?

Visa or MasterCard, or cash when you arrive.
