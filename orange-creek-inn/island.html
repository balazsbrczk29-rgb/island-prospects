Getting here · Cat Island

Nassau or Fort Lauderdale, then 3.5 miles.

Most guests fly to Nassau and connect to Cat Island; others fly direct from Fort Lauderdale Executive with Makers Air. Arthur’s Town Airport is the closest, about 3.5 miles from the inn.

01On Cat Island

Quiet beaches, flats and a blue hole.

Cat Island is one of the least crowded islands in The Bahamas. December to April is the most popular season, but it is a year-round island.

  1. 01Bonefishing and saltwater fishingThe inn is near some of Cat Island’s bonefishing flats.

  3. 03BicyclingRent a bicycle at the inn for $10 a day.
  4. 04SightseeingBlue Hole, Port Royal Beach and Fernandez Bay.
