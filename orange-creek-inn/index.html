A grocery store, laundry and an ice machine. Cars for about $80 a day, waiting at the airport if you like, and bicycles for $10.

The top of Cat Island, at walking pace.

Orange Creek is where a creek runs out to the sea, with white sandbars at low water and bonefishing flats nearby. Guests write about turtles, stingrays and bonefish in the creek.

  • On the water Bonefishing, saltwater fishing, kayaking and boat rides.
  • Along the island The Blue Hole, Port Royal Beach, Fernandez Bay, straw markets and sightseeing tours.
  • Dinner out About six restaurants nearby serve Bahamian food, $12 to $18 a meal.
