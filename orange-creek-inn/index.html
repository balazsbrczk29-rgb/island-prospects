Dear traveller,

The beach is across the road.

Orange Creek Inn sits at the top of Cat Island, just north of Arthur’s Town: studios with their own kitchenettes, a grocery store on site, and white sand the width of one quiet road away.

Where the creek runs out to the sea.

Orange Creek is the settlement immediately north of Arthur’s Town. The inn is a white two-storey building with the beach in front of it and the creek a walk away. Cat Island is one of the least crowded islands in the Bahamas, and up here you will often have the sand to yourself.

December to April are the most popular months. The airport is 3.5 miles down the road, and we can come and collect you.

What things cost up here.

The grocery store, the laundry and the ice machine are on the property. We rent cars and bicycles, and a car can be waiting at the airport when you land. Around us there are about six restaurants, all cooking Bahamian food.

Days here are mostly water.

Some of Cat Island’s best bonefishing flats are close by. There is saltwater fishing, kayaking, bicycling, boat rides and sightseeing tours, the straw markets, and the Blue Hole, Port Royal Beach and Fernandez Bay to drive to.
