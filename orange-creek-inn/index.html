01The inn

Two storeys, one road, then white sand.

Orange Creek Inn sits directly across the road from a white-sand beach, at the quiet north end of Cat Island. Downstairs there is a store for groceries, a laundry and an ice machine. The inn rents cars and picks guests up at the airport.

It suits couples, families, anglers and anyone who wants an uncrowded island and a kitchenette of their own.
