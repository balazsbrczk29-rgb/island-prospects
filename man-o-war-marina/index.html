Twenty-three slips, and seven feet of water under you at low tide.

Man-O-War Marina has served this harbour since 1976: locals, marina guests and boats in from off the island. Hurricane Dorian did great damage in 2019. The marina rebuilt, and the docks, the fuel pumps, the restaurant and the shop are serving boats again.

Slips take vessels up to 120 feet long with beams up to 20 feet, and drafts to 7 feet even at low tide. Every slip has 30 and 50 amp power and fresh water. Call on channel 16 as you come in and the dock staff will help you alongside.

A day at the marina

Monday to Saturday, by the hours the marina keeps. The line shows where the day is now, in Bahamas time.

  1. 8:00 am

    The fuel dock and the office open. Gasoline and diesel alongside; the office and gift shop share one room.

  2. 9:00 am

    The roadside pump opens for drivers on the island.

  3. 11:00 am

    Dock N’ Dine starts lunch, local and international cooking on the water.

  4. 12:00 pm

    The office and both pumps break for lunch.

  5. 1:00 pm

    The fuel dock and the office reopen.now

  6. 2:00 pm

    The roadside pump reopens.

  7. 3:00 pm

    Lunch service ends.

  8. 4:00 pm

    The office and the fuel dock close for the day.

  9. Evening

    For marina guests: showers and restrooms, laundry, the grill area and the pool.

Everything ashore is a short walk down the boardwalk.

Dock N’ Dine serves lunch, dinner and ice cream with the harbour in front of you, to marina guests, islanders and off-island visitors. The office doubles as the gift shop: custom t-shirts, local art and marine grade dive gear.

Man‑O‑War Cay is two miles long and half a mile wide.

From the marina you walk to the grocery, the Heritage Museum and its coffee shop, the bakeries, the boat-building shops, the churches, the playground, the beaches and the ferry.
