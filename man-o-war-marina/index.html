Man-O-War Marina: Twenty-threeslips in aboatbuilders’ harbour.

Man-O-War Marina sits on the sheltered harbour of a small Abaco cay, with the island’s only fuel dock, a waterfront restaurant and slips for yachts to 120 ft.

02The harbour

Since 1976, and rebuilt after Dorian.

Man-O-War Marina Village has served locals, marina guests and off-island visitors since 1976. Hurricane Dorian tore through Abaco in 2019. The marina rebuilt, and kept serving the boaters who keep coming back.

It is the only fuelling station on the island, for boats at the dock and carts on the road.

04On the dock

Tie up, then walk to lunch.

The cay is two miles long and half a mile wide, with a grocery, the heritage museum and coffee shop, boatbuilding shops, beaches and the ferry.
