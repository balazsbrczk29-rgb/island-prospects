Slips, fuel, lunch and a hot shower, all on one dock.

Everything Man‑O‑War Marina offers, with the hours it keeps. To book a slip, call (242) 554-9500 or send a request.

Dockage

Twenty-three slips for vessels up to 120 feet long and up to 20 feet in beam.

Drafts to 7 feet, even at low tide.

30 and 50 amp power and water at every slip.

Assisted docking: call on VHF channel 16.

Dockage rates (PDF)

Fuel, dockside and roadside

The only fuelling station on the island, for boaters and drivers.

Gasoline and diesel.

Dockside: Monday to Saturday, 8 to 12 and 1 to 4.

Roadside: Monday to Saturday, 9 to 12 and 2 to 4.

Fuel prices move with the market; ask the office for today’s.

Dock N’ Dine

The waterfront restaurant: local and international cooking for marina guests, islanders and off-island visitors.

Lunch, dinner or ice cream with a harbour-side view.

Lunch Monday to Saturday, 11 to 3.

Office and gift shop
