02Fuel and hours

The only fuel on the island.

Gasoline and diesel for boats at the dock and for drivers at the roadside pump. Fuel prices move with the market; ask the office for today’s price.

  • Marina office and gift shopMon-Sat 8am-12pm, 1pm-4pm
  • Dockside fuelMon-Sat 8am-12pm, 1pm-4pm
  • Roadside fuelMon-Sat 9am-12pm, 2pm-4pm
  • Dock N' Dine, lunchMon-Sat 11am-3pm

03Facilities

Who can use what.

Guest facilities are for boats staying at the marina. Boats on moorings, at anchor outside the harbour or passing through are welcome at the restaurant, the shop and the fuel dock.

Marina guests only

  • Clean restrooms
  • Spacious showers
  • Laundry facilities
  • Grill area
  • Pool
  • Power at the slip

Open to everyone

  • Dock N’ Dine restaurant
  • Office and gift shop
  • Dockside and roadside fuel
  • Water tank fill, on request
