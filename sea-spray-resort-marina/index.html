01The marina

Tie up between the Atlantic and the Sea of Abaco.

A newly renovated marina in White Sound, sheltered on Elbow Cay. 68 slips take boats from 20 to 130 feet, with up to 7 feet of draft even at low tide, plus electricity, water, ice and restrooms on the dock.

03The story

Named for a boat. Rebuilt by boaters.

  1. 1980sMonty Albury and his wife Ruth start building Sea Spray boats on Elbow Cay. The business grows into a resort and marina.
  2. 2019Hurricane Dorian destroys the property.
  3. 2022In February, Charleston boyhood friends Francis Johnson, Harry Johnson, Donovan Smith and Mills Buxton, with their families, begin bringing Sea Spray back.
  4. TodayA renovated marina and a new bar are open; cottages and residences follow in phases.
