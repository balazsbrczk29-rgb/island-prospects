Out

From here the Sea of Abaco opens wide. Marsh Harbour is twenty minutes by boat, Tahiti Beach is closer still, and the offshore banks and the Marls are within easy reach.

Back

Fuel at the dock, gas and diesel. Ice, and bait: ballyhoo, chum, squid and shrimp. Showers are ready when you get in.

The bar

Just up from the docks, where most afternoons naturally lead. Order food online.

Thursday

Live music, a DJ or Junkanoo on Thursday nights. Happy Hour, Game Night and Karaoke through the week.

Ashore

Stay on land as well as on the water. Book a room or villa.

Monty and Ruth Albury built Sea Spray boats here in the 1980s. After Hurricane Dorian, four boyhood friends from Charleston and their families began bringing it back.
