The basin

The harbour
Not a conventional marina: a working, living harbour at the centre of a non-gated village, open to visitors by land and by sea.

Published position N 26° 09.529′, W 077° 10.633′. Call on VHF 16, working channel 77, and the Harbour Master will bring you in.
