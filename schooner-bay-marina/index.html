02The harbour

A harbour with
a village around it.

The marina calls it what it is: a working, living, recreational harbour, the centre of a village. Owners and passing boats share the basin, and nobody is gated out.

The harbour village, Schooner Bay

Closest marina to Hole in the Wall, on the run between the Abaco Cays and Nassau.

The famed sport fishing grounds are the reason many boats come south. A protected basin is the reason they sleep well when they get here: fuel at the dock, 30 and 50 amp power, showers, Wi-Fi and the General Store a few steps away.
