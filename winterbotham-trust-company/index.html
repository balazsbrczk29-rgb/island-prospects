02 · Heritage

Two centuries of a family name.

Local government, missions and railway engineering: the Winterbothams were pioneers in public life long before the family name came to Nassau, carried by values of honesty and integrity.

  1. 1855 to 1912

    James Batten Winterbotham

    Of Cheltenham, Gloucestershire. The founder is his direct descendant.

  2. 1920 to 1923

    Clara Winterbotham

    Mayoress of Cheltenham.

  3. East Africa

    Cicely Hooper

    A pioneering missionary, alongside her husband Handly Hooper.

  4. South America

    George Washington Winterbotham

    Railway engineer, laying the foundations of railways across the continent.

  5. Nassau

    Geoffrey Hooper

    After a long career in international banking, founds The Winterbotham Trust Company Limited.
