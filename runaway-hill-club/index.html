From the house to the sand.

  1. The Main HouseA one-and-a-half-storey manor from 1946 on one of the highest points of the island: the lobby, the bar and the restaurant, with views over the ocean.
  2. The poolThe tiled pool and its sun deck step down from the house.
  3. The staircaseThe stairs run on down to the beach, where loungers, umbrellas, kayaks and paddleboards wait.

Dinner on the hill.

The restaurant and bar sit in the Main House with views over the ocean. Room service in your room, and a full concierge for everything else.

Weddings and celebrations.

The inn hosts weddings and special events, and its events team sees to every detail. With the Infinity House next door, the whole party can stay together.

Write to the events team

Days on the island.

Unwind on one of the world’s most beautiful beaches, below the house. Water sports, boutique shops, and a massage, yoga or fitness class when you book one.
