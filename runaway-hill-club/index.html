01 The house

From the hall
to the sand.

The inn is a walk downhill. Scroll and take it: four stops, about thirty feet, from the manor house to the beach.

  1. Stop 1 of 4

    The Main House

    A one-and-a-half-storey manor from 1946, with five rooms upstairs and down. The lobby, bar and restaurant look out across the ocean.

  2. Stop 2 of 4

    The pool and sun deck

    A few steps down from the house: the tiled pool and its deck, the patio where evenings start.

  3. Stop 3 of 4

    The staircase

    Down through the garden on the hill, around thirty feet above the beach.

  4. Stop 4 of 4

    Pink Sands Beach

    175 feet of the inn's own beachfront, with loungers, umbrellas, kayaks and paddle boards waiting.
