Getting to Port Howe.

Cat Island is a short flight from Fort Lauderdale or Nassau into New Bight (CAT). From the airport, an island taxi or a rental car brings you to the door.

Plan the route

The route

  1. Fort Lauderdale Executive (FXE)

    MakersAir flies to New Bight (CAT) several times a week, leaving at 7am or 1pm depending on the date.

    Greenwood guests get 10% off the fare: ask Greenwood for the promo code.

  2. New Bight, Cat Island (CAT)

    Return flights to FXE leave at 10:20am or 4:20pm, depending on the date.

  3. Port Howe

    Greenwood Beach Resort, 1 Ocean Drive, at the south end of the island.

From the airport
