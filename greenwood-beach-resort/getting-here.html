Getting here · New Bight (CAT) · Port Howe

How to reachCat Island.

Cat Island is a little different from the usual Bahamian getaway. Fly to New Bight, then take a local taxi or a rental car south to Greenwood Beach Resort.

01In the air

Two ways to fly in.

MakersAir

Fort Lauderdale Executive (FXE) to New Bight (CAT)

  1. Flights from Fort Lauderdale Executive to New Bight several times a week.
  2. Outbound at 7am or 1pm, return at 10:20am or 4:20pm, depending on the date.
  3. Resort guests get 10% off the airfare: ask the resort for the promo code.
  4. From New Bight Airport, a local taxi or a rental car brings you south to Port Howe.

02On the ground

Taxi or your own wheels.

Local drivers know the island well and bring you from the airport to the door. A rental car lets you explore Cat Island at your own pace.
