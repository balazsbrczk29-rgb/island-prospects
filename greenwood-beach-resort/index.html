05Getting here

The journey is part of it.

Taxis and car rental

MakersAir

Fort Lauderdale Executive (FXE) to New Bight (CAT)

  1. Flights from Fort Lauderdale Executive to New Bight several times a week.
  2. Outbound at 7am or 1pm, return at 10:20am or 4:20pm, depending on the date.
  3. Resort guests get 10% off the airfare: ask the resort for the promo code.
  4. From New Bight Airport, a local taxi or a rental car brings you south to Port Howe.
