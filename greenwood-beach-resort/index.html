Deep blue

From March to June, oceanic whitetip sharks visit these waters. Guided boat dives, small groups, and respect for the animal.

The reef

A snorkel over a small reef, then a secluded beach. The island's only sea-accessible bat cave is reached by boat.

Table

Breakfast eight to ten. Lunch noon to two. Dinner six to eight. Dinner is booked a day ahead, on the terrace by the beach.

Night

Oceanic whitetip off Cat Island. Photograph: Pauline Vaz Branco.

Every spring, oceanic whitetip sharks come to the deep blue water off Cat Island. Greenwood runs guided boat dives with experienced dive professionals, in small groups, following responsible practice for a critically endangered species.

Packages combine your nights, the number of whitetip dive days, and half or full board. Tell Pauline and Antoine what you have in mind; they reply with availability and rates.
