How a Gunite pool is built

One seamless shell, built in five steps.

  1. 01DesignWe design it with you, or with your architect, structural engineer, designer or main contractor.
  2. 02Dig and steelThe ground is excavated and a cage of steel is tied to the exact shape of the pool.
  3. 03GuniteGunite, concrete sprayed at high pressure, is shot over the steel to form one seamless shell.
  4. 04Tile, coping and finishWaterline tile, coping and the interior finish go on.
  5. 05WaterThe plumbing and mechanical systems are tested, and the pool is filled.

Swimming pools, spas, water features, infinity edges, tension edges and play areas. We also offer plumbing systems, mechanical systems, full pool engineering and pool value engineering.

A family firm since 1972.

Peter and John Whitehead founded Gunite Pools of Nassau in 1972. Thomas joined in 1973 and Phillip in 2008, and the company is now entering its third generation of family ownership.

It started in the residential market and has grown into one of the largest pool construction companies in the Caribbean, taking on some of the largest and most complex commercial projects in the region, and the go-to company for high-end residential pools in the Bahamas. It is wholly Bahamian owned.

Member of the Pool and Hot Tub Alliance (PHTA).
