From a little store on Robinson Road.

Carl G. Treco, a building contractor, opened Commonwealth Building Supplies in March 1973 with six staff and two distributorships.

  1. 1973

    Opens on Robinson Road with six staff and two major distributorships.

  2. Carmichael

    Moves to the 30,000 sq ft flagship Home Center at Southwest Plaza.

  3. 2014

    Opens the Kohler showroom as the official Kohler distributor.

  4. Today

    CBS Bahamas, CBS PROstore for contractors and Aluminum Fabricators Ltd.
