Six staff and two product lines, in March 1973.

Carl G Treco was a building contractor. He opened Commonwealth Building Supplies with Kawneer architectural aluminium and Steelcraft hollow metal doors and frames. Fifty years on, CBS still carries both.

His son John and his nephew Brent Burrows joined in the late seventies. Today CBS supplies and installs architectural building products for homes and commercial buildings, and runs a 30,000 square foot Home Center at SouthWest Plaza.

Free to the mailboat, for every Family Island.

  1. Order at cbsbahamas.com and choose mailboat delivery to Exuma.
  2. CBS prepares the order. There is no same-day mailboat delivery.
  3. CBS carries it to the dock your boat sails from, Potter's Cay or Arawak Cay, at no charge, whatever the size or weight.
  4. At the handoff the load is photographed and a proof of delivery is signed.
  5. You collect on Exuma and pay the boat its own freight fee there.

From the CBS guide to shipping on the mailboat. Which boat serves Exuma, and when it sails, is confirmed with you when you order.
