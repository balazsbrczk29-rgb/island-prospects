02 · Family Islands

From Carmichael Road to your dock.

Choose your island. CBS drops your order at the mailboat for free, with photos and a signed proof at handoff.

Illustration, not to scale

  1. 1

    Choose the mailboat at checkout

    Pick "Deliver to Mailboat: Potter's Cay". Most boats leave from there; a few from Arawak Cay. Not sure? Choose either, the CBS dispatcher corrects it for Exuma.

  2. 2

    Write your island in the address

    George Town, Exuma via the next available mailboat
  3. 3

    Add the freight acceptance date

    Enter the day the boat takes cargo for your port in Order Details. Unsure? Choose the next date and CBS reschedules. Same-day mailboat drop-off is not available.

  4. 4

    CBS picks, preps and packs

    Your order is consolidated and packed to keep the boat's freight low and your items safe.

  5. 5

    Photos and a signed proof at the dock

    Email updates throughout, photos of your items at handoff, and a proof of delivery signed by the mailboat.

Drop-off at the boat is free on every order, whatever the size. The mailboat charges its own freight fee when you collect on Exuma; CBS cannot quote it in advance. Questions after collection: online@cbsbahamas.com.
