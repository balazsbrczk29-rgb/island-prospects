Illustrative photography, not the couple's own

Rose and Marcel, 21 May 2011: 120 guests, the Ocean Club, then Atlantis. Wedding planner: Bahamas Bridal Affairs, with Wildflowers Bahamas.

On the day, every guest is collected by limousine and driven to the One and Only Ocean Club.

It is sweltering. While everyone waits for the bride, there is chilled champagne, and lemonade.

The I dos, among old stone arches and a Versailles-inspired garden, with Nassau across the harbour.

Dinner at Atlantis: white drapery, one great chandelier, and white roses, orchids and calla lilies on every table.

At the end of the night, nobody wants the jazz band to stop playing.

So the newlyweds and their best friends jump into the pool, fully clothed. The dress survives.
