01What we run

Six links in one energy chain.

Each FOCOL company runs one part of the journey. Scroll, and the drawing shows where each one works.

  1. FOCOL Group

    Petroleum products arrive by ship for import and distribution across The Bahamas and the Turks and Caicos Islands. LNG for New Providence will arrive by marine vessel, supplied by Shell from the United States.

  2. Sun Oil Limited and Freeport Oil Company

    Sun Oil supplies gasoline, diesel, marine fuel and aviation fuel to commercial and residential customers across the country. Freeport Oil was established in 1967 by the Grand Bahama Port Authority to supply Freeport's industrial and commercial community.

  3. New Providence Gas Ltd.

    The LNG regasification terminal at Clifton Pier is moving from plans to construction. It will receive LNG, store it and convert it back to natural gas for power generation on New Providence. Sun Oil holds 60 percent, Shell 40 percent.

  4. Bahamas Utilities Company Ltd.

    Services that support utilities, power generation and the essential infrastructure that keeps communities and industries on New Providence running.

  5. Bahamas Solar & Renewables

    Utility-scale, commercial and industrial, micro-grid and distributed renewable projects, from development and construction to long-term operation and maintenance.

  6. Convenience Retailing Limited and Grand Sun Investments

    Shell service stations across New Providence, run since 2007 by the Group's retail arm, and stations across Freeport and Grand Bahama with convenience stores and commercial fueling.
