Five minutes from New Bight airport.

  1. From Fort Lauderdale

    Makers Air from Fort Lauderdale Executive (FXE) straight to New Bight (MYCB). Guests get 10% off.

  2. From Nassau

    Western Air, three times a week, about thirty minutes on a 50-seat jet.

  3. From Your own plane

    New Bight has 5,000 ft of paved runway, VFR, customs 9:00 am to 5:00 pm. Pilots take 15% off with the code PILOT.
