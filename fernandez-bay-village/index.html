01/The bay

A mile of white sand, and very little else.

Fernandez Bay Village has been family run for decades. The cottages sit along a quiet, mile-long beach on Cat Island, with calm, clear water out front and a Clubhouse at the centre for coffee, dinner and the Tiki Bar. Days are barefoot: a chaise on your patio, a book, a float in the bay.

03/Days here

One day in the bay, sunrise to bonfire.

Morning

  1. 01 · Morning

    Breakfast at the Clubhouse

    Hot or continental, sweet and savory, on the patio over the bay. Coffee is complimentary.

  2. 02 · Mid-morning

    Paddle to the Boiling Hole

    Wide, stable kayaks and paddleboards in calm water. The front desk hands out maps through the creek to a blue hole with an attitude.

  3. 03 · Midday

    Conch burgers and fritters

    Grilled fish, sandwiches and salads, or take the 13 ft Whaler out with a picnic and snorkel around the cays.

  4. 04 · Afternoon

    The island, or nothing at all

    Drive the Queen's Highway to The Hermitage, go shelling and sea-glassing, or book a massage by the sea.

  5. 05 · Sundown

    The Tiki Bar, on the beach

    Order anything; if you would rather stay on your chaise, they bring it to you.

  6. 06 · Night

    Dinner, then the bonfire

    Appetizers, a meat and a seafood, sides, dessert and coffee, then a fire on the sand.
