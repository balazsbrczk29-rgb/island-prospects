01Our story

Opened in 2011. Rebuilt after Dorian.

Firefly opened quietly in late 2010 and celebrated its grand opening in 2011. On September 1, 2019, Hurricane Dorian hit Hope Town with sustained winds of 185 mph; the resort lost two villas and its dock, bar and dining deck. The dock, the outside bar and the dining deck have been rebuilt, and power is back.

Today General Manager Tom Hazel and his team run five villas, a Bar & Grill and a boutique, and many guests book their next stay before they check out.

06Getting here

Four easy steps from the runway to your deck.

  1. 1

    Fly to Marsh Harbour

    Commercial or private, into Abaco. Abaco is under 180 miles east of Florida.

  2. 2

    Taxi to the ferry

    About 15 minutes to the Albury's or G&L ferry dock.

  3. 3

    Ferry to Hope Town

    About 20 minutes across to Elbow Cay.

  4. 4

    The resort shuttle

    Waiting at the dock in Hope Town harbour to take you to your villa.

Renting a boat for the week? Many rental companies will meet you in Marsh Harbour; the concierge can arrange it.
