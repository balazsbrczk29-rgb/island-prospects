  • Mint To Be

    2 bed, 2 bath

    Almost suspended over the Sea of Abaco, with a private plunge pool and a back deck facing the sunset. Built for honeymoons, or two couples.

  • Java Rum Plantation

    3 bed, 2 bath

    Wide wooden decks and French doors, steps above the beach. Named after our Sea Island Java Rum.

  • Irvin House

    3 bed, 2.5 bath

    The original owners’ house, on one level: two living rooms, a big kitchen, and a covered porch for the sunset over the pool.

  • Firefly

    2 bed, 2 bath

    Modern tropical rooms, each bedroom with its own full bath, high over the Sea of Abaco.

  • Dragonfly

    2 bed, 2 bath

    The same island-cottage design as Firefly, with elevated views over the water.

  • Butterfly

    2 bed, 2 bath

    Two bedrooms, two baths and the sunset from the deck.
