Proof 05 · Since 1966

Sixty years of keeping island offices printing.

  1. 1966

    Xerox (Bahamas) Limited

    The company opens in Nassau. It has been in the document business ever since.

  2. 1968

    Freeport

    Two years later, a subsidiary office opens in Freeport, Grand Bahama.

  3. 1987

    Bahamian hands

    In March the management team buys the company from Xerox and keeps the full Xerox line. Later that year it adds NCR.

  4. 2002

    Bahamas Business Solutions

    In August the company merges into Bahamas Business Solutions Limited, the name it carries today.

  5. Today

    Two islands and Turks & Caicos

    100% Bahamian owned, more than 80 full-time employees, more than 3,000 customers.

Trusted by government, commercial and private banks, utility companies, healthcare, and hotels and hospitality across The Bahamas and the Turks and Caicos Islands.
