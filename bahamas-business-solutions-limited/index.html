03Since 1966

Sixty years of keeping island offices printing.

  1. 1966

    Xerox (Bahamas) Limited

    The company opens in Nassau. It has been in the document business ever since.

  2. 1968

    Freeport

    Two years later, a subsidiary office opens in Freeport, Grand Bahama.

  3. 1987

    Bahamian hands

    In March the management team buys the company from Xerox and becomes the local distributor for the full Xerox line. Later that year it adds NCR.

  4. 2002

    Bahamas Business Solutions

    In August the company merges with Bahamas Business Solutions Limited and takes the name it carries today.

  5. Today

    Nassau, Freeport, Turks & Caicos

    100% Bahamian owned, more than 80 full-time employees, and more than 3,000 customers across The Bahamas and the Turks & Caicos Islands.
