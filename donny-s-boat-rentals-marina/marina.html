Dockage at the end of Black Sound

A cruiser-friendly family marina on Green Turtle Cay: fixed-price dockage to 50 ft LOA and 7 ft draft, moorings with plenty of swing room, and New Plymouth a ten-minute walk away for Customs, restaurants and groceries.

The way in

With a 6 ft draft, enter Black Sound when the tide is 2 ft above low water. Stay close to the last red marker, then head for the middle of the sound.
