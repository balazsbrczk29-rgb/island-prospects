No ATM on the island: the terms before you go

Green Turtle Cay runs on cash and the weather. These are Donny’s rental terms in plain words.

The full terms
Cash, paid in full before you go
There is no ATM on Green Turtle Cay and cards are not accepted, so bring it with you.
A $100 deposit, non-refundable
It holds your boat for your dates.
Three days minimum
Rentals are only for guests staying on Green Turtle Cay.
Collect from 8 am, back by 5 pm
6 pm in summer time. Late returns are $30 an hour. Sunrise to sunset only.
Same fuel back
Missing fuel is charged at $1 over the price per gallon.
Inside the Sea of Abaco
Ocean fishing within a mile of shore only if the weather allows and Donny agrees at the start.
Weather is the island’s call
There is no refund or credit for days lost to weather.

Coming in on your own boat? Dockage to 50 ft, 7 ft draft.

Fixed-price dockage, moorings with plenty of swing room, and Donny living on site. Shower and bathroom, trash, dinghy dock, electric, water and ice, with the fuel dock and boat yard close by. New Plymouth is a ten-minute walk for Customs, restaurants and groceries.
