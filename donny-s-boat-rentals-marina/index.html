03 · Cruisers

Will your boat fit?

Fixed-price dockage up to 50 ft LOA and 7 ft draft, and moorings with plenty of swing room. Customs is a 10-minute walk away in New Plymouth.

Illustration, not to scale

Within Donny's published limits.

Mind the tide: a 6 ft draft enters Black Sound at 2 ft above low water. Stay close to the last red marker, then aim for the middle of the sound.
