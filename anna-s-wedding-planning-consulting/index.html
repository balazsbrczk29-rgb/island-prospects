About Anna

Two decades of weddings, one planner who answers.

Annamae Fox has planned destination weddings in The Bahamas for more than twenty years, with a network of trusted professionals around her.

Her aim is simple: you enjoy the day without worry, and it feels like nobody else's.

  1. Full wedding planning

    Anna keeps you informed of every detail, from the first call to the last dance.

  2. Wedding day coordination

    She works with every vendor so the day runs on time and as you pictured it.

  3. Vendors she trusts

    Recommendations and management for flowers, music, photography, cake and transport.

  4. Your licence appointment

    She schedules the Registrar's appointment and prepares your documents for the interview.

  5. Ceremony touches

    A sand ceremony, a reverse unity candle shared with every guest, or a hand fasting with ribbon.
