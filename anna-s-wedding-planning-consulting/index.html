Two decades of weddings, one planner who answers.

Annamae Fox has planned destination weddings in The Bahamas for more than twenty years, with a network of trusted professionals around her. Her aim is simple: you enjoy the day without worry, and it feels like nobody else's.

Couples have flown to her from Australia, Egypt, Ethiopia, North and South Africa, Dubai, India, Germany, Switzerland, Sweden, France, and both Americas.
