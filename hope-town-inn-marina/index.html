Getting here

Fly to Marsh Harbour, then the ferry to Hope Town.

GNL Ferry and The Ferry Limited run from Marsh Harbour to Hope Town. You can also fly into Treasure Cay and come over by boat. Arriving on your own boat, book the slip ahead on Dockwa.
