Getting here.

By sea

Romora Bay Marina is an official port of entry: clear customs and immigration at the dock. Send your length, power and dates to the marina before you leave.

marina@romorabay.com242.470.1520Marina Map (PDF)

By air

Fly into North Eleuthera airport. From there it is a short taxi ride to the North Eleuthera dock, then about ten minutes by water taxi across to Harbour Island.
