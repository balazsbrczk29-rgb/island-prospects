Cape Santa Maria sits at the far northern tip of Long Island, where the beach runs long and white and the water turns every shade of turquoise. The beachfront bungalows line the sand beside the Beach House, so the walk from your door to the sea is a few barefoot steps.

Days here are as busy or as empty as you make them: scuba diving, snorkelling and a nature cruise, fishing, kayaking and paddleboarding from the beach, or nothing at all. The resort hosts weddings too. In the evening the villas' west-facing windows and the bungalow verandas look straight into the sunset.
