Cape Santa Maria sits at the far northern tip of Long Island, where the beach runs long and white and the water turns every shade of turquoise. The bungalows line the sand beside the Beach House, so the walk from your door to the sea is a few barefoot steps.

The Beach House.

The resort's restaurant and bar sits on the sand with the bungalows on either side, so dinner is a short barefoot walk from your veranda.

Ask for the menu and dinner times when you book, and tell the team about any dietary needs before you arrive.

Days here are as busy as you make them.

Scuba diving, snorkelling and a nature cruise, fishing, kayaking and paddleboarding straight off the beach, or nothing at all.

Weddings. The resort hosts weddings on the beach. Write to resort@capesantamaria.com with your date and the number of guests.

Every way to Long Island.

Every route ends at Stella Maris Airport (SML), a short taxi ride from the resort, or on the beach itself. The resort arranges your taxi.
