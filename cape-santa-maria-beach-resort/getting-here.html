01Private pilots

A favourite of pilot fly-ins for decades.

Stella Maris Airport has fueling and customs, a short taxi from the lobby. The South Eastern Bonanza Society and Caribbean Flying Adventures (since 2007) bring their groups here, and private pilots receive 15% off accommodations, with some blackout dates, when booking by phone.
