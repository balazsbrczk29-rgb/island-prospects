Flying yourself.

Stella Maris Airport (SML) has fueling and customs. Private pilots get 15% off accommodation, with some blackout dates, when they book by phone on +1 800-926-9704. The resort has welcomed fly-ins such as the South Eastern Bonanza Society and Caribbean Flying Adventures.

Write, call, or check your dates.
