# Playbook: real estate (brokerages, developments, listings)

## 1. The business and the buyer
Bahamian brokerages and developers selling homes, land and island property, largely to foreign buyers
(US, Canada, Europe) and to locals. Buyers fear: misrepresentation, legal and residency complexity, buying
blind from abroad. They choose the agency that shows the place truthfully, has real listings that are easy
to search, and explains the process with confidence.

## 2. What wins the owner
"Buyers abroad can fall in love with the island and find the right listing on your site, not on a portal."
Concepts must keep real listings and search (a listings site stays a listings site).

## 3. Big ideas
- **Choose your island.** The hero is the archipelago: a beautiful map or chart (not a cartoon) where each
  island opens to its real character and the agency's real listings there. (ScrollStage with a styled map,
  PhotoDepth for each island's photograph)
- **Live there for a minute.** A listing presented like a film: scroll moves through the real listing photos in
  order (arrival, the view, the rooms, the water), with the key facts set calmly beside. (ScrollStage, PhotoDepth)
- **The process, demystified.** For foreign buyers: the buying journey as a clear, elegant sequence (offer,
  lawyer, government approvals, closing), linking official sources and never stating unsourced numbers.
- **Fixed split.** One side a fixed photograph that changes per listing, the other side the scrolling list:
  calm, luxurious browsing.

## 4. The page
The place (big idea), search (island, type, price band only from real listings), featured real listings, the
buying process, the agents (real, sourced), contact and viewing request.

## 5. Imagery and 3D
Their real listing photos (verbatim URLs), presented large and in sequence; Commons for islands and towns;
Lovable for lifestyle atmosphere only, labelled illustrative, never a property.

## 6. Interactions
Listing search and filters (real data only); a viewing or call request per listing (Enquiry); saving favourites
in session storage.

## 7. Motion
Slow, cinematic, luxurious: crossfades between photographs, gentle depth, no gimmicks.

## 8. Type and colour
Elegant serif display with a refined sans; colour from the island light (sand, sea, salt white); light pages.

## 9. Voice
Honest, warm, informed; specific places and facts. Tone example, write your own: "Two acres on the Exuma Sound
side, with the morning sun on the water."

## 10. Conversion
Primary: request a viewing or a call about a listing. Secondary: talk to an agent, WhatsApp.

## 11. SEO
`RealEstateAgent`, `Residence`/`Offer` per listing only with real data. Queries: "homes for sale <island>",
"Bahamas real estate <settlement>".

## 12. References
theagencyre.com (big listings photography, clean search), sothebysrealty.com (trust and detail), aman.com/residences
(the lifestyle story), compass.com (search usability).

## 13. Traps
Invented listings or prices; stock luxury interiors; unsourced claims about residency or taxes; a brochure that
drops their real listings.

## 14. Done looks like
A buyer abroad feels the island, finds a real listing that fits, and asks for a viewing in two taps.
