# Playbook: professional services (law, trust, banking, securities, insurance, accounting)

## 1. The business and the buyer
Nassau law firms, trust companies, investment advisers, insurance brokers. Buyers: international families with
assets, corporate clients, Bahamian businesses and homeowners. They fear: indiscretion, incompetence, being
treated as a number, regulatory trouble. They choose the firm that radiates calm authority, discretion and
precision, and makes a first conversation feel safe.

## 2. What wins the owner
"Your firm looks like the private banks and international firms your clients also use." Many have dated or
template sites that undersell decades of reputation.

## 3. Big ideas (wow here is restraint, light and space, not effects)
- **Light on stone.** One extraordinary architectural photograph (Bay Street colonnades, cut coral stone, a quiet
  office with louvres and sea light) made subtly alive with PhotoDepth: light slowly moving across it. Large
  confident type. (PhotoDepth)
- **The long view.** For old firms: the firm's real history as a slow, elegant timeline, every date sourced,
  photographs of the real buildings and documents where available. (HorizontalTrack)
- **Start with who you are.** The navigation is the client's situation (a family, a company, a buyer of property,
  a claim), each leading to plain-language answers and the right person. Calm and useful.
- **Typography as architecture.** No imagery at all: a monumental, perfectly set statement, a strict grid,
  generous space. Right for the most discreet firms.

## 4. The page
The statement (big idea), what they do (practice areas in plain words), who they serve, people (only real,
sourced names and roles), regulation and registration (sourced, e.g. Securities Commission register), offices,
arrange a first conversation, contact.

## 5. Imagery and 3D
Architecture and materials, not stock people shaking hands. Their own office photos; Commons for Nassau's
historic buildings and streets; Lovable for abstract-but-real details (a fountain pen on paper, sea light through
louvres), labelled illustrative. No 3D gimmicks.

## 6. Interactions
"Start with who you are" routing; a consultation request (Enquiry: matter type, preferred contact, confidential
note warning not to include sensitive details); a people filter by practice.

## 7. Motion
Almost still. Slow opacity, light that moves imperceptibly, crisp hover states.

## 8. Type and colour
A beautiful text serif or a precise grotesk, set with care; ink and paper with one deep jewel colour.
Lots of space. Never stock-photo blue corporate.

## 9. Voice
Plain, calm, exact. No jargon, no superlatives. Tone example, write your own: "Most of our clients stay for
decades. The first conversation is simple."

## 10. Conversion
Primary: arrange a first conversation. Secondary: call the office. Discreet but always visible.

## 11. SEO
`LegalService`, `FinancialService`, `InsuranceAgency`, `AccountingService`; `employee` only with sourced people.
Queries: "law firm Nassau", "trust company Bahamas", "<practice area> Bahamas".

## 12. References
pictet.com (calm authority, architecture), lombardodier.com (restrained type, space), slaughterandmay.com (quiet
confidence), cravath.com (typographic restraint).

## 13. Traps
Handshake stock photos; gavels and scales icons; flashy 3D; invented lawyers or awards; walls of text.

## 14. Done looks like
A family office in Geneva would feel at home; a local client understands what the firm does and how to start,
in under a minute.
