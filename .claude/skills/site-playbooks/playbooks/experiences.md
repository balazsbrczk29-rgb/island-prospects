# Playbook: tours, dive, boat rental, taxi and transfers, attractions and gardens

## 1. The business and the buyer
Snorkel and dive operators, swimming-pig and sandbar tours, boat rentals, island taxis and transfer services,
gardens, zoos and attractions. The buyer is usually a cruise passenger or resort guest deciding today or this
week, on a phone, often standing in line or on a ship's wifi. Some plan from home (dive trips). They fear: a
scam, wasting their one free day, a boat that is not what the photo showed. They choose the one that shows
exactly what they will see and lets them book instantly.

## 2. What wins the owner
"Your tour sells itself in the first screen, and booking takes three taps on a phone." Many operators rely on
Facebook or TripAdvisor and lose direct bookings.

## 3. Big ideas
- **Dive in.** The page is a descent: scrolling takes the camera from the surface (Ocean) below the waterline,
  the light shifts from turquoise to deep blue, and real underwater photos appear at real depths with what lives
  there. (ScrollStage, Ocean, PhotoDepth)
- **The route.** A map-led page: the real route from the dock to each stop (sandbar, reef, pig beach), each stop
  revealing its real photo and time. The map is an elegant chart, never a cartoon. (ScrollStage + a styled map)
- **Pick your day.** The tour selector is the hero: real tours side by side in a HorizontalTrack, each with its
  real duration, what you see, price if sourced, and one tap to ask.
- **Film first.** Their own boat or reef footage as a scroll-scrubbed film. (ScrollVideo)
- **Your driver.** For taxis and transfers: a calm, trustworthy page built around the airport arrival moment:
  "look for your name", real routes and the one number to call or WhatsApp.

## 4. The page
The experience (big idea), the tours or boats (real names, durations, what is included only if sourced), what
to bring and what to expect, where you meet (real dock or pickup), booking, safety and licences only if sourced,
contact.

## 5. Imagery and 3D
Their own action photos and reels first. Commons for the real places (Exuma cays, Thunderball Grotto, Dean's Blue
Hole). Lovable for atmosphere only (the boat from above in clear water, gear detail, hands at the helm), never
claiming to be their boat or guests. Ocean for surface and descent moments.

## 6. Interactions
Booking enquiry (date, people, tour: Enquiry); a tour comparison; for rentals, the boat chooser with real specs;
for dive, a depth slider that reveals what you see at each depth (real species for that site only).

## 7. Motion
Energetic but controlled: a confident camera, quick crisp responses on taps, smooth momentum on the tracks.

## 8. Type and colour
Bolder, friendlier display faces (a wide grotesk or warm humanist), saturated sea colours as fields (aqua,
blue hole blue), sunlit whites. Never cartoon fish, emoji or clip art.

## 9. Voice
Vivid and concrete, present tense, short. Tone example, write your own: "Ten minutes out, the water turns
the colour of glass. That is where we stop."

## 10. Conversion
Primary: book or ask on WhatsApp (where most cruise guests are). Secondary: call. Meeting point and time obvious.

## 11. SEO
`TouristTrip` or `TouristAttraction`, `Offer` only with sourced prices, `LocalBusiness` with geo if sourced.
Queries: "<activity> <island>", "Nassau snorkel tour", "boat rental <cay>".

## 12. References
visitfaroeislands.com (landscape storytelling), patagonia.com/stories (documentary truth), airbnb.com/experiences
(clarity of an experience), oceanx.org (ocean imagery and depth).

## 13. Traps
Cartoon sea life; generic stock-like turquoise water; fake reviews; prices not sourced; a booking form that
pretends to take payment; burying the meeting point.

## 14. Done looks like
A cruise guest on a phone knows in five seconds what they will see, trusts the operator, and sends a booking
message in under a minute.
