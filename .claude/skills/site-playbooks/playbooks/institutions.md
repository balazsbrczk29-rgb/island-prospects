# Playbook: institutions (tourism boards, chambers, associations, public bodies)

## 1. The business and the buyer
Island promotion boards, chambers of commerce, trade and professional associations, consumer and development
bodies. Audiences: visitors choosing an island, members and would-be members, citizens with a task, investors.
They fear: not finding what they need, bureaucracy, out-of-date information. They choose (and trust) the body
that is clear, current and proud of its place.

## 2. What wins the owner
"Your organisation looks as modern and helpful as the best public sites in the world, and people find what they
came for." Usually priority D: build after SMEs.

## 3. Big ideas
- **The island, alive.** For tourism boards: the island's real places as a living map or a scroll journey
  (beaches, caves, towns, events), each with real photographs and how to get there. (ScrollStage, Ocean, PhotoDepth)
- **What do you need to do?** For public bodies and associations: the hero is the visitor's task in plain words,
  with the top real tasks as large, direct entries (join, renew, file a complaint, find a member).
- **The community.** For chambers and associations: the members themselves (real directory), events and the
  value of joining, presented with warmth and pride.

## 4. The page
The purpose (big idea or the task list), top tasks, what the organisation does, events or news (real, dated),
membership or services, people and offices, contact.

## 5. Imagery and 3D
Their own photography of real events and places; Commons for landscapes and landmarks; Lovable for atmosphere,
labelled illustrative. Ocean for coastal tourism heroes.

## 6. Interactions
Task routing; a member or business directory search (real data); an events calendar (real dates); a membership
or complaint enquiry (Enquiry).

## 7. Motion
Purposeful and accessible: clear focus states, gentle transitions, nothing that hinders reading.

## 8. Type and colour
Highly legible type (a humanist sans or a clear serif), strong contrast; colour from the island or the
organisation's identity. Accessibility first: WCAG AA throughout.

## 9. Voice
Plain, welcoming, active voice. Tone example, write your own: "Joining takes ten minutes. Here is what you need."

## 10. Conversion
Primary: the top task (join, plan a visit, file, contact). Secondary: call the office.

## 11. SEO
`GovernmentOrganization`, `Organization`, `TouristInformationCenter`, `Event` with real dates. Queries: "<island>
things to do", "<association> membership Bahamas".

## 12. References
gov.uk (task-first clarity), visitfinland.com (warm, photo-led), visitfaroeislands.com (place storytelling),
nycgo.com (destination structure).

## 13. Traps
Out-of-date events; PDF-only processes; walls of text; stock tourists; invented statistics.

## 14. Done looks like
A visitor or member does what they came for in under a minute and leaves with a better feeling about the place.
