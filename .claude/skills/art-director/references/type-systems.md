# Type systems

A system is more than two fonts: it fixes the scale, the case, how labels look and whether numbering
exists. Families are Google Fonts; pick another family only if it keeps the system's character and
is not Inter, Roboto, Arial or a system font. No bold condensed uppercase display faces.

| System | Display | Text | Scale and behaviour | Labels | Numbering |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| T01 Monumental serif | Fraunces (opsz 144, soft 50), or Playfair Display | Newsreader / Source Serif 4 | display 14-22vw, one or two words per screen, tight -0.04em | lowercase italic serif, no tracking | no |
| T02 Text-led editorial | Newsreader display 48-64px max | Newsreader / Literata 19px | nothing above 72px; hierarchy from weight, italic, column width | small caps of the text face | no |
| T03 Swiss grotesk | Schibsted Grotesk, Instrument Sans or Hanken Grotesk (one family only) | same family | 5 sizes only (14/18/28/56/120), weights 400 and 600 | same face, sentence case, no tracking | yes (1.0, 2.0) |
| T04 Technical mono | Space Grotesk or Sora | IBM Plex Sans + IBM Plex Mono for data | data and labels in mono, numbers tabular | mono uppercase, +0.04em | yes (codes) |
| T05 Script and sans | Pinyon Script or Italianno (3-5 words per page only) | Jost or Josefin Sans light | script appears only as signature-like accents | Jost uppercase +0.12em | no |
| T06 Wide grotesk | Syne, Unbounded or Darker Grotesque | Manrope / Work Sans | wide letterforms large, lowercase or sentence case | wide face small | optional |
| T07 Slab poster | Zilla Slab or Bitter heavy | Rubik / Figtree | poster sizes, playful overlaps with photos | slab small, sentence case | no |
| T08 Warm humanist | Young Serif or Gloock | Nunito Sans / Mulish | friendly mid sizes (64-96px), generous leading | the text face bold | no |
| T09 Didone fashion | Bodoni Moda or Prata | Jost / Montserrat Alternates light | high contrast, very large italics allowed | spaced small caps | no |
| T10 Old-style book | EB Garamond or Cormorant Garamond | EB Garamond | classic book typography: drop caps, small caps, ornaments | small caps | roman numerals allowed |
| T11 Neo-grotesk display | Bricolage Grotesque (opsz) or Geist-like "Figtree" heavy | Figtree | big tight display, playful optical size changes | the text face medium | optional |
| T12 Hand and type | Caveat or Gaegu for notes (sparingly) | Literata or Lora | handwriting only for annotations on photos | handwritten | no |

Rules
- The micro-label "small uppercase tracked text above a headline" exists only in T04, T05 and T09. Everywhere else, sections are introduced by the headline itself, a rule, a number (T03, T04) or nothing.
- A headline never uses the "last line in italic or accent colour" trick unless the system is T09.
- Each system may appear at most 9 times across the 100 sites; a display family at most 4 times.
