# Page archetypes

Each archetype is a whole way of building a page: hero, section grammar, navigation, where the
crucial facts live, and motion. Two sites with different archetypes cannot look alike even with
similar colours. Build the dealt one fully. "Fits" lists the niches the dealer may give it to.
"Study" lists real sites to scrape for this archetype (pick two, firecrawl_scrape with screenshot).
"Style seeds" are awesome-design-skills folders to read for component details.

---

## A01 Magazine feature
The site reads as a long-form travel or business feature in a quality magazine.
- Fits: hospitality-stay, marina, professional-services, food-events, institution
- Hero: masthead row (name set like a magazine title, a dateline with island and season), then one full-bleed photograph; headline and standfirst sit BELOW the photograph, not on it.
- Grammar: drop-cap opening paragraph in 2 columns; pull quote set huge (only a sourced quote); photo essay of 3-5 images with real captions; a "facts box" sidebar inside the article (phone, address, how to book) like a magazine info panel; closing "colophon" with contact.
- Nav: masthead links in one thin row, no sticky bar; a small "Contents" button opens a full overlay table of contents.
- Crucial facts: the facts box, plus a slim bottom action bar on mobile styled as a magazine tear-off strip.
- Motion: very quiet. Image crossfades, text never animates; page-turn style section transitions are allowed once.
- Study: kinfolk.com, cerealmag.com, monocle.com, the-gentlewoman.com
- Style seeds: editorial, paper, refined

## A02 Catalogue index
The inventory is the hero. Like Aesop, Muji, Hay.
- Fits: trade-retail, industrial-b2b, food-events
- Hero: no big headline. A fixed left index column (departments or product families as a typographic list) and a photographic grid on the right that changes as you hover the index.
- Grammar: department pages as photo grids with plain spec text under each item (name, use, size), no cards, no shadows; a "counter" section (how to order: in store, phone, delivery islands); a store section with a large photo of the real street or shopfront.
- Nav: the index column IS the navigation; on mobile it collapses into a horizontal scrolling chip row pinned under the logo.
- Crucial facts: a permanent footer line in the index column (phone, address, hours if sourced).
- Motion: image swaps on hover (no fade-ups), smooth filter transitions with layout animation.
- Study: aesop.com, muji.com, hay.com, store.moma.org
- Style seeds: minimal, index, mono

## A03 Cinematic chapters
One continuous film. Full-screen pinned photographs crossfade as you scroll; copy appears like subtitles.
- Fits: marina, hospitality-stay, tours-experiences, real-estate
- Hero: 100svh photograph with only the name, small, in a corner, and a one-line subtitle centred low like film subtitles.
- Grammar: 4-6 chapters, each a pinned full-bleed image (or a short muted video loop if they have one) with 2-3 subtitle lines and a chapter title like a title card; between chapters one plain text card on solid colour; end with "credits": every practical fact rolling like end credits, then the enquiry.
- Nav: a single "Menu" word top-right that opens a full-screen chapter list with thumbnails; a small persistent "Enquire" text link bottom-right.
- Crucial facts: a chapter-0 title card right after the hero carries phone, island and the one booking action, set like an opening title.
- Motion: scroll-scrubbed crossfades and slow Ken Burns; nothing slides in.
- Study: aman.com, belmond.com, oneandonlyresorts.com, sixsenses.com
- Style seeds: immersive, dramatic, storytelling

## A04 Swiss information grid
Visible structure, one grotesk family, sizes only, tables. Confidence through order.
- Fits: professional-services, institution, industrial-b2b
- Hero: a 12-column grid with visible hairlines; a two-line statement at a huge size spanning 9 columns; a data table on the right (established, offices, regulator register numbers, practice areas: only sourced facts).
- Grammar: numbered sections (numbering allowed here), each a strict grid table: service | who it is for | how to start; a people or offices table; a documents/downloads list; contact as a table.
- Nav: top row of plain text items with their section numbers, no button styling; current section underlined.
- Crucial facts: the hero table.
- Motion: none on content; only hover states and a grid-line draw on load.
- Study: experimentaljetset.nl, pentagram.com, lombardodier.com, julius baer
- Style seeds: square, mono, professional

## A05 Chart and map
The page is a map or nautical chart of the real place. You move through the business by moving across it.
- Fits: tours-experiences, marina, real-estate, hospitality-stay, institution
- Hero: a full-viewport map (MapLibre with a free style, or a custom SVG chart drawn from real coastline coordinates, or a real aerial photograph) with the business pinned; the headline sits in a legend panel.
- Grammar: each section is a stop on the map (the camera flies there as you scroll): a pinned map left, the stop's photograph and text right; distances and times as chart annotations (sourced only); a route planner interaction.
- Nav: a legend panel (fixed, bottom-left on desktop, bottom sheet on mobile) listing the stops.
- Crucial facts: in the legend panel, always visible.
- Motion: camera flyTo between stops, route lines drawing.
- Study: visitfinland.com, explore.org, the pudding map essays, noaa charts
- Style seeds: perspective, levels, contemporary

## A06 Object study
One real object, very large, on a coloured ground, explained part by part.
- Fits: industrial-b2b, trade-retail, professional-services (only if they have a physical product)
- Hero: the object (a photographed or generated house with its roof, a water-from-air unit, a glass panel, an armoured van, a POS terminal, an upholstered chair) cut out on a strong colour field, filling 70% of the viewport; name and one line small beside it.
- Grammar: scroll explodes or rotates the object (img2threejs model, or a sequence of photographs); callouts with leader lines name each part or service; a materials or specification table; real projects as a ledger (client, island, year: only sourced); quote request styled as a work order.
- Nav: a slim toolbar with the company name and a "Request a quote" button; the services list doubles as a part selector.
- Crucial facts: printed on the toolbar and on the work-order panel.
- Motion: scroll-scrubbed object animation; callout lines draw.
- Study: apple.com product pages, teenage.engineering, nothing.tech, rivian.com
- Style seeds: perspective, sleek, skeumorphism

## A07 Letter and stationery
The page is correspondence: a letter, an invitation, an envelope with pressed photographs.
- Fits: food-events, hospitality-stay (small inns), professional-services (family firms)
- Hero: a sheet of textured paper with a letterhead, a handwritten-feeling salutation line (script face used for 3-5 words only), and a photograph clipped or taped to it.
- Grammar: the story as the body of the letter; services as an itemised "enclosure" list; photos as prints with real handwritten-style captions; the enquiry as a reply card you fill in.
- Nav: a ribbon bookmark tab on the side opens the sections; the reply card is always one tap away.
- Crucial facts: the letterhead line (address, phone, email) like real stationery.
- Motion: paper physics (slight rotation on hover, cards that lift), envelope open once.
- Study: papier.com, smythson.com, minted.com, wedding planner portfolios on siteinspire
- Style seeds: paper, vintage, sketch

## A08 Fixed split
The viewport is permanently divided: one half is a fixed photograph that changes per section, the other half scrolls.
- Fits: real-estate, hospitality-stay, professional-services, marina
- Hero: left 50% photograph full-height; right 50% the name huge and the offer.
- Grammar: every section swaps the fixed photograph (crossfade) while the right column scrolls its text; listings or rooms as rows with a thumbnail that becomes the fixed photo on hover; contact as a quiet final screen.
- Nav: inside the scrolling column, at its top, as a vertical list.
- Crucial facts: a fixed footer strip across the scrolling half.
- Motion: photo crossfade synchronised to scroll position.
- Study: sothebysrealty.com listings, theagencyre.com, rosewoodhotels.com
- Style seeds: spacious, premium, clean

## A09 Colour poster
Bold full-colour grounds that change section by section, huge type, photographs as cut-out stickers.
- Fits: food-events, tours-experiences, trade-retail, hospitality-stay (playful ones)
- Hero: a saturated full-bleed colour (from the lane), the name or offer set enormous, one cut-out photograph overlapping the type.
- Grammar: each section a different colour ground from the lane; products or tours as cut-out photographs with price tags (sourced prices only); a ticket-style order or booking block.
- Nav: a floating dock bottom-centre with 3-4 items and the main action.
- Crucial facts: the dock and a ticket stub.
- Motion: sticker drops with spring physics, colour wipes between sections.
- Study: mailchimp.com (brand pages), oatly.com, graza.co, liquiddeath.com (tone only)
- Style seeds: colorful, vibrant, expressive, riso

## A10 Spec sheet
A technical document: drawings, data, ledgers. Built for procurement and engineers.
- Fits: industrial-b2b, institution, professional-services (security, IT)
- Hero: a real photograph of the yard, plant or fleet (own, Commons or generated) with a technical overlay: dimension lines, coordinates, a data block (capacity, certifications: sourced only).
- Grammar: capability matrix (rows of capabilities, columns of what is included); project ledger table; plant or fleet register; safety and compliance documents list; a request-for-quote form styled as a numbered form.
- Nav: a toolbar with section codes (e.g. 1.0 Capabilities) and a search field if there is enough content.
- Crucial facts: a "Contact desk" block in the toolbar dropdown and the RFQ.
- Motion: overlays drawing on the photograph; counters only for sourced numbers.
- Study: arup.com, maersk.com, fosterandpartners.com, bechtel.com
- Style seeds: enterprise, mono, codex

## A11 Horizontal journey
The core of the page scrolls sideways: rooms, boats, vehicles, tour stops, a timeline.
- Fits: hospitality-stay, tours-experiences, trade-retail (fleet/lineup), marina
- Hero: a short opening screen, then the page pins and vertical scroll moves a row of large photographs sideways.
- Grammar: the horizontal track (each item a large photo + name + one sourced spec line + action); a comparison view of two items side by side; a vertical closing section with practicalities.
- Nav: a progress timeline along the bottom of the track that doubles as navigation.
- Crucial facts: the last frame of the track is a booking frame; plus a corner action button.
- Motion: pinned horizontal scroll with momentum, items scale slightly in focus.
- Study: porsche.com model pages, airbnb luxe, ferrari.com range pages
- Style seeds: immersive, modern, bold

## A12 Service desk
Utility first, like gov.uk: the visitor's task is the hero.
- Fits: institution, industrial-b2b (utilities, transport), professional-services
- Hero: "What do you need to do?" style question (in their voice) with 4-8 real tasks as large text links, plus search if there is enough content.
- Grammar: each task gets a plain, numbered how-to with what to bring, where, cost if sourced; an offices list with opening times; a short "about us" last.
- Nav: minimal header, breadcrumbs on inner pages.
- Crucial facts: in the hero, directly beside the tasks.
- Motion: none beyond focus and hover.
- Study: gov.uk, nycgo.com, service.nsw.gov.au
- Style seeds: clean, basic, friendly

## A13 Scrapbook history
A family album: overlapping photographs, dates, notes, a timeline of real history.
- Fits: food-events, hospitality-stay, trade-retail, marina (family owned with history)
- Hero: a loose collage of 3-5 real photographs at slight angles on a textured ground, a hand-set title.
- Grammar: a timeline of sourced years and events; "then and now" pairs; the offer as a pinned note board; visit details on a luggage-tag style card.
- Nav: a tab strip like album dividers.
- Crucial facts: the luggage tag, repeated in the footer.
- Motion: photos settle into place with physics, drag to rearrange (desktop).
- Study: patagonia.com/stories, a24films.com, family business archives on siteinspire
- Style seeds: vintage, doodle, sketch

## A14 Quiet luxury
Almost no interface. One photograph per screen, tiny type, extreme space.
- Fits: marina, hospitality-stay, real-estate, professional-services (trust, private banking)
- Hero: a single photograph, full-bleed, with the name in small caps or a small serif centred, nothing else.
- Grammar: alternating screens: one image, one sentence; a single "Enquire" screen with an understated form; contact details set like a calling card.
- Nav: one word ("Menu" or the name) opening an overlay; no sticky bar.
- Crucial facts: the calling card screen, and an always-visible "Enquire" word on mobile.
- Motion: slow opacity only, long durations (1.2-2s), nothing moves position.
- Study: aman.com, therow.com, bottegaveneta.com, pictet.com
- Style seeds: minimal, refined, premium

## Navigation patterns (the dealer pairs one with the archetype; the archetype's own nav wins if stated)

N9 bottom progress timeline | N10 minimal header + breadcrumbs

## Motion signatures
M1 scroll-scrubbed crossfade | M2 pinned horizontal track | M3 clip-path mask reveal on images only |
M4 object scrub (rotate / explode) | M5 map camera flights | M6 spring physics stickers and cards |
M7 stillness (opacity only, long) | M8 hover image swap + layout animation | M9 line and overlay drawing |
M10 page transitions between routes (view transitions API)
