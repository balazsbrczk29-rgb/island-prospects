01The inn

On Bay Street since 1972, rebuilt after Dorian.

The Conch Inn first opened in 1972. Hurricane Dorian closed it in 2019; it reopened in March 2021 and the family has since renovated every room, suite and apartment. What has not changed is the address: the harbour front of Marsh Harbour, steps from the slips and a short walk from town.

02The marina

Seventy-three slips, in the middle of town.

A family-owned, full-service marina with slips and mooring balls. Power and water at every slip, free WiFi, clean private showers, laundry, fuel and ice, with 24-hour security on the docks.

05Ashore

Snappas, then a walk into town.

Snappas Bar & Grill is the inn's dockside restaurant and bar, with live music and a harbour view. Navigare Yachting, Dream Yacht Charters and the Renu Day Spa are on the property too.
