P.S.

The house rules a captain asks about:

  1. Rates are on length overall, bow pulpit to swim platform.
  2. Check-in and check-out at 12 noon.
  3. Cancel within 48 hours of arrival and the first night is charged.
  4. Some slips have a minimum length; no slip is guaranteed until assigned.
  5. Hull and liability insurance required.
  6. No wake in the marina, no fishing, music low after 9 pm, dogs on a leash.
