Services

From membraneto masonry.

Residential, commercial and industrial properties throughout New Providence and the surrounding islands.

01 /Roofing

Roofing

  1. TPO & PVC single-ply membrane systems

    High-performance thermoplastic membranes for commercial and residential flat roofs.

  2. Modified bitumen & torch-applied systems

    Durable multi-layer assemblies for waterproofing and UV resistance.

  3. Standing seam & metal roofing

    Custom fabricated aluminium or steel panels with concealed fasteners and Kynar finishes.

  4. Shingle roofing

    Architectural, synthetic and 3-tab shingles for pitched residential and commercial roofs.

  5. Roof replacement & re-roofing

    Full tear-off and reinstallation of aged or failing roof systems.

02 /Waterproofing & specialty

Waterproofing & specialty

  1. Terrace & balcony waterproofing

    Liquid-applied membranes beneath tile or concrete toppings.

  2. Solar plinth & equipment curb flashing

    TPO-compatible detailing for solar bases, HVAC curbs and mechanical penetrations.

  3. Planter box & green roof coordination

    Waterproofing continuity beneath movable or built-in planters.

  4. Roof overburden installation

    Gravel ballast, pavers or decorative stone over membrane systems.

03 /Maintenance & inspection

Maintenance & inspection

  1. Preventative maintenance plans

    Scheduled inspections, cleaning and minor repairs to extend roof life.

  2. Leak detection & emergency repairs

    Rapid response to active leaks or storm-related damage.

  3. Roof condition assessments

    Detailed reports with photos, core cuts and repair recommendations.

04 /Custom fabrication

Custom fabrication

  1. Perimeter flashing & edge metal

    ANSI/SPRI ES-1 compliant fabrication in Kynar-finished aluminium or galvanized steel.

  2. AC curb installation

    Custom fabricated mechanical curbs to support rooftop HVAC units.

  3. Gutters & downspouts

    Seamless aluminium or copper systems with debris guards and custom profiles.

  4. Skylight & hatch integration

    Flashing and waterproofing around daylighting and access features.

05 /General construction

General construction

  1. Interior & exterior painting

    From precision detailing to full-scale repainting.

  2. Pressure cleaning

    Driveways, roofs, gutters and facades.

  3. Gutter & downspout cleaning

    Keeping drainage clear to protect foundation and roofline.

  4. Tree cutting & hauling

    Safe trimming, removal and debris hauling.

  5. Carpentry

    Framing, decking, custom cabinetry, doors and trim.

  6. Masonry

    Block work, poured concrete, stone and brick for walls, driveways, patios and retaining structures.
