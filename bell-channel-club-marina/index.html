The setting

A quiet club with water on both sides.

Bell Channel Club & Marina sits on the Bell Channel inlet in Freeport, Grand Bahama, where boats run in from the sea toward Port Lucaya Marketplace. The complex faces the beach on one side and the canal on the other, so most homes look out over moving water.

It is a residential club, not a hotel. Owners share the grounds, the private marina and the amenities, and the homeowners' association looks after the beach.

The canal side

Private docks for owners, a short run out through the inlet. Space is offered when available, so ask the office first.

Common questions

Can visiting boats dock here?

The marina is a private marina for owners, offered when space is available. Call the club office on (242) 373-2673 to ask about dock availability before you arrive.

Is it a hotel?

No. Bell Channel Club is a gated residential club of condominiums and townhouses. Homes are owned privately; some owners sell or rent through local agents.

What is included for owners?

Use of the heated pool, tennis courts and owners' library, the private marina when available, a limited number of storage garages, and reverse osmosis water to every unit. The homeowners' association maintains the beach.

Speak to the club office.

Questions about docks, owners' matters or the grounds go to the office in Freeport.
