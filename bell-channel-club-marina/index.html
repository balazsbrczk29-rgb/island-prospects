A quiet club between two waters

Bell Channel Club & Marina is a gated community of condominiums and townhouses in Lucaya, with the canal on one side, the beach on the other, and a private dock for its owners. From the units you look across the inlet into Port Lucaya.

Bell Channel Club & MarinaLucaya, Grand Bahama

Two kinds of water, one gate, and a dock kept for the people who live here.

There are two kinds of water at Bell Channel Club. On one side runs the canal, still and sheltered, where owners keep their boats at the club’s private dock. On the other is the beach, which the homeowners’ association looks after. Between them stand the condominiums and townhouses, behind a gate, in a quiet corner of Lucaya.

Look out from the units and you see the inlet: the stretch of water that carries boats in from the ocean to Port Lucaya and its Marketplace. Boats come and go below, and the club is a quiet place to watch them from.

The club keeps the things that make a second home easy. A heated pool. Tennis courts. A library for owners. A limited number of storage garages. And reverse osmosis water for every unit, which on an island counts for more than it sounds. Pets are welcome.

The marina is for owners first. Visiting boats can ask, and the office will tell you whether a slip is free. The same goes for anyone thinking of buying or renting here: the quickest answer is a phone call to the club office.
