Living here

Homes on the inlet, inside the gate.

Bell Channel Club is a gated community of condominiums and townhouses in Freeport, Grand Bahama. Units overlook the ocean inlet into Port Lucaya Marketplace.

What comes with a home here

  • The beach

    On the beach side of the complex, maintained by the homeowners' association.

  • The canal

    On the other side, with the club's private marina for owners when docks are available.

  • Heated pool

    Shared by owners, inside the gate.

  • Tennis courts

    On the club grounds.

  • Owners' library

    A shared room for owners.

  • Storage garages

    A limited number on site.

  • Water

    A reverse osmosis system serves every unit.

  • Community

    Gated, quiet and family oriented. Pets are allowed.

Buying or renting

Homes at Bell Channel Club are privately owned. When one comes up for sale or rent it is usually listed through a Grand Bahama real estate agent.

For questions about the club itself, the grounds or the homeowners' association, call the club office.
