The marina at Bell Channel

A private dock, kept for owners

The club’s marina is part of the complex on Bell Channel, the inlet that runs in to Port Lucaya. It is private. Owners come first, and other boats are welcome to ask whether a slip is free.

Dock enquiries by telephone: (242) 373-2673

Before you come in

Who is asking?

Ask before you come in.

Bell Channel Club’s marina is private and kept for owners. Other boats are taken only when a slip is available, so call the office before you plan on it.

Have your boat’s length, beam, draft and dates ready when you ring.

Ask about dock availability (242) 373-2673

Why we don’t print the numbers

Most marina pages open with a table: slips, lengths, depths, power, rates. This one does not, on purpose. A private dock depends on the owners who use it, and the only reliable answer about what is free is the one the office gives you on the day.

So the honest version is short. The marina belongs to Bell Channel Club. It serves owners. If a slip is available, a visiting boat may be able to use it. Call before you plan on it, with your boat’s particulars to hand.

  • Your boat’s length overall and beam
  • Draft
  • Arrival and departure dates
  • Whether you are an owner, a guest or visiting
