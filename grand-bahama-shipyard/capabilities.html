02 / Trades

Every service, in order of the job.

  1. 01Drydocking and afloat repairsFloating docks, a pier and a wharf, certified and maintained to ABS classification rules.
  2. 02Steel and aluminium fabricationA 35,000 ft² workshop for fabrication, welding and fitting in new construction, repairs and retrofits.
  3. 03Hull treatmentHP and UHP blasting and coating by NACE approved specialists, class certified antifouling, markings.
  4. 04Pipe workLRQA certified pipe welders for CuNi, stainless, carbon and GF+ pipe on every vessel system.
  5. 05UTM hull gaugingClass approved by ABS, BV, DNV-GL and LR, with ASNT Level II certified calibrations.
  6. 06Electrical and propulsionFull motor rewinds with balancing; azipod and thruster repairs in dock or underwater.
  7. 07Project planning and schedulingEvery project has a designated project manager; every client receives a detailed schedule for planning and execution.
  8. 08Naval architects and engineersCertified naval architects and engineers on staff prepare workshop and fabrication drawings.
  9. 09Mechanical servicesCertified mechanics and machining; engines, pumps and valves overhauled on site.
  10. 10Tank cleaningBallast, fuel, fresh water, slops and void spaces, among others.
  11. 11LogisticsFull logistics support for each project.
  12. 12Material handling and customs brokerageThe yard's own customs clearing and brokerage department handles equipment and materials delivered straight to the yard.
