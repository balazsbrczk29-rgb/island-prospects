02 / The yard

Built by cruising, for every kind of ship.

Grand Bahama Shipyard is a joint venture between Carnival Corporation, Royal Caribbean Group and MSC Cruises. It was established in Freeport in 1999 and docks cruise and commercial vessels alike, from planning and steel to coatings and propulsion.

04 / Back to full strength

From one dock to three.

The yard is now a two-dock operation again, with a third dock on the way. Planners booking a window should know where it stands today.

  1. 1999Established in Freeport as part of a plan to build a maritime centre on the island.
  2. 2019Prior docking assets lost. The yard begins rebuilding its multi-dock capability.
  3. Jan 2026Carnival Elation enters the new East End floating dock on 16 January.
  4. Feb 2026Carnival Elation returns to service on 9 February, as planned.
  5. Jun 20261.45 million trade labour hours without a lost time injury.
  6. Late 2026The Lucayan floating dock is expected, taking the yard to three docks.

08 / Questions

What planners ask first.

The East End floating dock is approximately 357 metres long with a beam of about 70 metres and is engineered to lift vessels of up to 93,500 tons. Every docking is confirmed by the planning team against full vessel particulars.
