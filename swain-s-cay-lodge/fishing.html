The fishing

Bonefishing onAndros, from the beach.

I. Four ways to fish

From the flat
to the blue.

  • One

    Bonefishing

    Mangrove Cay gives easy access to the South Bight or the fabled Middle Bight of Andros. Handpicked local independent guides know the spot for the wind and tide. Or wade the flat off the beach on your own.

  • Two

    Reef fishing

    The Andros Barrier Reef runs 140 miles and lies less than a mile from shore in most places. Expect yellowtail snapper, Nassau grouper, triggerfish, barracuda, reef sharks and porgy.

  • Three

    Deep sea fishing

    Outside the reef the bottom drops to 6,000 feet: wahoo, dolphin fish, tuna and billfish.

  • Four

    Spearfishing

    Hunt your own dinner. The team arranges guided tours and lessons, beginners welcome.
