Questions anglers ask.

What types of fishing are available at Swain’s Cay Lodge?
Bonefishing, deep-sea fishing, reef fishing and spearfishing, all suited to the fishing grounds around Mangrove Cay and Andros.
Do I need a guide to go bonefishing?
Not necessarily. The lodge works with handpicked local guides on the South and Middle Bight, and there is a wadeable bonefish flat right off the beach if you would like to fish on your own.
What fish can I expect on a deep-sea trip?
Just outside the Andros Barrier Reef the depth drops quickly, which suits large pelagic fish such as wahoo, dolphin fish, tuna and billfish.
What is caught while reef fishing?
Guests fishing the barrier reef with local guides typically catch yellowtail snapper, Nassau grouper, triggerfish, barracuda, reef sharks and porgy.
Is spearfishing suitable for beginners?
Yes. The team can arrange guided spearfishing tours and lessons for guests who are new to it.
Can fishing be combined with other things?
Yes. Many guests combine fishing with kayaking, snorkelling, cycling or exploring Mangrove Cay.
Can the lodge tailor a package?
Yes. Call +1 (242) 422-5018 or +1 (242) 369-0296, or email fish@swainscaylodge.com, for tailored packages, extra non-guided days or self-guided packages.
