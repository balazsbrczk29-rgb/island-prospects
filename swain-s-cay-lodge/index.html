The home flat

Wade it yourself, no guide needed

  • Bonefish

A wadeable bonefish flat right off the lodge beach. Spend a day on the sand and try to fool a bonefish on your own. They are very clever fish.

II. A day here

Out with the tide,
back for dinner.

Breakfast before the tide. A transfer to the dock and a day on the flats with your guide. Back to the beach, a drink at the Reefside, Bahamian cooking for dinner and fishing stories with the other guests.
