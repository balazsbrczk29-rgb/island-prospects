Where you'll fish.

  1. The home flat

    Wade it, right off the lodge beach

    Bonefish

    A wadeable bonefish flat right off the lodge beach.

  2. The Middle Bight

    Guided skiff, two anglers a boat

    Bonefish

    Mangrove Cay sits between the bights, so the lodge can fish the Middle Bight or the South Bight with handpicked local independent guides.

  3. The South Bight

    Guided skiff, two anglers a boat

    Bonefish

    The other side of the cay: airport and dock transfers to either bight are part of the package.

  4. The Andros Barrier Reef

    Reef fishing, spearfishing tours and lessons

    Yellowtail snapper, Nassau grouper, triggerfish, barracuda, reef sharks, porgy

    140 miles long, and less than a mile from shore in most places.

  5. Outside the reef

    Deep sea fishing

    Wahoo, dolphin fish, tuna, billfish

    Beyond the reef the depth drops to 6,000 feet.

Photographs courtesy of Lauren Brown Photography and Ian Davis at Yellow Dog Fly Fishing.

Through Nassau to Mangrove Cay.

  1. Fly to Nassau. Airfare to and from Nassau is yours to book.
  2. On to Mangrove Cay by air or with Bahamas Ferries.
  3. Met and driven. Airport and dock transfers are part of every fishing package.
