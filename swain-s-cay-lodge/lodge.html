Mangrove Cay, Andros. An Orvis Endorsed fly fishing lodge.

The lodge on the beach at Mangrove Cay.

Twelve units, up to 26 guests, Reefside Restaurant and Bar on the sand, and free bicycles, kayaks and snorkelling gear.

Four ways to sleep by the flat.

Book a room

Reefside Restaurant & Bar.

Right on the beach: authentic Bahamian cuisine, fresh and home-cooked, for breakfast and dinner.

For the one who isn't fishing.

Bicycles, kayaks and snorkelling gear are free for guests.

  • Kayak along the bights, and spot the creatures of the air and sea.
  • Snorkel the tropical underwater landscape.
  • Ride a bike around Mangrove Cay for its flora and fauna.
  • Do nothing on a beach lounger while a warm breeze blows.

Through Nassau to Mangrove Cay.

  1. Fly to Nassau. Airfare to and from Nassau is yours to book.
  2. On to Mangrove Cay by air or with Bahamas Ferries.
  3. Met and driven. Airport and dock transfers are part of every fishing package.
