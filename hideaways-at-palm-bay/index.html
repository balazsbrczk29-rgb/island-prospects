A private beach on Elizabeth Harbour.

Jolly Hall Bay is calm, clear water and white sand, with Stocking Island across the harbour. The water toys are free for guests, the pool is open all year, and you watch the sunset from your own patio.

Daily housekeeping, grocery delivery, a free shuttle into George Town and massage in your room, if you want it.
