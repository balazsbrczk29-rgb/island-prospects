1The beach

Your little slice of Elizabeth Harbour.

Hideaways sits right on one of the private white-sand beaches of Elizabeth Harbour, with crystal clear, calm water out front. Borrow the water toys for free, splash in the pool, book a massage in your suite, and watch the sunset from your patio.

  • Free WiFi throughout
  • Free town shuttle
