Island Marine Covering Ltd (IMC) makes awnings, weather curtains, roll-away hurricane shutters and marine canvas for homes, businesses and boats in Nassau, from its workshop on Sabal Close.

Four things, made to fit.

Every opening and every boat is a different size. Call with your measurements to start a quote.

  • Awnings

    Fabric shade over windows, doors, patios and storefronts.

  • Weather curtains

    Drop-down curtains that close a porch, patio or terrace against wind and rain.

  • Roll-away hurricane shutters

    Shutters that roll down over an opening when a storm is coming and roll away after.

  • Marine canvas

    Canvas work and covers for boats.

Order before the season, not the week of the storm.

The Atlantic hurricane season runs from 1 June to 30 November. Shutters and weather curtains are made to measure, so the time to order is while the weather is quiet.
