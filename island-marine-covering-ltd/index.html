      1992

      One sewing machine and one roll of canvas, in an old cottage in Montagu village.

      That is how Island Marine Covering began in 1992. The trade came from the family: Henry Nixon founded Nixon's Upholstery Services and, with his wife Viola, upholstered for Nassau hotels including The Montague, The British Colonial and The Royal Victorian.

      The family's work reached Abaco with Kirk Nixon. Today the company trades as Cover Up and still goes to awning and marine canvas seminars in North and South America to keep learning the craft.
