1992

"I started with one sewing machine and one roll of canvas, working in an old cottage in Montagu village."

That is how Island Marine Covering began, with Ricky Nixon and what he learned from his father. Henry Nixon founded Nixon's Upholstery Services and, with his wife Viola, upholstered for Nassau hotels including The Montague, The British Colonial and The Royal Victorian.

The family's work reached Abaco with Ricky's brother Kirk. Today the company trades as Cover Up and still goes to awning and marine canvas seminars in North and South America.
