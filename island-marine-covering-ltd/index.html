02 · Our story

A family trade that began in Nassau's grand hotels.

  1. Before

    Henry and Viola Nixon

    Nixon's Upholstery Services dresses the lobbies, halls and rooms of Nassau hotels including The Montague, The British Colonial and The Royal Victorian.

  2. 1992

    One machine, one roll

    Ricky Nixon starts Island Marine Covering with one sewing machine and one roll of canvas, in an old cottage in Montagu.

  3. Then

    To the Abacos

    The family trade reaches Abaco with Ricky's brother, Kirk.

  4. Now

    Cover Up

    A manufacturing workshop in the Airport Industrial Park, still learning at awning and marine canvas seminars across North and South America.

  5. "I started with one sewing machine and one roll of canvas working in an old cottage in Montagu village."
