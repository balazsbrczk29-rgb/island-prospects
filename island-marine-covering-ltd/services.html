What Island Marine Covering makes.

Four product lines from the workshop in the Airport Industrial Park, Nassau. Prices depend on size and fabric, so every job is quoted. Rates on request.

Awnings

Fabric shade for the places that take the sun: windows, doors, patios, decks and storefronts.

  • For homes and for businesses
  • Quoted to the size of the opening

Weather curtains

Curtains that drop down to close off a porch, patio or terrace when wind and rain come in, and roll up when the weather clears.

  • Keep outdoor space usable in rough weather
  • Quoted to the size of the opening

Roll-away hurricane shutters

Shutters that come down over windows and doors when a storm is forecast, and roll away out of sight afterwards.

  • Order well before hurricane season (1 June to 30 November)
  • Quoted per opening

Marine canvas

Canvas work for boats, made in Nassau.

  • Tell us the make, model and length of your boat
  • Quoted per boat
