What a week really costs.

Pick a cottage, your nights and meals. We add the published rates, the 10% VAT, the 6% resort levy, the 15% meal gratuity and the island taxi, so there are no surprises.

No deposit required. The club holds a card to reserve and payment is due on departure. Cancel 30 days or more before arrival and nothing is charged.

04Days here

Unplugged, but never bored.

01 · Morning

Complimentary coffee and tea at the beach bar, then breakfast if you want it.

02 · Midday

Kayaks, canoes, paddle boards, bikes and snorkel gear are yours to use. Walk or ride the 200 acres.

03 · Afternoon

Flats fishing, eco tours, caving or diving nearby, or a massage and a hammock.

04 · Evening

The palapa honor bar, a bonfire on the beach, and dinner Wednesday to Saturday.

05Weddings

Toes in the sand, or a 100-year-old church.

For two, or for family and friends of up to 40 guests, with planning before, during and after.
