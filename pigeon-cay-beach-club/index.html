One flight to New Bight, then a taxi up the island

  1. Fly to New Bight, Cat Island (TBI)

    Makers Air flies seven days a week from Fort Lauderdale Executive (FXE). Western Air also flies into New Bight, and most other guests connect through Nassau. Marlin Air charters from Nassau: 242-342-3014.

  2. Take the taxi

    It is $60 one way, $120 for both ways. Tell the club your flight and they will help arrange the transfer.

  3. Arrive near The Bluff

    The club is on the north of Cat Island, three miles south of Bennett's Harbour. A rental car is worth having only if you want to explore.

  4. Fly free from Nassau

    Pre-book an air-inclusive stay of 4 to 6 nights and one airline ticket from Nassau is free. Stay 7 nights and two are, at double occupancy. Book by June 30, 2027 and travel by October 31, 2027. The offer runs through the Bahama Out Islands Promotion Board and its terms apply.

Before you ask

Do I pay anything when I book?

No. The club holds a credit card number to reserve your cottage, takes no deposit, and payment is due on departure.

What if I have to cancel?

Cancel 30 days or more before arrival and nothing is charged. Inside 30 days, one night's lodging is charged to the card on file. If the cottage is booked again for the same dates you get a credit, or you can use that night on a future stay within a year of the original booking.

Do the cottages have kitchens?

Every cottage has a fully equipped kitchen, except the Sea Studio, which has a kitchenette. Each has a refrigerator, a coffee and tea maker, bottled water, air conditioning and ceiling fans.

Is there Wi-Fi?

Free wireless internet is available in the public areas. Guests come here to unplug.

Do I need a car?

Not for the beach, the bar, the kayaks or the bikes. Car rentals start at $75 and are recommended if you want to explore the island's other beaches, caves and settlements.
