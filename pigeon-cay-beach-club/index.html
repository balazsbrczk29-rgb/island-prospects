The palapa honor bar, open to the beach and the breeze.

200 private acres, one beach, and everything to play with

Pigeon Cay sits three miles south of Bennett's Harbour on the north of Cat Island. Behind the beach are soft sandy flats for bonefishing and trails to walk or cycle.

Included in every stay

  • The palapa honor bar on the beach
  • Morning coffee and tea at the beach bar
  • Kayaks, canoes and paddle boards
  • Bicycles for the property's trails
  • Snorkel gear
  • Wi-Fi in the public areas
Watch the Pigeon Cay film

Kayaks wait on the sand. Take one out whenever the water is calm.

Cook in your own kitchen, or let the club bring dinner

Every cottage has a kitchen. Breakfast is offered each morning and dinner from Wednesday to Saturday. A 15% gratuity is added to meals.

Car rentals start at $75, depending on the vehicle. Cribs are available, and the club lists child care. Flats fishing and boat excursions can be arranged.

Tap to add them to your estimate for 2 guests and 7 nights.

Dinner counts the 4 Wednesday to Saturday evenings in your dates. Meal sums include the 15% gratuity.

Two airports on Cat Island, then a short taxi ride

The club is on the north of the island, three miles south of Bennett's Harbour. Tell them your flight and they will arrange the transfer.

  1. 1

    From Florida, direct

    Makers Air flies from Fort Lauderdale Executive (FXE) to New Bight, and since May 2026 to Arthur's Town (ATC) in the north.

  2. 2

    Or through Nassau

    Most guests connect in Nassau. Bahamasair flies twice a week to Arthur's Town, Western Air to New Bight, and Marlin Air charters from Nassau, 242-342-3014.

  3. 3

    Taxi to your cottage

    $60 one way, $120 round trip. A rental car is only worth it if you want to explore.

Fly free from Nassau

Pre-book an air-inclusive stay of 4 to 6 nights and one round-trip ticket from Nassau is free; stay 7 nights and two are, at double occupancy. Book by June 30, 2027 and travel by October 31, 2027. A Bahama Out Islands Promotion Board offer: book at least 14 days ahead, blackout dates and its terms apply.

The terms, in plain words

To book
A card number holds your cottage. No deposit.
To pay
On departure.
To cancel
Free up to 30 days before arrival. Inside 30 days, one night is charged, and can be credited or used within a year.
Taxes
VAT 10% and resort levy 6% on lodging. 15% gratuity on meals.
Do the cottages have kitchens?

Every cottage has a fully equipped kitchen, except the Sea Studio, which has a kitchenette. Each has a coffee and tea maker, bottled water, air conditioning and ceiling fans.

Is there Wi-Fi?

Free wireless internet is available in the public areas.

Can we bring a baby?

Cribs are available, and the club lists child care among its services. Ask when you book.

Do I need a car?

Not for the beach, the bar, the kayaks or the bikes. Car rentals start at $75, depending on the vehicle, if you want to explore the island.
