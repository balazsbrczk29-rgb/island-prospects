Stella Maris

“The turquoise or ink-blue of the two near shorelines.”

The Atlantic side

Porches and pools over the Atlantic at Sand Dollar, Sea La Vie, Carolyn's Favourite, Rainbow Villa and Flamingo House, with Gecko House right on the beach. Big game fishing off the shore.

The calm side

Bonefishing in Adderley's and Glenton's bays, the Moonshine beach bar and grill on the shore, kayaks and paddle boards, and the weekday morning trips to Cape Santa Maria Beach and Columbus Harbour.

Dean's Blue Hole, and the bonefish bays.

Stella Maris Water Sports runs the diving: Shark Reef, The Wreck, Conception Wall and Dean's Blue Hole. Bonefishing is close by in Adderley's and Glenton's bays, and big game fishing waits off both shorelines.
