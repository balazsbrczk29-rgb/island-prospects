01 / Rebuilt and ready

New docks, new fuel dock, same corner of the harbour.

Lighthouse Marina has sat at the foot of the Elbow Reef Lighthouse since 1980. The docks and fuel station have been rebuilt and the gift shop renovated. Excursions, golf carts and bikes are just across the harbour, and the settlement's restaurants and provisioning are within easy reach.
