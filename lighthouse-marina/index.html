Two gas pumps, one diesel, and the only marine fuel station on Elbow Cay.

The docks and the fuel station have been rebuilt, and the gift shop renovated. Fill the tank, then pick up what the day needs on the same dock.

  • Bait: ballyhoo, squid, shrimp, minnows
  • Ice, snacks and soft drinks
  • Tokens for the laundry, sold on the fuel dock

Along the dock, left to right.

Slips, the yard, the store. All of it a short walk from the pumps.

Six protected slips

Up to 60 ft LOA and 6 ft of draft at low water. Deep water dockage, 30 and 50 amp power, hot showers and a laundry, backed by a 60 kW standby generator.
