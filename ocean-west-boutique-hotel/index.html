The terrace

Ten rooms, a pool on the terrace, the sea beyond the balustrade. Choose a room.

Seventy feet above sea level

Lake Killarney to the south. The Atlantic to the north.

The hotel stands on a ridge in western New Providence, with views of the lake on one side and the ocean on the other. Access is for hotel guests only.

Lynden Pindling International is a five minute drive. The Cable Beach strip, with its restaurants and casino, is a few minutes east.

All ten, side by side

Three with a balcony. Seven straight at the sea.

Every room has a fridge, a microwave, cable TV, a hair dryer and free Wi-Fi. Single or double occupancy, children under 12 free.
