02The setting

Seventy feet up, and very quiet.

Ocean West stands on high ground in the western district of New Providence. To the south lies Lake Killarney; to the north, the Atlantic. Access is for guests only.

The airport is five minutes away, and the Cable Beach strip, with its casino, fine dining and nightlife, is three to four miles east.
