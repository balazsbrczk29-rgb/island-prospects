Three things that make a complaint strong

  1. 1Go to the business first.Give them the chance to fix it. If you don't get a satisfactory answer, bring it to us.
  2. 2Take clear photos.Of the item, the fault and anything that came with it, before you hand it back.
  3. 3Lost the receipt? You still have rights.A bank or card record of the payment shows what you paid and when.

Phone, internet or TV?

Complaints about phone, internet and pay TV providers go to URCA, the communications regulator, at its consumer centre. For everything else a business sold you, start here.
