01How it works

Three things you do. Four things we do.

  1. You · 1

    Talk to the business first

    Explain the problem and give them a fair chance to put it right.

  2. You · 2

    Gather your proof

    Receipts, a bank record of the payment, clear photos, messages and any warranty.

  3. You · 3

    Bring it to the Commission

    If they refuse to help, file a complaint and the Commission will step in.
