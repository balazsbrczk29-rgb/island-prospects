Five things to have ready.

  1. 1Your first name and a phone numberSo the Commission can reach you about the complaint.
  2. 2The businessIts name and where it is.
  3. 3What happened, in orderDates, what was promised, what went wrong, what the business said.
  4. 4Your proofReceipt or invoice, a bank record of the payment, clear photos, messages.
  5. 5What you wantA repair, a replacement, a refund: say it plainly.
