1993

Started with not more than a dozen fully trained officers.

Byron C. Rodgers, Chairman and CEO, founded Maximum Security & Patrol Services in 1993. The aim from the first day: to change an industry known for turnover and low standards.

Today the company supplies officers, patrols, alarm monitoring, investigations and consultancy from one office on Carib Road, and is listed on the URCA public register of licensees.
