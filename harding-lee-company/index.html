i.The firm

Small by design. Forty years of Bahamian practice at the head of it.

Harding-Lee & Company has practised since 1996. It is a boutique firm by choice, built on a client-centred approach, and it serves a wide range of institutional and individual clients, at home and worldwide.

Its general practice centres on commercial law, conveyancing, company incorporation, international and domestic transactions, insurance and banking, from chambers in Nassau and an office in Salt Pond, Long Island.
