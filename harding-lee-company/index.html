A senior lawyer's attention, the same one for thirty years.

  1. 1982

    Donna M. Harding-Lee is called to The Bahamas Bar.

  2. 14 years

    At Cash, Fountain & Company, becoming a partner. She works closely with Sir Gerald Cash and tutors under Sir Cyril Fountain, a former Chief Justice.

  3. 1996

    She founds Harding-Lee & Company, a general practice in Nassau with an office in Salt Pond, Long Island.

  4. 2005

    Nadia Hope Johnson, called to the Bar of England & Wales and to The Bahamas Bar that year, joins the firm in November.

  5. Today

    Mrs. Harding-Lee chairs the Board of Bank of The Bahamas. The firm practises in every court of the Commonwealth.

What happens after you write.

  1. 1

    You write or call

    Tell us the kind of matter and how to reach you. A line is enough; keep confidential details for the meeting.

  2. 2

    Your attorney replies

    The attorney who handles that kind of matter contacts you to arrange a first meeting, in Nassau or in Salt Pond.

  3. 3

    Bring what you have

    Deeds, company papers, letters or contracts: whatever you already hold about the matter.

  4. 4

    Terms before work

    How the matter will be handled and charged is agreed with you before the work begins.
