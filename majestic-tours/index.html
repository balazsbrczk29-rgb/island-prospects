Before you go

How do I book a Majestic tour?

Send your plan with the form above, call the tour line on (242) 677-2600, or visit the Majestic desk in your hotel lobby.

Does the tour include a hotel pickup?

It depends on the tour. The Fantastic Exuma Day Trip includes the hotel transfer; the Flying Cloud evening dinner sail lists pickups from Cable Beach, downtown and Paradise Island. Each tour page says which.

How far ahead should I book an airport transfer?

Majestic asks that transportation bookings are finalised at least 5 days ahead, and accessible transport at least 48 hours ahead.

Do I clear US Immigration in Nassau?

Yes. Flights to the United States clear US Immigration and Customs in Nassau, and departure pickup times allow for it.

Which languages do your representatives speak?

Besides English: Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese and Swiss German.
