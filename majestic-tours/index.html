03 · Since 1959

A family idea that became Nassau's tour company.

William “Bill” Saunders was convinced tourism had a future in The Bahamas. With his wife Iris he founded Majestic Tours in June 1959, on one idea that still runs the company: a service second to none.

Today Majestic runs air-conditioned vans, buses, limousines, town cars and SUVs, has representatives in the hotel lobbies, and is a member of ASTA, CHA and IATA.

04 · Arriving in Nassau

Someone holding a Majestic sign, after Customs.

Shared buses stop at three or four hotels. Private cars, limousines and SUVs wait with your name on the sign. Majestic is the only provider on the island offering private transport for disabled guests, arranged 48 hours ahead.
