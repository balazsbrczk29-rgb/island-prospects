Airport transfers · Lynden Pindling International

From the arrivalshall to your hotel.

Air-conditioned vans, buses, limousines, town cars and SUVs, with a representative waiting outside Customs. Please finalise transportation bookings at least 5 days in advance.

01 · How it works

Choose your transfer. Three steps each way.

A shuttle bus that stops at three or four hotels on the way, on arrival and on departure.

Arriving

  1. 1Print your vouchers and travel documents before you fly.
  2. 2After Bahamas Customs, go out of the main doors toward Ground Transportation. A Majestic representative holds a Majestic Tours sign.
  3. 3Take the main crosswalk, turn left to the Majestic buses and show your voucher.

Departing

  1. 1Confirm your return time with the head office or the Majestic desk in your hotel lobby.
  2. 2Be at the front driveway of your hotel or tower at your pickup time. The bus may stop at other hotels.
  3. 3You are picked up well before your flight, allowing for US Immigration and Customs in Nassau.
