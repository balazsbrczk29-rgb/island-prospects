A little history

The first resort in the Out Islands,and now the oldest.

  1. Early 1950s

    Bluff House begins as the private home of Pearce and Kitty Coady, who host boaters from all over the world looking for a night on land, good company and good food.

  2. 1978

    The Davies family takes over. Melissa and Leslie Davies, 24 and 25, run Bluff House until 1986.

  3. 1986

    Martin Havill owns and operates Bluff House until 1995.

  4. 1995

    Roger and Susan Phillips arrive, and welcome guests for the next 17 years.

  5. 2011

    A group of investors led by Roger's nephew, Ian Phillips, takes on the resort.

  6. 2018

    TripAdvisor Certificate of Excellence Hall of Fame; general manager Molly McIntosh named Caribbean Journal's Hotelier of the Year.

  7. December 2025

    VIP Marinas Group, the family-run company of Austin and Jenny Cameron with Felecia and Dan Ongley, acquires Bluff House: its first property in The Bahamas.

01What comes next

A thoughtful revitalization, with the charm kept.

The new owners' long-term plans include enhanced services and amenities, rebuilding the iconic hilltop restaurant, and improvements for cottage owners and guests, while preserving the relaxed island experience everyone loves.
