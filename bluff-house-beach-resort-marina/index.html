No. 3 · The marina

Forty slips, seven feet at low water.

From the Sea of Abaco a dredged channel leads into White Sound. Every boat gets its own dock space and finger pier, the dockmaster meets you, and the Boathouse bar looks over the docks.

No. 5 · The story

Seventy-odd years on the bluff.

Bluff House began as a house, not a hotel. The Coadys opened their door to the boaters coming into White Sound, and it became the first resort in the Bahamas’ Out Islands. It is now the oldest.

Six owners on, the new chapter is VIP Marinas Group’s, their first property in The Bahamas. What has not moved: sunset and moonrise from the balcony, the beach a few steps from the door, and a dockmaster who meets your boat.

  1. Early 1950sThe private home of Pearce and Kitty Coady, who took in the boaters who found White Sound
  2. 1978The Davies family, until 1986
  3. 1986Martin Havill, until 1995
  4. 1995Roger and Susan Phillips, until 2011
  5. 2011Investors led by Ian Phillips
  6. December 2025VIP Marinas Group: Austin and Jenny Cameron, Felecia and Dan Ongley
