The first resort in the Out Islands is still on its bluff.

Twelve acres of palms and pines from White Sound to the Sea of Abaco, the only private beach on Green Turtle Cay, a forty-slip marina, and eight suites with a balcony over the water.

Choose your suiteUpper and lower suites, from $240 a night+1 242 824 9550

In the early 1950s Bluff House was the private home of Pearce and Kitty Coady, who opened their door to the boaters who found their way into White Sound. It became the first resort in the Bahamas’ Out Islands, and it is now the oldest.

The Davies family kept it from 1978 to 1986, Martin Havill until 1995, then Roger and Susan Phillips until 2011, when investors led by Ian Phillips took it on.

In December 2025 it passed to VIP Marinas Group, led by Austin and Jenny Cameron with Felecia and Dan Ongley: their first property in The Bahamas.

What has not moved: sunset and moonrise from the balcony, the beach a few steps from the suite door, and a dockmaster who meets your boat.
