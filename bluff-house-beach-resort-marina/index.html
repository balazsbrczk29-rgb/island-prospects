01Stay

A suite over the water, or a cottage of your own.

8

spacious suites

Air-conditioned, with a hint of the Caribbean and the British West Indies. Every suite has a balcony with sunset and moonrise views of the Sea of Abaco, steps from the island's only private beach.

Upper suites with a king, lower suites with a queen, each with its own living area.

02The marina

Forty slips in White Sound.

From the Sea of Abaco, a dredged 7 ft (MLW) channel leads into White Sound. Every boat gets its own finger pier with power, water and free Wi-Fi, and the dockmaster meets you.
