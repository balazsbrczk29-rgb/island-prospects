# Orange Creek Inn: job spec
Current site: orangecreekinn.com (WordPress one-pager, Book Now goes to BahamaGo) · Kind of site: small inn brochure + booking handoff

1. **The one job:** check your dates and book a studio. BahamaGo is the live engine; an email enquiry is the alternative.
2. **The visitor:** couples, anglers and divers planning a quiet Cat Island week, often comparing the Out Island specials. On a phone, from the US or Nassau. They need to decide whether it is beautiful, what the studio is like, how to get there, and which special applies to them.
3. **The product:**
   - **The studios:** spacious studios with kitchenettes, across the road from a white-sand beach, north of Arthur's Town.
   - **On site:** a grocery store, car and bicycle rental, laundry.
   - **Flights:** Western Air twice a day from Nassau, about $190 round trip. The airport is 3.5 miles away, and they offer pickup.
   - **Specials:** six published Out Island specials with dates.
   - **Nearby:** bonefishing flats and kayaking.
4. **The core tool (the hero):** a dates bar on screen 1.
   - Arrival, nights and guests, with "Check availability" handing off to BahamaGo.
   - Below the bar, the specials that fit your dates light up (from the previous concept, which did this well), plus your getting-here line: which flight on your arrival day, and the taxi or pickup.
5. **Keep:** from their site, the BahamaGo link, the specials with their booking and blackout dates, flight and airport facts, phone and email, and their photo gallery. From the previous concept, the specials matcher.
6. **Fix:**
   - A one-page site built from #!/ links.
   - A "Welcome to" headline, with the beach across the road buried in FAQ 20.
   - Specials in a slider.
   - Prices for flights, taxi and car buried at the bottom of 27 FAQs.
   - Photos shown as small square crops.
7. **Screens:**
   1. The beach across the road, in their aerial or beach photo, with the dates bar.
   2. The studio: photos and what's in it.
   3. The island around you: the flats, kayaks, creek and restaurants.
   4. Getting here, with flights by day and costs.
   5. Specials matched to your dates.
   6. Contact.
8. **Look and feel:** warm, family-run, sunny, unpretentious. No stationery metaphor: the beach and the studio lead. Their photos are 900 px, so use generous framed images rather than full-bleed. One serif and one sans.
