# Nassau Paper Company Ltd.: job spec
Current site: nassaupaperco.com (WordPress / WooCommerce webshop, about 600 products, many "contact us for a quote"; blocks automated previews with 403) · Previous concept: https://balazsbrczk29-rgb.github.io/island-prospects/nassau-paper-company-ltd/ · Kind of site: webshop (catalogue + quote list), handing off to their store or an emailed quote

1. **The one job:** get a Nassau business to send a supply quote list (cups, can liners, roll towels, copy paper, cleaning supplies, print) or order from the store, so the monthly reorder lands at Carib Road instead of another wholesaler.
2. **The visitor:** purchasing and office managers, restaurant and takeaway owners, cleaning crews, schools and event organisers in New Providence; phone or desktop; repeat buyers who need to know: do they stock it, are they open, how fast can I get a quote.
3. **The product (brief, Nov 5):**
   - **Departments:** Disposables (176), Gift Wrap, Bows & Accessories (200), Eco-Friendly (89), Cleaning & Janitorial, Office & School / Copy Paper & Supplies, Hotel Restaurant & Bar, Candy & Gum, Warehouse & Shipping; brands named on the store (Rubbermaid, Dart, Marcal, Solo).
   - **Print:** Napco Printing Service, full commercial printing, same address and phone.
   - **Contact:** #34 Carib Road (Carib & Mount Pleasant Roads), P.O. Box N-4139, Nassau; (242) 393-3556; info@nassaupaperco.com; Mon to Fri 8:00 to 5:00. Tagline "We're more than just paper!"
4. **The core tool (the hero):** the quote list on screen 1.
   - Department rail with real counts; pick items or category-standard lines with case steppers; a print line (business cards, flyers, menus, forms) added to the same list.
   - Collect at Carib Road or "ask about delivery"; the list writes an email to info@nassaupaperco.com and links each item back to its product page on their store; no invented prices.
5. **Keep:**
   - **From their site:** every department and its count, product pages and the "request a quote" route, the shop link, the tagline.
   - **From our previous version:** the department idea (tags that open a department), the quote builder with case steppers, the collect/delivery toggle, the pen-blue and kraft palette.
6. **Fix:**
   - The store refuses link previews and automated tools (403), so shared links show nothing.
   - Hours and address only live in directories; title reads "- Nassau Paper Company Ltd. -".
   - Category typos ("Catagory", "Air Refresheners"); most items have no price and no fast quote path.
   - Printing sits on a separate Facebook page.
7. **Screens:**
   1. One line (what, where, open now) and the quote list beside the department rail.
   2. Departments in depth with counts and real product names.
   3. Napco commercial printing on the same order.
   4. Visiting Carib Road: hours, map, parking side of the store, phone.
   5. FAQ (quotes, delivery, account customers) and the quote list again.
8. **Look and feel:** practical, tidy, stocked; a supply house you trust with the monthly order. Keep Sora + Rubik or move to one serif + sans pairing per the benchmark; paper ground, ink, pen-blue accent for the quote action only. Photography: their product images if the store can be read (a Lovable gallery of their URLs), otherwise studio-lit still life of generic product forms (no brand marks), marked illustrative. Wow: add "Can liners, 10 cases" and the stack on screen grows case by case while the email line updates.

Research this run (Nov 22): none (facts from the Nov 5 brief and audit; the store blocked our tools then).
Assumptions to confirm: item and print examples are category-standard; delivery terms; whether prices can be shown; account customers; photos of the warehouse and counter.
