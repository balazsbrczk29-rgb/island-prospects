# Water Technologies Bahamas Limited: job spec
Current site: none working (air2waterbahamas.com does not resolve; directory listings only) · Previous concept: https://balazsbrczk29-rgb.github.io/island-prospects/water-technologies-bahamas-limited/ (machine illustration, 5-stage air-to-water diagram, machine chooser) · Kind of site: product brochure + demonstration request

1. **The one job:** get a home or office to request a demonstration or quote for an Air2Water machine that makes drinking water from the air.
2. **The visitor:**
   - Nassau households and offices tired of bottled-water delivery.
   - Facility managers.
   - Owners of remote or off-grid properties on the Family Islands.
   On a phone from a Google search or a referral, they must decide: does it really work in our air, which machine fits a home or an office, and who services it.
3. **The product (BahamasIndex and Yabsta listings, rechecked Nov 22: no newer source found):**
   - Air2Water machines (technology by Worldwide Water, Inc.) extract drinking water from the air and filter it continually through a patented UV purification process.
   - Stand-up machines and portable countertop units for home and office.
   - Listing keywords: Dolphin 1 to 4, Triton mobile, industrial water machine, reverse osmosis, ozonation, water coolers.
   - #47 (BahamasIndex) or #46 (Yabsta) Fairwind St, Hawkins Hill, Nassau; phone 242-326-1202.
4. **The core tool (the hero):** a "how much water do you need" chooser on screen 1.
   - Inputs: home or office, number of people, and whether bottled water is delivered today.
   - Output: the type of machine that fits (countertop, stand-up or larger), with no invented litre figures; output figures come only from the manufacturer's sheet if supplied.
   - Hands off to "Call 242-326-1202 for a demonstration", with the summary composed.
5. **Keep:**
   - From their listings: Air2Water, UV purification, home and office units, Hawkins Hill, the phone.
   - From our previous version: the 5-stage air-to-water explainer, the machine chooser and the airy, clean mood. Replace the SVG machine drawing with a studio-lit 3D object or the manufacturer's real product photos, if supplied.
6. **Fix:**
   - The domain is dead, so the business is invisible online.
   - The street number conflicts (46 vs 47).
   - Manufacturer boilerplate is the only description.
   - Nothing shows the machines or how they work.
7. **Screens:**
   1. The machine, with condensation forming, beside the chooser.
   2. How it works: air in, condense, filter, UV, water out, as a scroll stage.
   3. Home and office machines (named models only once confirmed).
   4. Why it suits Nassau's humid air. This is a category claim, phrased carefully.
   5. A demonstration request, the address and the phone.
8. **Look and feel:** clean, airy, clever. Mist and white grounds with one deep-water accent. A studio-lit 3D machine is the wow: droplets bead on the coil, and water fills a glass as you scroll.

Research this run: 1 search (Nov 22). It found no website, no social page and nothing newer than the 2012 listing.
**Confirm the business still trades before approving.** Assumptions to confirm: the street number, the current models, service and warranty, an email or WhatsApp, and real product photos.
