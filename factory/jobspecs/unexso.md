# UNEXSO (Underwater Explorers Society), Sanctuary Bay, Grand Bahama: job spec
Current site: unexso.com has an SSL certificate error. UNEXSO lives inside sanctuarybaybahamas.com (Wix, pages set to noindex), and its experiences are booked through Peek. · Previous concept: https://balazsbrczk29-rgb.github.io/island-prospects/unexso/ · Kind of site: experiences booking (Peek hand-off)

1. **The one job:** book a dolphin, snorkel or glass-bottom-boat experience for a date, through their Peek booking links.
2. **The visitor:**
   - Cruise passengers and resort guests on Grand Bahama deciding today, on a phone.
   - Families with children.
   - Divers who remember UNEXSO and want to know when diving returns.
   They must decide: which experience fits our ages, time and budget, and can we book it now.
3. **The product (sanctuarybaybahamas.com, brief Nov 5; prices exclude VAT):**

   | Experience | Price (adult/child) | Length | Note |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | Dolphin Encounter | $85/$50 | | |
   | Dolphin Swim | $179 | | |
   | Assistant Marine Mammal Specialist | $389 | 4.5 h | |
   | Open Ocean Dolphin Swim | $219 | 2.5 h | returning soon |
   | Eco-Snorkel | $159 | | |
   | Snorkel Sea Safari | $50/$40 | 1.5 h | |
   | Glass Bottom Boat | $65/$55 | 1.5 h | 56 ft boat |
   | Educational Visits | | | |

   - Diving (wreck, shark, concierge) is "returning soon", with a new UNEXSO Lab for education and research, and a coral dome with the Perry Institute.
   - History: opened 1965 as one of the first recreational scuba centres in The Bahamas; the 1971 Nemo submersible; Theo's Wreck sunk in 1982; the first Dolphin Experience in 1987.
   - Contact: +1 242 373 1244/5/6, +1 800 992 3483, info@unexso.com; Sanctuary Bay, Sterling Circle, Freeport; Mon to Fri 9 to 5; 9-acre lagoon.
4. **The core tool (the hero):** an experience picker on screen 1.
   - Inputs: who is coming (adults and children), how much time you have (port-day window), in the water or dry.
   - It shows the fitting experiences with real prices, lengths and photos, and a live total for the group (prices x people, plus "VAT added").
   - Each "Book" opens its exact Peek link.
   - Diving shows "Returning soon: tell me when", which composes an email to info@.
5. **Keep:**
   - From their site: every experience with its price, length and Peek link; the lagoon and dolphins; the Lab and coral dome; the contacts and hours.
   - From our previous version: the 1965 to 1987 history timeline and the experience pages.
6. **Fix:**
   - unexso.com fails with an SSL error.
   - The Sanctuary Bay pages are noindex, so UNEXSO cannot be found in Google.
   - The history is buried.
   - Diving says "returning soon" without capturing interest.
   - Stock header photos.
7. **Screens:**
   1. A real lagoon or dolphin photograph with the experience picker.
   2. The experiences compared: price, length, age, in the water or dry.
   3. Sixty years under the water: the 1965 to 1987 timeline with archive photos.
   4. Diving returns: the Lab, the coral dome and "tell me when".
   5. Getting there from the cruise port or a hotel (only sourced facts), hours, contacts and booking.
8. **Look and feel:** bright, living water, family-safe, with legendary history. Their own lagoon and archive photos. A living photograph (subtle water caustics on the lagoon photo) is the wow; the archive timeline is in period-true monochrome.
