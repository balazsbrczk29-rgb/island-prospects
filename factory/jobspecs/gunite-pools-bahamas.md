# Gunite Pools of Nassau (listed as Gunite Pools Bahamas): job spec
Current site: gunitepoolsbahamas.com (Wix; pages for New Pool Construction, Pool Maintenance, Fiberglass Tanks, Repair & Renovation, Residential portfolio, Commercial "Showpieces", About, a blog; every service ends in a "Request Quote" contact form; the "Store" tile links to a Google Maps search) · Previous concept: none (never built) · Kind of site: trade brochure + quote request (two kinds of buyer: a new pool, and monthly service)

1. **The one job:** get a quote request with enough detail to act on: either "build or renovate my pool" (with the site, shape and size in mind) or "put my pool on your monthly service programme" (with the pool and the address).
2. **The visitor:**
   - Homeowners and second-home owners in New Providence (and now Great Exuma for service), on a phone or laptop, planning a new pool or tired of an unreliable pool man; often owners who are away and need to trust someone with the pool.
   - Architects, developers and hotel managers looking for a contractor for a large or complex commercial pool.
   They must decide whether this company can build what they imagine, whether service will actually happen when promised, and how to start.
3. **The product (their site, read live Nov 21):**
   - **Services:** new pool construction ("geometric or free-form, clean simple lines or meandering curves, anything is possible"), pool maintenance and service, repair and renovation, fiberglass tanks, a retail store; "Now offering Pool Service in Great Exuma".
   - **The service programme, their three promises:** monthly field-manager visit with an equipment, chemical and pool health report; technicians tracked by GPS so clients can see when staff were on site; technicians trained and reviewed quarterly.
   - **Track record:** founded 1972 by Peter and John Whitehead, Thomas joined in 1973, Phillip joined in 2008, now entering its third generation of family ownership; wholly Bahamian owned; "one of the largest pool construction companies in the Caribbean", "the go-to company for high-end residential pools in the Bahamas", residential portfolio and commercial showpieces pages.
   - **People shown on their site:** Phillip Whitehead (President), Peter Whitehead and Thomas Whitehead (Vice Presidents), José Viana (Director of Construction).
   - **Photography:** their own project photos on the Wix CDN (residential and commercial pools, construction shots, a 4000 px wide hero), plus some stock-named files (the "2021-08-30 utc" envato-style images) not to be used.
   - **Contact:** contact-us form only seen; record lists Montrose Avenue, Nassau. Phone, email and store hours to be read from their contact page at build time.
4. **The core tool (the hero):** a two-door quote builder on screen 1, over their best finished pool photograph.
   - **Door 1, "Build or renovate":** pick a pool type from their own portfolio photos (geometric, free-form, infinity or vanishing edge, spa or water feature, renovation of an existing pool), the island, the stage (dreaming, have plans, have an architect), rough size as a slider drawing the outline; optional photo of the yard. Hands off as a structured quote request (form to their inbox, plus WhatsApp if they have it).
   - **Door 2, "Service my pool":** island (New Providence or Great Exuma), pool size and type, how often you are on island, current problems (green water, equipment, leaks); shows exactly what the programme includes (monthly report, GPS-verified visits, trained technicians) and a sample of what the monthly report looks like. Hands off as a service request.
   - No prices are invented: "your quote follows a site visit".
5. **Keep:**
   - **From their site:** every service and its page, the Great Exuma service news, the three service promises, the 1972 family story and the four named people, the residential and commercial portfolios with their own photos, fiberglass tanks and the store, the blog articles (safety covers, backyard renovation), Request Quote on every service.
   - **From our previous version:** none, this is the first build.
6. **Fix:**
   - Every page ends in the same generic contact form; nothing captures what kind of pool or what kind of service, so every enquiry starts from zero.
   - The best selling point, GPS-tracked visits and a monthly health report, is a small paragraph on one inner page.
   - "Since 1972, third generation" is hidden on the About page; the homepage gives no year and no phone number.
   - The "Store" tile sends people to a Google Maps search; there are no store hours or address on the site.
   - Stock photographs mixed in with their own work; duplicated "Our Values" heading; image file names like "Amarillo Experimental Arte Deco ... Portafolio" leak into the page.
   - The site title says "Gunite Pools of Bahamas", the company says "Gunite Pools of Nassau", the domain says "Gunite Pools Bahamas".
7. **Screens:**
   1. A finished pool of theirs, full width, with the two-door quote builder.
   2. The work: residential and commercial pools as a large swipeable portfolio, each with island and type, free-form and geometric side by side.
   3. How a pool gets built: design, dig, steel, gunite shell, tile and finish, using their own construction photos (a scroll sequence from bare ground to water).
   4. The service programme: the monthly report, the GPS visit log, the quarterly trained technicians, New Providence and Great Exuma.
   5. Repairs, renovations, fiberglass tanks and the store.
   6. Since 1972: the Whitehead family and José Viana, three generations, then contact, store hours and the map.
8. **Look and feel:**
   - **Mood:** water you could step into, engineering you can trust; calm, bright, exact, a family firm that builds the island's best pools.
   - **Type:** a precise grotesk for display in sentence case with a readable text face; tabular figures for dimensions and the report sample. Not condensed uppercase.
   - **Palette:** pale limestone and white plaster grounds, pool-water turquoise as the accent drawn from their own photos, deep shell grey for text. No blue-purple gradients, no orange.
   - **Photography:** their own project and construction photos only (never the stock-named files); real-time water caustics on the hero photo only if it reads as real, never as a toy.
   - **Wow:** in the "Build" door, as you drag the size slider and choose free-form or geometric, the outline is drawn over a real yard photo and fills with water, so the visitor sees their pool before they ask for the quote.
