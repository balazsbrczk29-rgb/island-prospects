# Greenwood Beach Resort: job spec
Current site: greenwoodbeachresort.com (WordPress + Elementor 2026, booking engine direct-book.com/properties/greenwoodbeachresort) · Previous concept: https://balazsbrczk29-rgb.github.io/island-prospects/greenwood-beach-resort/ (gen 2, needs-review) · Kind of site: booking

1. **The one job:** check dates and book one of the two room types through their direct-book engine. A whitetip dive package enquiry comes second.
2. **The visitor:** US, Canadian and French-speaking divers, kiters and couples planning weeks ahead on a phone. They compare Greenwood with other Cat Island stays and OTAs. They need to know:
   - which room fits them;
   - what they will do in the water, and when (whitetip season, kite wind);
   - how hard the trip is to get here.
3. **The product:**
   - **Rooms:** Executive King Room and Executive Family Room (private bath, patio).
   - **Meals:** breakfast, lunch and dinner on the beachfront terrace.
   - **On the water:** supervised diving and kitesurfing from the beach, and oceanic whitetip dives from March to June.
   - **The place:** Port Howe, at the south end of Cat Island, hosted by Pauline and Antoine.
   - **Getting here:** Western Air and Makers Air to New Bight, plus taxis and car rental.
4. **The core tool (the hero):** a room and dates strip on screen 1.
   - Pick the King or Family room (their own photos swap in, with sleeps and beds), then arrival, nights and adults.
   - "Check dates" opens direct-book with the dates filled in.
   - A season line beside it changes with the month: "Whitetips: March to June" and "Kite wind" (the months they publish).
   - Under it sits a whitetip package builder (nights, divers, non-divers, board) that sends to their email.
5. **Keep:**
   - From their site: the direct-book handoff, both rooms, restaurant hours, the WhatsApp dinner booking, the flight, taxi and car guide, and WhatsApp +1 242 808 1846 plus the info@ email.
   - From our previous version: the letterbox photography of the water and the dates composer.
6. **Fix:**
   - Identical room descriptions, and the photos swapped between the two rooms.
   - Dead buttons: View room, Discover activities, Lunch, Dinner.
   - Port Howe is never named.
   - Whitetip season buried on a sub-page.
   - Star-graphic review cards.
7. **Screens:**
   1. Their kite or whitetip photo, Port Howe named, and the room and dates strip with the season line.
   2. The two rooms compared (photos, sleeps, beds, who each is for).
   3. The water by season: whitetips (March to June), kite, reef and snorkel, each with their own photo, plus the package builder.
   4. The terrace: meals and hours, with WhatsApp to book dinner.
   5. Pauline and Antoine.
   6. Getting here: from the USA or from Nassau, to the airport, then to the door.
   7. Contact, then book.
8. **Look and feel:**
   - **Mood:** salt-washed, remote, personal.
   - **Photography:** the water photography is the star, framed at its native size.
   - **Type:** one serif with a clean sans.
   - **Palette:** deep sea blue for the whitetip field and limestone for the ground.
   - **Wow:** the season line on screen 1 turns the calendar into a reason to book.
