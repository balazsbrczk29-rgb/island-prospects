# Stella Maris Resort Club & Marina: job spec
Current site: stellamarisresort.com (WordPress + Elementor; a rates page with two season tables, all-inclusive packages, specials, a generic contact form; reservations by email or phone, credit card only) · Previous concept: https://balazsbrczk29-rgb.github.io/island-prospects/stella-maris-resort-club/ (gen 2, three pages: split editorial hero with a star-of-the-sea mark, a two-shoreline band, a "Who's coming?" house finder over the ten accommodation types, activity chips composing the enquiry, Stay / Wedding / Fly in tabs) · Kind of site: brochure + priced stay enquiry (no booking engine seen; the rates page mentions online reservation deposits, to confirm)

1. **The one job:** pick the right room, cottage or house for your group and dates, see what it really costs, and send the stay request to Reservations@stellamarisresort.com (or call 242-338-2050); flights in through the resort's own air service as the second step.
2. **The visitor:** repeat Out Island travellers, divers, bonefishers, older couples from the US and Europe (the site is translated into ten languages), and families or friend groups renting a three or four bedroom house; planning months ahead on a laptop or phone, comparing with Exuma and Eleuthera. They must decide which of ten places to stay fits their party, what a week costs once the surcharges are added, and how they get to Long Island.
3. **The product:**
   - **Ten accommodation types** (their accommodations page, two photos each): Atlantic ocean view hotel rooms (king or two doubles); one-bedroom cottages (king, small living room, facing a small fresh water pool, partial ocean view); one-bedroom apartments (two doubles, pull-out couch, kitchenette); Sand Dollar Cottage 2BR (screened porch over the Atlantic); Sea La Vie Cottage 2BR; Gecko House 3BR (on the beach); Carolyn's Favourite 3BR (panoramic Atlantic, large open kitchen); Rainbow Villa 3BR (infinity salt water pool on the Atlantic); Flamingo House 4BR (porch over the Atlantic); Dolphin House 4BR (fresh water pool among coconut palms).
   - **Published nightly rates, two seasons** (rates page, updated 19 Nov 2026), Summer 2026 (20 Apr to 19 Dec 2026) / Winter 2026-27 (19 Dec 2026 to 18 Apr 2027):
     - Hotel room / apartment: single 190 / 210, double 230 / 250, triple 270 / 290.
     - 1BR cottage: single 240 / 260, double 280 / 290, triple 320 / 330.
     - Sand Dollar and Sea La Vie (each): double 360 / 420, triple 400 / 460, quad 440 / 500.
     - Gecko House and Carolyn's Favourite: six 660 / 700. Rainbow Villa with pool: six 770 / 800. Flamingo House: six 660 / 700. Dolphin House with pool: six 770 / 800.
   - **Surcharges:** VAT 10%; resort levy 6% + 10% VAT; maid service $6 per person per night; energy surcharge $4 pp/night (hotel rooms) or $7 pp/night (cottages and villas) + 10% VAT; additional adult $40 per night (subject to surcharges); children 12 and under free.
   - **Included:** three pools and beach towels, weekday morning beach excursions to Cape Santa Maria Beach or Columbus Harbour, bicycles, Wi-Fi around the clubhouse.
   - **Terms:** deposits 1 to 3 nights: 1 night; 3 to 5: 2 nights; 5 to 7: 3 nights; 10+: 5 nights; packages $750. Cancellation $75 + VAT; deposit forfeited inside 14 days (21 days at Christmas and New Year). MAP meal plan $83 pp/night + 15% gratuity + 10% VAT, drinks excluded.
   - **Also on their site:** Sun & Sea all-inclusive packages (5 nights single $1,867, double $2,945, and 7 nights, valid to 19 Dec 2026), specials ($250 air credit on air-inclusive packages from the US, blackout 3 to 6 July 2026), diving with Stella Maris Water Sports (Shark Reef, The Wreck, Conception Wall, Dean's Blue Hole), bonefishing in Adderley's and Glenton's bays, the Moonshine beach bar and grill, weddings, the resort air service (Stellamarisresortairservice@gmail.com), Stella Maris Airport (SML) with flights to Nassau.
   - **Contact:** 242-338-2050 / 2051 / 2027, fax 242-338-2052, Reservations@, Jill@, info@stellamarisresort.com, Ocean View Drive, P.O. Box LI 30-105, Stella Maris, Long Island; 29 rooms (Out Islands board listing).
4. **The core tool (the hero):** a stay planner on screen 1, on their photograph of the Atlantic shore.
   - Arrival date, nights, adults and children 12 and under; the season is picked automatically from the arrival date (and split if a stay crosses 19 December).
   - The ten places to stay appear as one row of photographs, ordered by how well they fit the party (bedrooms, own pool, on the beach, kitchen); places that cannot sleep the party are dimmed with the reason.
   - Choosing one shows the real total, itemised: nights x the published rate for that occupancy, extra adults, resort levy, VAT, maid service and energy surcharge per person per night, then optional MAP for the party, and the deposit due under their rule. Tabular figures, "estimate, confirmed by reservations" beside the total.
   - A second tab compares the same dates against the Sun & Sea all-inclusive package where it applies.
   - "Send this stay to reservations" opens a prefilled email (place, dates, party, estimate, activities ticked); "Add flights from Nassau" adds the air service address and the airport; call buttons beside.
5. **Keep:**
   - **From their site:** every accommodation type with its own two photos and its exact description, the full rate tables for both seasons, every surcharge, deposit and cancellation rule as written, the packages and specials, diving, fishing and activities, weddings, the air service, all phone numbers and email addresses, the ten-language translation (as a language switch).
   - **From our previous version:** the "Who's coming?" house finder (it becomes the stay planner's fit ordering), the two-shoreline band in their words ("the turquoise or ink-blue of the two near shorelines"), the activity chips that compose the enquiry, the Stay / Wedding / Fly in routing, Sorts Mill Goudy with Mulish, the paper, lagoon and atlantic palette.
6. **Fix:**
   - The rates live on a separate page as raw tables, with six surcharge lines nobody can add up; no page tells a family what a week in Gecko House actually costs.
   - Accommodation photos sit with no rate, no capacity and no way to ask about that unit.
   - The phone shows as "3382050/1/27".
   - The air service and the airport, their best answer to "how do we get there", are buried; there is no getting-here page.
   - The marina is in the name but never described.
   - The previous concept's finder had no prices, although the resort publishes them.
   - Earlier audit found hidden spam links on the homepage; re-check before pitching (a strong opening if still present).
7. **Screens:**
   1. The Atlantic shore photograph with the stay planner: dates, party, the ten places in a row, the itemised total, send.
   2. The two shorelines: Atlantic side and the calm side, where each house sits between them.
   3. The places to stay, one by one: both photos, beds, own pool or beach, the rate for your party in each season.
   4. What is included and what to do: beach excursions, bikes, pools, diving (Dean's Blue Hole, Conception Wall), bonefishing bays, the Moonshine bar; packages and specials.
   5. Getting here: your flight to Nassau, the resort air service or a scheduled flight to Stella Maris Airport, the drive to the club; the fly-in enquiry.
   6. Weddings and celebrations, then contact, policies in plain words, and the map.
8. **Look and feel:**
   - **Mood:** an old-school family Out Island club, unhurried, sociable, two blues; honest and well kept rather than glossy.
   - **Type:** keep Sorts Mill Goudy (display and italic) with Mulish; tabular figures for every rate and total.
   - **Palette:** keep the previous version: paper, ink, lagoon accent, atlantic, shallows, sand. No orange.
   - **Photography:** their own site photos only (hero, resort, each unit's two images), never their "OLYMPUS DIGITAL CAMERA" gallery captions; Wikimedia Commons for Dean's Blue Hole and the Long Island coast, credited, if their own images are too small.
   - **Wow:** as you change the party size, the row of houses reorders itself and the total recalculates line by line, so a family of six sees Gecko House on the beach for their week with every surcharge already added, before they have written a word.
