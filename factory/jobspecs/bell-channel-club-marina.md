# Bell Channel Club & Marina: job spec
Current site: none (bellchannelclub.com now serves a gambling site; only an unclaimed marinas.com listing) · Previous concept: https://balazsbrczk29-rgb.github.io/island-prospects/bell-channel-club-marina/ (chart of the inlet, a who-is-asking router, call the club office) · Kind of site: brochure + call (one page)

1. **The one job:** get the right person to call the club office, (242) 373-2673: an owner or guest, a prospective buyer or renter, or a visiting captain asking about dock space.
2. **The visitor:** current owners and their guests (US, Canada, Freeport), buyers and renters researching Bell Channel after seeing a realtor listing, and boaters who found the marinas.com pin. Mostly on phones. They must decide: is this the real club, where exactly is it, is the marina for me, who do I call.
3. **The product:**
   - A gated condominium and townhouse club on both the canal and the beach, overlooking the inlet into Port Lucaya Marketplace (26.5147, -78.6368).
   - A private marina for owners ("if available").
   - Heated pool, tennis courts, owners' library, a limited number of storage garages, reverse-osmosis water for all units, the beach maintained by the HOA.
   - Phone (242) 373-2673, fax 242-373-3802, P.O. Box F-44053, Freeport. Unknowns (slip count, depths, rates, email, number of units) are shown as "ask the office".
4. **The core tool (the hero):** a "Who's asking?" chooser on screen 1 over a real map of the Bell Channel inlet: Owner or guest / Buying or renting / Visiting boat. Each answer shows the two or three facts that person needs (for a boat: private marina for owners, ask the office about availability before you come in; for a buyer: canal and beach, pool, tennis, garages) and one large Call button, plus directions to the gate.
5. **Keep:** from their old presence: the phone and fax, the private-marina-for-owners message, the amenities from the listings, the map pin. From our previous version: the who-is-asking router and the inlet chart.
6. **Fix:**
   - The old domain sends owners and buyers to a gambling site.
   - The only live listing is unclaimed, with no description or photos.
   - Nothing says the marina is for owners, so boaters call blind.
   - Amenities are known only from scattered realtor listings.
   - No modern contact path.
7. **Screens:**
   1. The name, where it sits on the inlet, the who's-asking chooser and Call.
   2. The club between canal and beach: what owners have (pool, tennis, library, garages, RO water, beach).
   3. The marina: private, for owners, what to ask the office.
   4. Finding the gate (map, Port Lucaya landmarks) and the office contact block.
8. **Look and feel:** quiet, private, between two waters, sun-faded nautical. No own photographs exist: carry the page with a real map of the inlet (MapLibre or a chart drawn from real coastline) and Commons photographs of Port Lucaya and Bell Channel if excellent ones exist; never realtor photos. The wow: the inlet drawn so you see the club sitting exactly where the channel meets the sea.
