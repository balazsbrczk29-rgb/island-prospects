# Schooner Bay Marina: job spec
Current site: schoonerbaybahamas.com (WordPress; homepage leads with real estate, marina facts on an inner page and a 2017 handout PDF; no online slip request) · Previous concept: https://balazsbrczk29-rgb.github.io/island-prospects/schooner-bay-marina/ (gen 2, three pages: a "check her against the harbour" fit checker on the homepage, the numbers page, the village and Discovery Stay page, enquiry tabs Slip / Fuel / Discovery Stay) · Kind of site: brochure + slip and fuel request (marina), real-estate enquiry (village)

1. **The one job:** request a slip (or fuel) from the Harbour Master for your boat and dates. Second job: book a Discovery Stay as a prospective buyer.
2. **The visitor:**
   - **Sportfish and cruising-yacht owners and captains** (US Southeast) running between the Abaco Cays and Nassau, or heading to Hole in the Wall. Phone in hand, patchy signal. They must decide: will she fit (length, draft at MLW), is there fuel and power, will someone answer on 16, what will it cost.
   - **Second-home buyers** arriving by boat or air, deciding whether to come for a Discovery Stay.
3. **The product:**
   - **The harbour:** "not a conventional marina" but a working, living harbour at the centre of a non-gated village on Great Abaco's south-east shore; published position N 26 09.529', W 077 10.633'; "closest marina to the famed Hole in the Wall sport fishing grounds"; protected basin on the route from the Abaco Cays to Nassau.
   - **Specs:** vessels up to 80 ft with 8 ft at MLW (marina page; the Abaco page says 70 ft); diesel and gas at market rates, call ahead; 30 and 50 amp; Wi-Fi, restrooms, showers; General Store (ice, marine supplies, snacks), laundry delivery, pet friendly, local transport help, golf carts, bait and tackle.
   - **Rates:** transient dockage daily, weekly or monthly; daily $3.00 per ft, varying with length of stay; covered secure boat storage for residents.
   - **People and hours:** Harbour Master Albert Russel, 7:30am to 4:30pm daily, VHF 16 and 77, 242.366.2044, harbourmaster@schoonerbaybahamas.com; main line 242.699.3470; Sales June Russell 242.577.5577.
   - **Ashore:** Blackfly Lodge in the village; Discovery Stay (3 nights / 4 days hosted, for prospective buyers); Marsh Harbour about 30 minutes, Sandy Point airstrip (MYAS) about 20 minutes.
4. **The core tool (the hero):** "Can I come in?" on screen 1, on their marina header panorama.
   - Length overall and draft (sliders and numbers) answered live against 80 ft and 8 ft at MLW, with the tide caveat; arrival date and nights.
   - The estimate: LOA x $3.00 x nights for daily stays, with a "weekly and monthly rates on request" line; fuel and power as checkboxes.
   - The position (N 26 09.529', W 077 10.633') shown on a real interactive chart (MapLibre with an open nautical or satellite layer, or a static chart if tiles are blocked), with Hole in the Wall and the Abaco Cays to Nassau route marked so the captain sees why to stop here.
   - "Request a slip" sends boat, LOA, draft, dates, power and fuel to the Harbour Master by email; "Call" and "VHF 16 / 77" beside it, with office hours.
5. **Keep:**
   - **From their site:** every spec and rate, the published position, Harbour Master name, hours, VHF and email, main line and sales line, the marina handout, the Discovery Stay and three steps to buying, Blackfly Lodge, their own photography (marina header panorama, harbour amenities, harbour village, community header, villa).
   - **From our previous version:** the fit checker (now with the chart and the estimate), the numbers page as a quiet spec table, the enquiry tabs Slip / Fuel / Discovery Stay, the By sea / By air arrival switch.
6. **Fix:**
   - The homepage still leads with a 2020 "We are open" banner and a real-estate pitch; a boater sees nothing about slips, fuel or VHF.
   - 80 ft on the marina page, 70 ft on the Abaco page.
   - The Harbour Master's number (366-2044) differs from the main line (699-3470), with no explanation.
   - A 2014 "Marina Spotlight" post still lists 14 transient slips and rates "starting at $2 per foot", against today's $3.00.
   - Specs are buried in paragraphs and a 2017 PDF; there is no way to request a slip or fuel online; Hole in the Wall is one sentence on a secondary page.
7. **Screens:**
   1. The harbour panorama with "Can I come in?" working: fit, estimate, chart, request, VHF.
   2. Why stop here: the chart from the Abaco Cays to Nassau, Hole in the Wall closest, the protected basin.
   3. The numbers: specs, power, fuel, rates, hours, in one table that reads on a phone.
   4. Ashore: the village, the General Store, Blackfly Lodge, the beach.
   5. Live here: Discovery Stay and three steps to buying, with the sales enquiry.
   6. Contact: Harbour Master, main line, sales, position, getting here by air.
8. **Look and feel:**
   - **Mood:** precise, salt-washed, sporting, sheltered, neighbourly.
   - **Type:** keep the previous version's Ibarra Real Nova and Geist pairing; large tabular numbers in the tool.
   - **Palette:** chart paper ground, deep Atlantic blue, a shallow-bank green for the chart; no orange or red.
   - **Photography:** their own site images; the chart carries the screens where photos are thin.
   - **Wow:** a captain sets 72 ft and 6 ft, the chart draws the run from the Abaco Cays to the harbour mouth with Hole in the Wall beside it, and the request is ready to send.
