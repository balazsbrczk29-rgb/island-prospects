# Anna's Wedding Planning & Consulting: job spec
Current site: annasweddingbahamas.com (WordPress, packages with a booking form) · Previous concept: the gen-1 version (restored, live now; Balazs likes its composition) · Kind of site: packages + date request

1. **The one job:** choose a package and request your wedding date with Anna.
2. **The visitor:** US, Canadian and international couples aged 30 to 45, planning a Nassau beach wedding or a cruise-day wedding months ahead, on a phone. They need to decide which package fits their guest count and arrival (staying in Nassau or in port for one day), what it really costs, and whether Anna is trustworthy.
3. **The product:**
   - **Beach packages:** Basic $950, Classic $1,750, Beach $2,990, Romantic $3,995.
   - **Cruise packages:** Cruise for 25 guests $3,495, Blue Ocean for 30 $7,645, Tropical for 30 to 40 $13,895.
   - **Other packages:** Ethnic, Deluxe and Renewal of vows.
   - **The planner:** Annamae Fox, over two decades of weddings.
   - **Services:** licence paperwork handled ($100 fee as published), full planning and day coordination.
4. **The core tool (the hero):** the package finder from the previous version, elevated. You set:
   - how you arrive: staying in Nassau, or a cruise port day;
   - your guest count;
   - the feel you want.

   The fitting packages come up with Anna's own photos, the price and what is included. "Request this date" builds the summary to call or send. Next to it sits the licence checklist, so couples see the paperwork is handled.
5. **Keep:** from their site, every package and price, the licence requirements, and the phones. From our previous version, the whole composition (split editorial hero with the arched photo), the package finder with arrival mode and guest slider, the licence checklist with its progress ring, and the date planner. The rebuild refines this; it does not replace it.
6. **Fix:**
   - Prices hidden inside package pages.
   - 2015 photos over http.
   - A repetitive slider.
   - No email or WhatsApp.
   - The cruise purser rule buried.
   - 20+ years of experience hidden on the About page.
7. **Screens:** keep the previous version's order, with the finder moved up so it is within the first two screens on a phone.
8. **Look and feel:** warm, romantic, assured. Balazs asked for new fonts: a refined serif display with a graceful text face, not Gloock and Figtree. Better rhythm and spacing. Her photos stay within native size, framed rather than blown up.
