# Bahamas Bus & Truck Co. Ltd.: job spec
Current site: bahamasbus.com (custom site; new-vehicle lineup with side views, used inventory with stock IDs and prices, departments page) · Previous concept: https://balazsbrczk29-rgb.github.io/island-prospects/bahamas-bus-truck-co-ltd/ (vehicle parade, lineup filter by use, department switcher) · Kind of site: dealership: inventory + department requests

1. **The one job:** turn a visitor into a showroom call or visit about a specific vehicle. The second job is a service, parts or test-drive request routed to the right department.
2. **The visitor:**
   - Nassau families.
   - Contractors needing trucks and flat beds.
   - Taxi and church operators needing vans.
   - Fleet buyers.
   - Owners needing service or parts.
   They are mostly on a phone and must decide: which model fits my use, is it in stock, what does a used one cost, and when is the right department open.
3. **The product (their site, brief Nov 5):**
   - Brands: Chrysler, Dodge, Jeep, RAM, GWM, Mitsubishi, CMC, FUSO, JCB.
   - 16 new models with side-view images and one-line descriptions.
   - Used vehicles with stock IDs and prices.
   - Departments:
     - Sales: Mon to Fri 8 to 5, Sat 9 to 1.
     - Service: Mon to Fri 8 to 5, by appointment, drop by 9 am, no body shop.
     - Parts: Mon to Fri 8 to 5, Sat 8 to 12, bring the VIN; genuine parts plus a small Japanese selection.
     - Accounts: Mon to Fri 8 to 5, no walk-ins.
   - #44 Montrose Avenue, Palmdale; (242) 322-1722 to 1725; info@, sales@, service@ and parts@bahamasbus.com.
   - Since 1944.
4. **The core tool (the hero):** a lineup finder on screen 1.
   - Pick the use (family, work and haulage, people and taxi, heavy equipment) and see their real models drive in, each with its own one-line description.
   - Each model has "Ask about this vehicle", which composes an email to sales@ with the model, or opens a call.
   - A department strip routes service (with VIN and date), parts (VIN) and test-drive requests to the right address.
5. **Keep:**
   - From their site: every new model with its photo and description, the used list with stock IDs and prices, the four departments with their own hours, emails and rules, and 1944.
   - From our previous version: the vehicle parade, the filter by use, the department switcher and the request tabs.
6. **Fix:**
   - The social icons go to bare facebook.com and instagram.com.
   - The header phone link is malformed.
   - The hours conflict (8:30 vs 8:00).
   - The stale "in stock this summer '26" line.
   - The 1944 heritage is hidden.
   - There is no service or test-drive request with a VIN.
7. **Screens:**
   1. The lineup finder with the vehicles driving in, and the phone.
   2. Used vehicles: a sortable list with prices and stock IDs.
   3. Departments: service, parts, sales and accounts, each with hours, rules and its request.
   4. Since 1944 on Montrose Avenue: brands, address, map link and close.
8. **Look and feel:** dependable, workmanlike, heritage. A confident grotesk and a road-line motif, asphalt and paper grounds with one accent for actions, their own vehicle side views large and crisp. Wow: the chosen use rolls its vehicles in along the road line in true scale order, small car to FUSO truck.

Research this run: none new (the Nov 5 brief was reused; recheck the used list at build time).
Assumptions to confirm: the use groupings are ours; which of the extra phone numbers belong to which department; the current used stock.
