# Kreston Bahamas: job spec
Current site: https://krestonbs.com/ (WordPress, 2022 content, contact form only) · Previous concept: none · Kind of site: brochure + enquiry

1. **The one job:** a business owner or finance lead sends an enquiry to the right partner for their need (audit, tax, accounts, advisory, outsourcing).
2. **The visitor:** an owner or CFO of an SME or mid-market company in The Bahamas, or an international client of the Kreston Global network. Often on a laptop at work, sometimes a phone. Must decide: do they handle my kind of business, and who do I speak to.
3. **The product:** five services (Accountancy, Advisory, Audit & Assurance, Outsourcing, Taxation), the two partners (Pretino P. Albury, Sean I. Rolle), membership of Kreston Global ("Kreston" is ancient Greek for "trust"), sector expertise incl. central and local government, "a personal and confidential service".
4. **The core tool (the hero):** a service finder. Pick what you need ("my annual audit", "VAT and tax", "books and payroll", "advice on a decision", "outsource finance") and the page shows the matching service, what the firm needs from you (a checklist of generic, non-numeric items), and a pre-filled enquiry (first name, surname, email, company, enquiry) that hands off to their contact form/email. No prices, no deadlines, no invented legal dates.
5. **Keep:** the five service names, the "Trust" meaning, Kreston Global link, the two partners and their photos, news/insight articles (list as links to their real posts), the enquiry fields and privacy consent.
6. **Fix:** stale 2022 look and news, stock slider photos, "Add testimonial here" placeholder, no way to tell which service fits, generic "Get in touch".
7. **Screens:**
   1. Hero: firm name, one line, the service finder working, call/enquiry in reach.
   2. The five services in plain words, expandable, same order as the finder.
   3. Partners (real photos from their site) and the Kreston Global network.
   4. Insights: their real articles as a list.
   5. Close: enquiry form (their fields), address/phone to be confirmed from their contact page.
8. **Look and feel:** quiet, exact, trustworthy. Warm paper ground, deep ink, one accent for the enquiry action only. One serif plus one clean sans. Generous space. Wow moment: choosing a need rearranges the page into your service, the checklist writing itself in tabular type. No stock photos; team photos are theirs, the rest is typographic.

Open facts to confirm (do not invent): office address, phone, email, partner bios, any credentials.
