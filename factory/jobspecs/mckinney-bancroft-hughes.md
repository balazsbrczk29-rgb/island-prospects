# McKinney, Bancroft & Hughes: job spec
Current site: mckinney.com.bs (WordPress; contact page set to noindex; images served from a staging server) · Previous concept: https://balazsbrczk29-rgb.github.io/island-prospects/mckinney-bancroft-hughes/ · Kind of site: law firm brochure + consultation enquiry (phone)

1. **The one job:** get an international or Bahamian client with a matter (dispute, property, trust, company, family, immigration, shipping) to call the right partner's desk to arrange a consultation.
2. **The visitor:** banks, trust companies, hotel groups, developers, HNW families and their foreign counsel on desktop; Bahamian businesses and individuals on phones. They judge standing, longevity, network and discretion; they need to know: does the firm handle my matter, who leads it, how do I reach them.
3. **The product (their site, Nov 5):**
   - **Practice:** 12 practice groups as listed on their site; Lex Mundi exclusive Bahamas member (21,000+ lawyers, 100+ countries, a single point of contact); over 30 specialists and a support staff of more than 100.
   - **People:** partners John F. Wilson KC (Senior Partner), Sean N.C. Moree KC, April N. Turner, Timothy A. Eneas KC; each client the responsibility of an individual lawyer.
   - **History:** established 1945 by Donald McKinney, Sir Oswald Bancroft and Sir Leonard Knowles; Dr. Colin A. Hughes joined 1947; from 3 attorneys and 3 staff; Boyle Building, then George Street from 1990; the "rings of longevity" mentor and pupil idea.
   - **Offices:** Mareva House, 4 George Street, P.O. Box N-3937, Nassau; Freeport; Lyford Cay. Tel +1 242 502 9750.
4. **The core tool (the hero):** the matter finder on screen 1: choose your situation in plain words (a dispute, buying property, setting up a trust or company, a family matter, a ship or aircraft, immigration), see the practice group that handles it and the firm's Lex Mundi reach if the matter crosses borders, then a consultation note to read or copy and "Call +1 242 502 9750". No email is published.
5. **Keep:**
   - **From their site:** all 12 practice groups, the partners, Lex Mundi, the history and founders, the three offices, the published client comments only as they appear on their page.
   - **From our previous version:** the concentric "rings of longevity" mark and ring history, the situation-to-practice finder, the consultation notes, the offices map, Spectral + Hanken Grotesk, ivory and library green with brass.
6. **Fix:**
   - The contact page tells Google not to index it; offices appear only as map links.
   - Images load from a staging server; dead Privacy Policy link; (c) 2025; "Corporae Law" typo; "70 year history" is out of date (1945 makes it over 80).
   - The homepage leads with slogans instead of what the firm does and who to call.
7. **Screens:**
   1. "Counsel in The Bahamas since 1945" with the matter finder.
   2. Practice groups in full, each with plain-language scope.
   3. The partners (headshots only with consent) and how a matter is led.
   4. Lex Mundi: one contact, 100+ countries.
   5. Since 1945: the rings, founders and moves.
   6. Offices with text addresses, hours to confirm, and the consultation note again.
8. **Look and feel:** established, discreet, exacting, humane. Keep the previous type and palette; brass reserved for Call. Photography: their headshots only with written consent and hi-res files; otherwise typographic with the rings drawn live; Commons photos of George Street, Nassau, credited. Wow: pick "a dispute with a party abroad" and the rings resolve into Litigation with the Lex Mundi ring drawing outward to the world.
