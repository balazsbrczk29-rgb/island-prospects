# Thermoset Roofing Bahamas Ltd: job spec
Current site: thermosetroofing242.com (Squarespace brochure; old thermosetbahamas.com still says South Florida) · Previous concept: https://balazsbrczk29-rgb.github.io/island-prospects/thermoset-roofing-bahamas-ltd/ · Kind of site: brochure + free estimate / R.A.D.A.R. assessment request

1. **The one job:** request a free roof estimate, or a free R.A.D.A.R. roof health assessment for a commercial or large property.
2. **The visitor:**
   - Who: facility and property managers, resort engineering teams, church building committees, general contractors, and homeowners with leaks.
   - What they want: reassurance against hurricanes, a warranty, and proof that Thermoset does buildings like theirs.
   - When and how: they browse at a desk or on a phone after a storm.
3. **The product:**
   - **Services:** roofing systems, waterproofing, maintenance and inspection, custom fabrication, general construction.
   - **Credentials:** factory-certified installers of Carlisle, Elevate, Englert, Sika, Siplast and Tremco; serving The Bahamas since 2002; 100% Bahamian owned.
   - **R.A.D.A.R. programme:** Roof Assessment, Diagnostics and Response, with plans for commercial complexes, industrial sites and luxury estates.
   - **Client list:** Baha Mar, Atlantis, LPIA, the U.S. Embassy, Christ Church Cathedral and about 55 more.
4. **The core tool (the hero):** a roof request on screen 1.
   - Choose your property type (home, commercial, industrial, church or institution, resort).
   - Choose the problem: a leak now, a new roof, a re-roof, waterproofing, or a maintenance plan.
   - Add the roof type if known and the island.
   - It recommends the matching service or R.A.D.A.R. plan, shows comparable names from their client list for that property type, and sends the request (their form or email, plus call buttons for the owner and the GM).
5. **Keep:**
   - From their site: the full services list, the client list, the R.A.D.A.R. programme and its benefits, the free estimate, and direct contacts for the owner and the GM.
   - From our previous version: the roof build-up (deck, insulation, membrane) as the explainer, moved below the request.
6. **Fix:**
   - The old site says South Florida.
   - A cart icon has nothing to buy.
   - The client list is buried.
   - WhatsApp is promised without a number.
   - The certifications are one red line.
   - The tax ID sits in the public footer.
7. **Screens:**
   1. Their own roof photo, with the roof request.
   2. The roof over the brands you know: the client list, searchable by type.
   3. Services, with the system build-up.
   4. R.A.D.A.R.: plans and the free assessment.
   5. Certified for these manufacturers.
   6. Contact, with the owner and GM.
8. **Look and feel:** precise, solid and hurricane-ready, in their logo teal. Use their own job photos (membrane install, scaffolding), never the third-party resort photos. The wow is the client list filtering to buildings like yours as you choose. Keep 3D only for the build-up, if it reads as real.
