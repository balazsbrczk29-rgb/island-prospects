# Romora Bay Resort & Marina: job spec
Current site: romorabay.com (modern 2026 React build; rooms on the officialbookings.com engine, slips on /marina/reservations, a Marina Map PDF) · Previous concept: https://balazsbrczk29-rgb.github.io/island-prospects/romora-bay-resort-marina/ (gen 2, three pages: "By sea / By air" arrival switch on the drone photo, a yacht length and power checker on the marina page, a room selector on the stay page) · Kind of site: booking (rooms on their engine, slip reservation request)

1. **The one job:** reserve a slip, or a room, at the one Harbour Island resort where both share a dock. Two jobs of equal weight, chosen on screen 1: "I'm arriving by boat" or "I'm staying with you".
2. **The visitor:**
   - **Yacht owners and captains** (60 to 190 ft, US East Coast, Florida) planning a Harbour Island stop, often clearing in from the US. Phone or laptop on board. They must decide: will she fit, is the power right, can I clear customs here, who answers.
   - **Couples and villa groups** comparing Harbour Island hotels on a phone. They must decide: which of the three room types, which view, when it is open, how to get here.
3. **The product:**
   - **Marina:** "40+ slips" (marina page; title and meta say 40), vessels up to 190 ft, official port of entry with customs and immigration on site, 24/7 yacht assistance, provisioning, concierge and yacht agency, video surveillance and guards, reverse osmosis water up to 17,000 gallons a day, 550kW generator backup, power 110V single phase, 220V 3 phase, 30 and 50 amp (and "408V 3 phase" as published, to confirm), pumpout, showers, laundry, resort amenities for marina guests. Marina Map PDF. Badges: Snag-A-Slip (Marinalife), Association of Bahamas Marinas Platinum Member.
   - **People:** Rodney Diegue (Dockmaster), Beriska Moss (Marina Manager), marina@romorabay.com; reservations 833.717.6499.
   - **Rooms (17):** King with Waterfront View (balcony, water views, walk-in shower); The Suites (vaulted wood ceilings, king plus daybed, garden views); The House (standalone villa, full kitchen, several bedrooms, terraces). Seasonal closure 17 Aug to 12 Nov.
   - **Around it:** Sip Sip waterfront lunch (11:30am to 4pm, closed Tuesdays), Upstairs @ Romora (breakfast, dinner, wine), pickleball club, kayaking, snorkelling, fishing, the pink sand beaches; new reception building and island rooms coming.
4. **The core tool (the hero):** a two-way arrival panel on screen 1, over the real drone photo of the harbour.
   - **By sea** (kept from our previous version, moved up): length overall (slider and number) answered live against 190 ft; power picker from the published list; "clearing in from abroad?" yes shows the port-of-entry line. "Request this slip" hands the boat, dates and power to /marina/reservations (or a prefilled email to marina@romorabay.com), with the Dockmaster named beside the button and the Marina Map PDF one tap away.
   - **By air**: arrival date and nights; dates inside 17 Aug to 12 Nov warn of the seasonal closure; the three room types shown as a strip to pick from; "Check availability" opens their officialbookings engine; the getting-here line under it (North Eleuthera airport and water taxi, to confirm in research).
5. **Keep:**
   - **From their site:** the booking engine, /marina/reservations, the Marina Map PDF, every marina service and spec, both named marina staff and marina@, the badges, all three room types with their photos, Sip Sip and Upstairs hours, pickleball, the seasonal closure, their own photography (drone, marina aerials, dock, rooms, Sip Sip deck, kayaking).
   - **From our previous version:** the By sea / By air switch, the length and power checker, the room selector, the "named people, one email" block.
6. **Fix:**
   - Two marina phone links dial different numbers from the ones printed (786-535-7322 dials 833-717-6499; 242.333.2325 dials 242-333-2500).
   - "40-slip" in the page title and Google snippet, "40+ slips" on the page.
   - No draft, beam or approach information, and "408V" looks like a typo for 480V.
   - A "Royal Marsh Harbour Yacht Club" badge (an Abaco club) sits among the marina credentials without explanation.
   - The homepage leads with a dated pickleball event, not with the marina and the rooms.
7. **Screens:**
   1. The drone photo of the harbour with the By sea / By air panel working.
   2. The marina in numbers: slips, 190 ft, port of entry, power table, water, pumpout, security, the Marina Map, the two named people.
   3. The rooms: the three types side by side with photos, view, beds, and "Check availability".
   4. A day here: Sip Sip lunch, Upstairs dinner, the beach, kayaks, pickleball (no dated event in the hero).
   5. Getting here: by sea (clearing in, VHF and phone) and by air (North Eleuthera and water taxi, to confirm in research).
   6. Contact: reservations, marina team, address, map.
8. **Look and feel:**
   - **Mood:** barefoot, polished, pink-sand Harbour Island, with yacht-club precision in the numbers.
   - **Type:** keep the previous version's Bodoni Moda and Outfit pairing; tabular figures for every spec.
   - **Palette:** paper ground, harbour blue, a soft pink-sand tone used sparingly (not orange or red).
   - **Photography:** their own site assets (drone, marina aerials, rooms, Sip Sip), used full width at good size.
   - **Wow:** the drone photo of the harbour is the switch. Choosing By sea brings the slips forward and the checker answers "she fits". Choosing By air brings the rooms forward on the same photo.
