# Fernandez Bay Village: job spec
Current site: fernandezbayvillage.com (Squarespace, Book Now goes to innRoad) · Previous concept: https://balazsbrczk29-rgb.github.io/island-prospects/fernandez-bay-village/ (gen 2, letterboxed film) · Kind of site: booking (cottages and villas, innRoad handoff, WhatsApp for groups)

1. **The one job:** pick the right cottage or villa for your party and check your dates on innRoad. WhatsApp 1 (242) 808-5711 is the alternative for villas, groups, pilots and weddings.
2. **The visitor:**
   - **Couples 40 to 70** from Florida and the Northeast, on a phone, comparing quiet Out Island resorts (Exuma, Eleuthera).
   - **Families and groups of friends** who need adjoining cottages or a whole villa.
   - **Private pilots** (5,000 ft paved runway five minutes away, 15% off with code PILOT).
   - **What they must decide:** which cottage fits us, how we get to Cat Island, and whether dinner is handled.
3. **The product:**
   - **Eight cottages:** Shane's Shack (water's edge, 180 degree view), Coconut Palm, Royal Palm, Traveler's Palm, Seabreeze, Tamarind Suite, Caretaker's Cottage, Reefs Room. Adjoining pairs: Coconut Palm + Royal Palm, Traveler's Palm + Tamarind.
   - **Two villas:** The Manor House (four bedrooms, full kitchen, open-air showers) and The Beach House (two bedrooms, garden bathrooms, for multiple families).
   - **The promise:** a beach view from every room, a mile of white sand, five minutes from New Bight airport.
   - **Meals at published prices:** hot breakfast $20, continental $10, lunch $8 to $18, dinner $48 per person with the beach bonfire.
   - **Getting here:** Makers Air from Fort Lauderdale Executive (10% off for guests), Western Air from Nassau (three times a week, about thirty minutes), or your own plane (customs 9 to 5, 15% off with PILOT).
   - **Rates:** live in innRoad only (not published), so the site never shows a room price.
4. **The core tool (the hero):** "Who's coming?" on screen 1, over their beach photograph.
   - Choose two of us / family / friends together, plus nights. The cottages or villa that fit light up with their own photos (the adjoining pairs for families, the villas for groups), each with "Check dates" handing off to innRoad.
   - Under the result, the meal line for your party at their published prices (breakfast and dinner, nights x guests), and your route line (from Fort Lauderdale, Nassau or your own plane).
   - Groups, pilots and weddings get a prefilled WhatsApp message with the party and nights.
5. **Keep:**
   - **From their site:** the innRoad link, all ten stays with features, the dining prices, getting here with the Makers Air and pilot discounts, WhatsApp and the reservations email, weddings and events, their professional 2500 px photography.
   - **From our previous version:** the party planner with the adjoining pairs, the meal counter, the three-route getting-here row, and the film of one day in the bay (now after the tool, as the "why", not the first screen).

7. **Screens:**
   1. The beach through the sea grape, "A beach view from every room. Fernandez Bay, Cat Island", and the Who's coming? tool with its matches and Check dates.
   2. The stays compared: eight cottages and two villas with photos, beds, view, what is special, adjoining pairs marked.
   3. A day in the bay (the film, shorter): breakfast, kayaks to the Boiling Hole, the Hermitage, the Tiki Bar, dinner and the bonfire, each frame with one real fact or price.
   4. Getting here: three routes, the pilot discount, five minutes from the airport.
   5. The Clubhouse and meals: Tiki Bar, dining room, boutique, meal prices, the meal counter repeated.
   6. Weddings and groups: the villas, WhatsApp enquiry.
   7. Contact: one front-desk number (to confirm), WhatsApp, email, Check dates.
8. **Look and feel:**
   - **Mood:** barefoot, unhurried, family-run, sunlit.
   - **Type:** one warm serif for display, one humanist sans.
   - **Palette:** pale sea-grape paper with casuarina pine as the action colour.
   - **Photography:** their own professional shoot (2500 px, one grade), shown large and full-bleed where the source allows.
   - **Wow:** you say who is coming and the village answers with the exact cottages for you, side by side on the sand, with dinner and the flight already worked out.
