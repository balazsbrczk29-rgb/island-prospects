# Bahamian Contractors' Association (BCA): job spec
Current site: bahamiancontractors.org (Unicorn Platform, built by Starfish Web Ventures, (c) 2026) · Previous concept: https://balazsbrczk29-rgb.github.io/island-prospects/bahamian-contractors-association/ · Kind of site: association brochure + membership sign-up + events

1. **The one job:** get a Bahamian contractor, supplier or developer to join at the right tier (or renew), and to RSVP to the next event.
2. **The visitor:** contractors from one-truck subcontractors to $5M+ firms, suppliers and developers, mostly on phones; also officials and foreign developers checking who represents the industry. They need: which tier am I, what do I get for the dues, what has the BCA won.
3. **The product (their site, Nov 5):**
   - **Tiers:** Associate $250/yr (suppliers, manufacturers, consultants, PMs, professionals); General $300/yr (general, specialty, subcontractors); Prime $1,000/yr (annual revenue $5M+); Developer/Corporate $5,000/yr (turnover $10M+). Open to Bahamian citizens or residents with the right to work.
   - **Benefits:** bid invitations (government and private), training seminars, certification, Contractor Seminar Series, testing and licensing support, monthly luncheons, industry reports, certificate, Contractor Register listing, trade discounts, free estimate printing, marketing and social promotion.
   - **Advocacy:** official voice of the industry; EPA negotiations; drove the Construction Contractors Act 2016; pushing for the Construction Contractors Board.
   - **Events and media:** "Skyfall" Q4 Mix & Mingle, December 11, Royal Blue Tavern; Nailed It! radio show; newsletters; Contractors Buy & Sell group.
   - **Office:** #27 Chesapeake Road, Suite #11, Nassau; Mon to Wed 9:00 to 3:00, Thu 9:00 to 12:00; (242) 356-8850; admin@bahamiancontractors.org. President Leonard Sands. Established 1959 (own site).
4. **The core tool (the hero):** the membership finder on screen 1: answer what you do and your annual revenue band, see your tier, its dues and benefits side by side with the next tier, then "Apply" writes an email to admin@ (or links their form) with your details.
5. **Keep:**
   - **From their site:** all four tiers and prices, every benefit, the advocacy record, events, newsletter, radio show, socials, office hours.
   - **From our previous version:** the tier finder that writes an email, the advocacy story, the "Find your membership" wording.
6. **Fix:**
   - The hero misspells the name ("Assoication") and the hero block appears twice.
   - Prices are hidden in an FAQ accordion; a contractor has to work out the tier.
   - Newest homepage post is from May 2025; 1959 vs 1958 conflict (use 1959, the site's own).
7. **Screens:**
   1. "The voice of Bahamian contractors since 1959" with the membership finder.
   2. What membership gets you: bids, training, register, discounts, set against each tier.
   3. What the BCA has won: the 2016 Act, EPA talks, the Contractors Board campaign.
   4. Next event (Skyfall, Dec 11) with RSVP, then the radio show and newsletter.
   5. Office, hours, contact, and the finder result again.
8. **Look and feel:** civic, sturdy, proud; a trade body with weight. Construction-drawing precision (grid, dimension lines) with Bahamian flag aquamarine and gold used sparingly, one accent for Apply. Photography: their own event and site photos if readable (Lovable gallery), otherwise Commons photos of Nassau construction credited; never a stock hard-hat model. Wow: choose "General contractor, $1M to $5M" and the tier card snaps into place with the dues and the step up to Prime shown.
