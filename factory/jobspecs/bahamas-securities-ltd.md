# Bahamas Securities (1987) Limited: job spec
Current site: none (directory listings; the registration appears only in the Securities Commission PDF) · Previous concept: https://balazsbrczk29-rgb.github.io/island-prospects/bahamas-securities-ltd/ (register-record card, consultation planner) · Kind of site: professional brochure + consultation request (phone)

1. **The one job:** get a prospective client to arrange a consultation by phone, after verifying that the firm is registered.
2. **The visitor:**
   - Bahamian professionals, business owners and families with savings or pension money.
   - Small companies.
   - These visitors come from a referral or a search, on a phone or desktop. They compare the firm with bank brokerages and other advisers. They must decide whether it is registered and legitimate, what it advises on, and how to start without being sold something.
3. **The product (Securities Commission register, 30 Sep 2026; Yello directory):**
   - Registered under the Securities Industry Act, 2024: "Advising on Capital Market Instruments".
   - Gresham House, Charlotte Street South, P.O. Box N-10144, Nassau; register lists George C. Culmer and Myles J. Culmer.
   - Founded 1987; tel 242-325-6591.
   - No website, no reliable email, no fees published.
4. **The core tool (the hero):** "Verify us, then talk to us" on screen 1.
   - A register record shows the firm's official entry field by field, with a link to the Commission's PDF.
   - Beside it, a consultation planner: goal (grow savings, income, a company's reserves, a second opinion), horizon, an approximate amount band (optional), and contact preference.
   - It composes a summary and hands off to "Call 242-325-6591". "Fees discussed at consultation."
5. **Keep:**
   - From their record: the registration entry, Gresham House on Charlotte Street, 1987, and the phone.
   - From our previous version: the register-record card with live field highlight, the consultation planner, and the quiet, exact typographic tone (no 3D).
6. **Fix:**
   - No website, so a client checking the firm finds only directories.
   - The directory emails look wrong.
   - The strongest trust signal, the registration, is buried in a PDF.
   - Nothing explains "advising on capital market instruments" in plain words.
7. **Screens:**
   1. The register record and the consultation planner.
   2. What we advise on, in plain words: shares, bonds and fund units (a category explanation, to confirm).
   3. How a first consultation works (to confirm), and what we never do (no product push). Only if the firm confirms it.
   4. Since 1987 on Charlotte Street: the people named on the register (with consent), address, map link and call.
8. **Look and feel:** quiet, exact, accountable, private. A light text serif with a plain sans, an ivory ground, one deep green for the call, and hairlines. No stock photography. A photograph of Charlotte Street from Wikimedia Commons only if an excellent one exists. Wow: the register record verifies itself line by line as the visitor reads it.
