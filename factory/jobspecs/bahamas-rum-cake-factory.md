# Bahamas Rum Cake Factory: job spec
Current site: thebahamasrumcakefactory.com (Shopify, about 80 products, real cart and checkout, US and Canada only; non-US buyers sent to an Etsy shop; old listings still point to bacardirumcake.com) · Previous concept: https://balazsbrczk29-rgb.github.io/island-prospects/bahamas-rum-cake-factory/ (gen 2 built Nov 21, two pages: a colour-poster hero that turns the flavour's colour when you pick it, price card with Add to bag, a six-pack builder, the 2000 story, cart drawer and checkout preview handing off to their Shopify cart) · Kind of site: webshop (keep the shop; hand off to their Shopify checkout)

**Note for Balazs first:** this was rebuilt to gen 2 earlier today, before the job spec rule. The spec mostly confirms that build and adds the second job it is missing: the cruise visitor walking to the shop.

1. **The one job:** put rum cakes in the bag and check out on their Shopify store (a single cake, a six pack, a mixed six pack); the second job is the cruise passenger finding the bakery on East Bay Street in the hours they have ashore.
2. **The visitor:**
   - US gift buyers and repeat customers at home, on a phone, often in November and December, deciding which flavours, how many, and whether it will arrive in time.
   - Cruise passengers on a phone at Prince George Wharf, deciding in minutes whether the shop is close, open, and whether the cake can go back on the ship.
3. **The product:**
   - **Fourteen flavours** (their store): Original, Pineapple, Pina Colada, Chocolate, Coconut, Caramel Caribbean, Original with Pecans, Banana, Amaretto, Kentucky Bourbon, Key Lime, Irish Cream, Strawberry (sold out), Pumpkin Spice (20 oz sold out), from the Nov 21 products.json audit.
   - **Sizes and prices:** 4 oz $6.99, 20 oz $21.99; single-flavour 4 oz six packs $37.75; mix-and-match Six Pack $37.75; 20 oz 2-packs $39.58; apparel $15 and $20; hair and skin care and air fresheners $10 to $31.50. Free ground shipping over $55; USPS, UPS and FedEx, Ground, Priority or Express, priced by dimensional weight at checkout.
   - **Facts that sell (their FAQ):** shelf stable, no refrigeration; cruise ship and airport approved, carry-on allowed; allergens egg, wheat, soy, milk, pecans only in Original with Pecans; not sugar-free, gluten-free, organic or kosher; free samples at the bakery only; 48 hours notice for orders over 50 cakes; also sold at the airport (US departures: 700 Wines store and kiosk; international: Bahama Pearl) and in shops downtown; they cannot deliver to the ships.
   - **The shop:** 602 East Bay Street, about 10 minutes' walk from the cruise dock along Bay Street towards the Paradise Island bridge. Mon to Sat 7:30 am to 4:00 pm, Sun 8:00 am to 2:00 pm; holiday hours published (closed Christmas Day, Boxing Day, New Year's Day). (242) 328-3750, bahamasrumcake@gmail.com.
   - **Story:** downtown Nassau, 2000, George Bates from the UK, the Original recipe, the same location since; recognised in 2014 as the Official Rum Cake of The Bahamas; marinated in Ole Nassau Bahamian Rum; master baker Simon Smith.
4. **The core tool (the hero):** the flavour shop on screen 1.
   - The 20 oz photograph of the chosen flavour fills the screen on that flavour's own colour ground; fourteen flavour chips along the bottom; picking one crossfades photo and colour.
   - Beside the cake: 4 oz or 20 oz, quantity, the price, sold-out states as they are, Add to bag; "Make it a six pack" opens the builder with this flavour in the first slot.
   - A free-shipping meter in the bag (two 20 oz cakes: "$11.02 to free ground shipping"), and the cart drawer hands off to their real Shopify cart with the right variants; the mixed six pack carries its flavours in the order note.
   - A second mode on the same screen, "I'm in Nassau today": opening hours with open or closed now (Nassau time), the walk from the cruise dock on a real map, the airport counters, and "cakes go in your carry-on".
5. **Keep:**
   - **From their site:** the Shopify cart and checkout (hand-off), every flavour, size, price and sold-out state, six packs and the mix-and-match six pack, 2-packs, apparel and hair and skin care as secondary lines, free shipping over $55, the Etsy link for non-US buyers, the full FAQ, holiday hours, their own studio photographs (never the AdobeStock-named files).
   - **From our previous version:** the colour-poster hero that changes with the flavour, the price card, the six-pack builder, the 2000 story, the cart drawer and checkout preview, Bricolage Grotesque with Figtree.
6. **Fix:**
   - The address, hours and the 10-minute walk sit only in the FAQ; the cruise visitor, their walk-in buyer, never sees them on the homepage.
   - "Cruise ship and airport approved, no refrigeration" is the answer to the visitor's biggest doubt and is buried in the FAQ.
   - Long repeated product grids on mobile; the six-pack choice is a separate product page.
   - Old directory listings and the listed domain point to bacardirumcake.com.
   - Previous concept: no "in Nassau today" path, no free-shipping meter, FAQ facts not used where they decide the sale.
7. **Screens:**
   1. The flavour poster with the shop tool (and the "In Nassau today" switch).
   2. The six-pack builder: six slots, tap to fill, $37.75, add; the 2-pack beside it.
   3. Travels well: shelf stable, carry-on, ships across the US, gift timing before Christmas, the airport counters.
   4. The story: 2000, George Bates, one downtown shop, Official Rum Cake of The Bahamas, the bakery photos.
   5. Visit the bakery: map of the walk from Prince George Wharf, hours, holiday hours, free samples, phone, email, large orders.
   6. The rest of the shop (apparel, hair and skin care) and the FAQ, then the full catalogue page.
8. **Look and feel:**
   - **Mood:** glazed, celebratory, giftable, a real downtown bakery; impulse-buy joy, not luxury.
   - **Type:** keep Bricolage Grotesque (sentence case, tight) with Figtree; tabular figures for prices and the shipping meter.
   - **Palette:** the flavour grounds from their own studio photographs, with straw, rum brown and palm green between them; no orange or red chrome beyond what the product photo itself carries.
   - **Photography:** their own Shopify studio shots (14 bundts on colour grounds, six-pack packshots), the shop and counter photos and the Carnival storefront photo.
   - **Wow:** picking a flavour turns the whole first screen into that cake's colour; switch to "In Nassau today" and the same screen becomes the walk from your ship to the counter, with whether they are open right now.
