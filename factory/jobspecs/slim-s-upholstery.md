# Slim's Upholstery: job spec
Current site: none (Facebook, Instagram, TikTok; lapsed domain slimupholstery.com) · Previous concept: https://balazsbrczk29-rgb.github.io/island-prospects/slim-s-upholstery/ · Kind of site: brochure + free-quote request

1. **The one job:** send Slim photos of your piece and get a free quote, by WhatsApp or a call to 814-5451.
2. **The visitor:**
   - Who: Nassau homeowners with a worn sofa or dining chairs, boat owners (marine seats), car owners, and churches (pews).
   - How: on a phone, coming from Facebook or Instagram.
   - What they need to decide: does Slim do my kind of piece, how do I get a price, and where and when.
3. **The product:**
   - **What they reupholster:** home (sofas, dining seats, chairs), auto, marine, aircraft, churches.
   - **How it works:** free quotes, appointments available, "From worn to wow, one stitch at a time".
   - **The shop:** #21 Claridge Road; Mon to Sat (Saturday hours differ between listings, so "call to confirm").
   - **Phones:** 814-5451 (WhatsApp), 341-2149 in older listings.
4. **The core tool (the hero):** a quote starter on screen 1.
   - Pick the piece (sofa, dining chairs, car seats, boat cushions, aircraft, church pews), how many, and the fabric wish (same look or new).
   - Add a note; it writes a WhatsApp message to 814-5451 and reminds you to attach 2 to 3 photos.
   - It also offers "Call" and "Book an appointment".
5. **Keep:**
   - From their socials: the 814-5451 WhatsApp CTA, free quote, appointments, the five specialities, their tagline, the address, and the social links.
   - From our previous version: the chair taken apart layer by layer (frame, webbing, foam, cloth) as the explainer of the craft, moved below the quote starter.
6. **Fix:**
   - The domain is lapsed.
   - The listings carry two numbers and two sets of Saturday hours.
   - An old directory page says "may have closed".
   - Their portfolio is trapped in social posts that Google does not show.
7. **Screens:**
   1. The quote starter, beside a real before/after if sourceable (otherwise the chair study).
   2. What we upholster: five specialities, each with what to send.
   3. One stitch at a time: the chair layers.
   4. How it works: photos, then a quote, then an appointment, then pick-up.
   5. Visit Claridge Road: hours and both numbers.
8. **Look and feel:** warm, crafted and tactile. Fabric and stitch detail, never SVG furniture. Their own photos if Facebook images can be sourced; otherwise illustrative Lovable close-ups of upholstery work, labelled. The wow is the chair rebuilding under the quote.
