# Hope Town Inn & Marina: job spec
Current site: hopetownmarina.com (Wix, rooms on Vertical Booking, slips on Dockwa) · Previous concept: https://balazsbrczk29-rgb.github.io/island-prospects/hope-town-inn-marina/ (gen 2, built) · Kind of site: booking (rooms and slips)

1. **The one job:** reserve a room or villa (Vertical Booking) or a slip (Dockwa).
2. **The visitor:**
   - **Guests:** US East Coast couples and families planning an Abaco trip months ahead on a phone.
   - **Boaters:** yacht and sportfish owners and captains crossing from Florida, checking LOA, power and slips.
   - **What both need:** to know whether their party or their boat fits, and how to get to Elbow Cay.
3. **The product:**
   - **Rooms:**

     | Room | Guests | Size |
     |---|---|---|
     | King Suite | 3 | 450 ft² |
     | King Bed Hotel Room | 2 | 350 ft² |
     | Double Occupancy | 4 | 270 ft² |

   - **Villas:**

     | Villa | Guests | Size |
     |---|---|---|
     | Two Bedroom Garden | 4 | 900 ft² |
     | Areca Palm | 8 | 1,210 ft² |
     | Hummingbird | 6 | 1,210 ft² |
     | Castaway | 6 | 1,210 ft² |
     | Flamingo | 6 | 1,210 ft² |
     | Oleander | 8 | 1,010 ft² |

   - **Marina:** 50 surge-free slips for boats to 125 ft, moorings to 45 ft, 30/50/100 amp power, RO water, ice, laundry, showers, wifi and the pool.
   - **Food and drink:** Bridget's Rum Bar and the restaurant.
   - **Johnny's Cay:** a private island rental.
   - **Getting here:** fly to Marsh Harbour, then the ferry to Hope Town.
4. **The core tool (the hero):** "By ferry or by boat" on screen 1.
   - **By ferry:** a party-size stepper (2 to 8) lights the stays that fit, with guests and size and their photo, and "Check rates" hands off to Vertical Booking.
   - **By boat:** an LOA input shows whether the boat fits a slip (to 125 ft) or a mooring (to 45 ft), plus power and services, and "Book a slip" hands off to Dockwa.
5. **Keep:**
   - From their site: both booking engines, all 9 stays with guests and sizes, the marina specs, Bridget's, and the fly-plus-ferry directions.
   - From our previous version: the split "two ways in" idea and the harbour aerial with the lighthouse.

7. **Screens:**
   1. The harbour aerial and the by-ferry / by-boat tool.
   2. The stays compared (rooms and villas with guests, size and photos).
   3. The marina: slips, power and services, with the slip check repeated for boaters.
   4. Bridget's Rum Bar and the restaurant.
   5. Johnny's Cay.
   6. Getting here: flight to Marsh Harbour, the ferry lines, by private boat.
   7. Contact with the working numbers only.
8. **Look and feel:**
   - **Mood:** harbourside, nautical, polished.
   - **Palette:** deep harbour blue and paper.
   - **Photography:** their own photos at or below native size (the room thumbnails are 395x300, so framed small, and full-res asked for at launch).
   - **Wow:** the screen splits by how you arrive, and every answer ends in the right booking engine.
