# The Conch Inn & Marina: job spec
Current site: conchinn.com (Wix; rooms book on RezExpert, slips by hand-written email) · Previous concept: https://balazsbrczk29-rgb.github.io/island-prospects/conch-inn-marina/ · Kind of site: booking (rooms hand-off) + slip request

1. **The one job:** hold a slip (or a mooring ball) for your boat and dates. Booking a room on RezExpert is the second job.
2. **The visitor:** cruising boaters and charter guests heading to Marsh Harbour, Abaco, often on a phone or tablet at anchor or in port. They need to know: will my boat fit (catamarans up to 30 ft beam), what will a night or a month cost, what's included (power, water, WiFi, showers, laundry), and how to ask.
3. **The product:**
   - **The marina:** 73 slips and mooring balls in Marsh Harbour, 700 Bay Street.
   - **Dockage:** $3.75/ft for 1 night, $3.25/ft a day for 2+ nights, $2.25/ft a day monthly (30+ nights). Short stops (up to 3 h) $1.00/ft.
   - **Moorings:** $30 a night or $500 a month (+VAT).
   - **Utilities:** power $0.75/kWh (30 and 50 amp), water $0.10/gal.
   - **Included:** WiFi, showers, 24-hr security, a dinghy dock; fuel and ice on site.
   - **Rooms:** six room types.
   - **On site:** Snappa's Bar & Grill, the pool, Renu Day Spa, and charter bases (Navigare, Dream Yacht).
   - **Getting here:** 3 miles from the airport.
4. **The core tool (the hero):** a slip calculator and request on screen 1.
   - You enter LOA, beam, draft, boat type and name, arrival and departure, and choose slip or mooring.
   - It shows the dockage estimate live (the right tier by nights) and the power and water rates.
   - It flags the 30 ft beam limit, then writes the email their office asks for: send by email, or call the office or VHF 16.
   - A "Book a room" switch hands off to RezExpert.
5. **Keep:**
   - From their site: RezExpert for rooms, all published rates and marina rules (LOA measured bow pulpit to swim platform, cancellation within 48 h, insurance required), the six rooms, Snappa's, both phones and VHF 16, and the office hours (8 to 5:30 daily).
   - From our previous version: the pre-written slip letter that fills itself in (now a calculator plus letter, not a stationery page).
6. **Fix:**
   - The homepage blocks Google (nofollow, nosnippet).
   - Wix stock rope and sailboat photos stand where their own docks should be.
   - Slip requests have to be typed by hand.
   - The two phone numbers sit on different pages.
   - Their own site never mentions the 1972 history, the slip count or the airport distance.
7. **Screens:**
   1. The harbour, with the slip calculator and request.
   2. What your night includes, plus the rules that matter.
   3. Rooms: six types, booked on RezExpert.
   4. Snappa's and the pool.
   5. Marsh Harbour around you: groceries, restaurants, 3 miles to the airport.
   6. Since 1972, rebuilt after Dorian (reopened 2021).
   7. Contact, with hours and VHF.
8. **Look and feel:** a practical, sunny, family-run boaters' place. The arrival by water is the product, so the wow is a top-down harbour plan: you watch your boat's length and beam drawn to scale in a slip as you type. Use real dock photos only if sourced; otherwise Commons photos of Marsh Harbour, credited. Large tabular numbers.
