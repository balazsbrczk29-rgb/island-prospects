# Man-O-War Marina: job spec
Current site: manowarmarinavillage.com (Wix, every title "My Site 1"; the listed manowarmarina.com now shows someone else's article; rates are a PDF, booking is a bare email link) · Previous concept: https://balazsbrczk29-rgb.github.io/island-prospects/man-o-war-marina/ (harbour broadsheet with a live "what's open now" dateline, a will-she-fit checker and a slip-request letter) · Kind of site: brochure + slip request

1. **The one job:** get a cruising captain to request a slip (prefilled email to mowmv@hotmail.com, or a call to (242) 554-9500) once he knows his boat fits and fuel is there.
2. **The visitor:** owners and captains of 35 to 90 ft sportfish, trawlers and catamarans crossing from Florida and the Carolinas, planning the Abaco leg on a phone, often on poor signal. They must decide: does she fit (LOA, beam, draft), is there fuel and power, will someone answer.
3. **The product:**
   - 23 slips, vessels to 120 ft LOA, beam to 20 ft, 7 ft at low tide; assisted docking on VHF 16; 30 and 50 amp power and water at every slip.
   - The only fuel on Man-O-War Cay: gasoline and diesel, dockside Mon-Sat 8-12 and 1-4, roadside pump Mon-Sat 9-12 and 2-4.
   - Office Mon-Sat 8-12, 1-4. Guest showers, restrooms, laundry, grill area, pool. Dock N' Dine restaurant (242) 554-9134, lunch Mon-Sat 11-3. Gift shop.
   - Full-service marina since 1976, rebuilt after Dorian. Dockage rates are a PDF on their site (to show as a link, or typed in if the owner sends it).
4. **The core tool (the hero):** a "Will she fit, and what's open?" check on screen 1: enter LOA, beam and draft (or pick a boat type) and get one plain answer against 120 ft / 20 ft / 7 ft at low tide, next to a live line for Bahamas time saying whether the fuel dock, office and Dock N' Dine are open now and when they next open. The answer carries straight into the slip request (dates, boat name, specs, power 30/50) sent as a prefilled email, with Call and "VHF 16 on arrival" beside it.
5. **Keep:** from their site: slip request by email, phone (242) 554-9500, dockage specs, fuel hours, office hours, the rates PDF, Dock N' Dine, MOW Life island guide (grocery, museum, ferry, beaches), Instagram, their photographs. From our previous version: the live dateline, the will-she-fit steppers, the slip-request letter, the hours timeline.
6. **Fix:**
   - The old domain shows someone else's article.
   - "My Site 1" titles.
   - The specs captains need are buried in a paragraph.
   - Rates only as a PDF; booking only a bare mailto.
   - "Only fuel on the island" is missing from the homepage.
7. **Screens:**
   1. Their dock photograph, the name, and the fit-and-open-now check with the slip request button.
   2. The docks: the captain's numbers set large (slips, LOA, beam, low-tide depth, power, VHF 16), facilities, rates PDF.
   3. Fuel: the only fuel dock on the cay, dockside and roadside hours, live open/closed.
   4. The slip request (prefilled email letter), call as secondary.
   5. Ashore: Dock N' Dine, gift shop, guest facilities, and the cay (MOW Life).
   6. Contact, hours, VHF, map pin.
8. **Look and feel:** steady, crafted, neighbourly, precise: a family harbour since 1976, not a resort. Their own dock and sign photographs used large. One serif with optical sizes plus tabular figures for every number. Sand ground with one Sea-of-Abaco aqua field. The wow: the open-now line ticking in Bahamas time and the fit answer arriving as a sentence a captain trusts.
