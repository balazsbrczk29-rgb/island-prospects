# Bahamas Bridal Affairs: job spec
Current site: none (bahamasbridalaffairs.com no longer resolves; directories and two magazines still link to it) · Previous concept: https://balazsbrczk29-rgb.github.io/island-prospects/bahamas-bridal-affairs/ · Kind of site: brochure + date enquiry

1. **The one job:** a couple sends their wedding date, guest count and venue idea, then calls Dr. Bernadette Burrows.
2. **The visitor:** couples in the US and Canada, about 28 to 45, planning 9 to 18 months ahead on a phone, often with a parent paying. They compare planners with resort in-house coordinators. They need to know: has she done weddings at the resort I love, will she personally look after us, and how do I start.
3. **The product:** luxury destination wedding planning in Nassau and Paradise Island:
   - more than 26 years of experience;
   - a preferred planner with the One&Only Ocean Club and Atlantis for many years;
   - flexible packages;
   - the owner's personal attention;
   - two published weddings (Weddingbells 2012: Ocean Club ceremony, Atlantis reception; Style Me Pretty 2013, Nassau).
4. **The core tool (the hero):** "Check your wedding date." An invitation-style composer on screen 1: your names, date, guests, venue idea (from her published record) and the help you need.
   - It reads back as one sentence to say on the call.
   - Two call buttons: Nassau 242-326-4029 and the US 1-954-892-6969.
5. **Keep:**
   - From the listings: both phone numbers, the address and P.O. Box, the Ocean Club and Atlantis record, the 26 years, links to the two magazine features.
   - From our previous version: the date composer and the published-weddings page.
6. **Fix:**
   - The dead domain, still printed in every directory.
   - The track record only appears in a directory blurb.
   - No way to send a date before calling.
7. **Screens:**
   1. The date composer, with the promise of personal planning.
   2. Where she plans: Ocean Club and Atlantis, as told in her published weddings.
   3. The two published weddings, with links to the magazines.
   4. How planning with her works: first call, venue, vendors, the day. Phrased as categories, each listed as an assumption.
   5. Meet Dr. Bernadette Burrows.
   6. Call from the US or from Nassau.
8. **Look and feel:** luminous, poised, celebratory. Paper, light and fine serif type. No own photos exist, so any illustrative photography is labelled and never shown as her weddings. The wow: the invitation card sets itself in type as the couple fills it in.
