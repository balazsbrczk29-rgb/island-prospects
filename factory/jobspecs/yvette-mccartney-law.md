# Yvette McCartney Law: job spec
Current site: https://www.goguild.com/nassau/c-yvette-mccartney/ (Guild directory template, web-renewal fee, 6 tiny photos) · Previous concept: none · Kind of site: brochure + enquiry

1. **The one job:** a prospective client states their matter and requests a consultation.
2. **The visitor:** a Bahamian or international individual, business or financial institution with a legal problem, often stressed, often on a phone. Must decide: do they handle my kind of matter, are they serious, how do I reach them.
3. **The product:** a full-service Nassau law firm: litigation, corporate/commercial, real estate, mortgage enforcement, family law, personal injury. Acts for high net worth individuals, financial institutions and leading commercial companies, using proprietary software technologies. Chambers at 7 Terrace W., Bldg. 10, Nassau (P.O. Box N-10095). Tel 242 328-6725 and 242 326-4620, fax 242 322-1816.
4. **The core tool (the hero):** a matter finder. Choose what you need (a property purchase or dispute, a mortgage default, a company matter, family, an injury, a court claim); the page shows the practice area, "what to bring" (generic checklist, no legal advice), and a call/enquiry button with the matter pre-filled. No outcomes, fees or time promises.
5. **Keep:** firm name, the six practice areas, client description, both phone numbers, fax, P.O. box, address, and their six photographs (two portraits, the yellow chambers building twice, a gavel and scales, law books) shown at native 200 x 150 only.
6. **Fix:** directory template, no matter-led path, photos unusable at any size, a renewal-fee link instead of a call to action, nothing on how to start.
7. **Screens:**
   1. Hero: firm name, one sentence, the matter finder, call button.
   2. Six practice areas in plain words (same order as the finder).
   3. The chambers: the building photo and address, map link, hours to be confirmed.
   4. How an enquiry works: three steps, no promises.
   5. Close: call, email form, address and contact lines.
8. **Look and feel:** serious, calm, human. Cream ground, near-black ink, one deep accent for the call action. A classical serif with a clean sans. Photos kept small and framed like prints on a wall of the chambers (native size, never enlarged); the large visuals are type and space. Wow moment: the matter finder turns into a one-page brief you could print and bring. Which portrait is which person is unknown: captions neutral until Balazs or the firm confirms names.

Open facts to confirm (do not invent): attorney names/titles beyond "Yvette McCartney", admission years, email address, office hours.
