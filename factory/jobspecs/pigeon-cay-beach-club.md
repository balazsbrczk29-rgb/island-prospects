# Pigeon Cay Beach Club: job spec
Current site: pigeoncaybahamas.com (WordPress one-pager, booking engine direct-book.com/properties/pigeoncaydirect) · Kind of site: booking

1. **The one job:** book a cottage for your dates through their direct-book engine.
2. **The visitor:** US East Coast couples, families and anglers planning an Out Island beach week months ahead, mostly on a phone. Comparing Cat Island cottages. They need to decide which cottage, for how many, at what real total, and whether the place is as beautiful as it looks.
3. **The product:** eight cottages on a private beach on north Cat Island, in clear tiers:
   - **Couples tier (sleeps 1 to 2), $215 to $250 a night:** Garden View $215, Hibiscus $215, Sea Studio $225 (kitchenette), Little House $250, Winston Cottage $250.
   - **Family tier, $315 to $425 a night:** Oleander $315 (sleeps 4), Casuarina $325 (sleeps 4), Big House $425 (sleeps 6).
   - **Taxes:** VAT 10% and resort levy 6%. **Policy:** no deposit, pay on departure.
   - **The 200-acre setting:** honor bar, kayaks, paddle boards, bikes and snorkel gear included, flats fishing nearby.
4. **The core tool (the hero):** a booking strip and cottage chooser on screen 1.
   - Pick dates and guests. The cottages that fit light up as photo cards: their own photos, sleeps, beds, nightly rate, and a total including taxes.
   - Tapping a cottage opens a full-screen gallery of that cottage.
   - "Check availability" hands off to direct-book.com with the dates.
   - The no-deposit, pay-on-departure promise sits next to the button.
5. **Keep:** from their site, the direct-book handoff, all eight cottages with rates, beds and galleries, the published taxes and fees, cancellation terms, weddings up to 40, getting here, and phone and email. From our previous concept, the honest total including taxes.
6. **Fix:**
   - The endless one-page scroll and the rotating generic slider headline.
   - Fees hidden in pop-ups and no-deposit buried at the bottom.
   - 2016 flight info and a broken widget.
   - Repeated reviews.
7. **Screens:**
   1. A big photo of their beach, the name, and the booking strip with the cottage chooser.
   2. Compare the tiers: the cottages side by side, each with photos, sleeps and rate.
   3. The 200 acres: the beach, honor bar, kayaks and paddle boards (their photos), and what's included.
   4. Meals and extras with prices.
   5. Getting here: flights to Arthur's Town or New Bight, and the taxi fare.
   6. Weddings: up to 40 guests, beach, church or Hermitage.
   7. Policies in plain words, contact, then book.
8. **Look and feel:** barefoot, honest, sunlit, generous. Their photography is the star, shown big but never past its native size (many files are 800 px, so use framed galleries rather than full-bleed blow-ups). One calm serif and a clean sans. The wow is the cottage chooser: you set your dates and the right cottages rise up with their real photos and totals.
