# Cheryl's Bahamas Taxi and Tours: job spec
Current site: cherylbahamastaxiandtours.com (static HTML + JotForm transfer booking) · Kind of site: booking (airport transfer + private tours)

1. **The one job:** book an airport pickup, or a private tour of Nassau.
2. **The visitor:** cruise passengers and resort guests on a phone, often deciding today or the week before arrival. Many families. Spanish speakers welcome. They need to know: will someone be waiting for me, what will I see, how long does it take, and what does it cost.
3. **The product:**
   - **Airport transfers:** a rep waits in arrivals holding a card with your name. Navy and yellow uniform, air-conditioned vans and SUVs, car and booster seats free.
   - **Tours:** private tours in English or Spanish with drinks included, built from 22 real stops with their prices (Forts and the 66 Steps, conch demonstration, Love Beach, Ardastra flamingos, Clifton Heritage, swimming with pigs, and more).
   - **Licensing and trust:** Registered Taxi Service #1080, 25+ years.
4. **The core tool (the hero):** two clear paths on screen 1, "Airport pickup" and "Plan a private tour".
   - **Pickup:** a short three-step form (flight, passengers and seats, hotel). It ends with the name card that will be waiting, then sends.
   - **Tour:** a visual route builder over a map of New Providence. Add stops (with their photos where seen), and the day's route, time and entry fees add up. Send as a request.
5. **Keep:** from their site, every transfer field (one way or round trip, van or SUV, flight, sign name, kids' seats, hotel, bags), all 22 stops with sourced prices, English and Spanish, office and out-of-hours phones, email, and #1080.
6. **Fix:**
   - Covid banner still up.
   - 2022 footer.
   - Five phone numbers.
   - A zoomed-out mobile view.
   - 22 tours in one long scroll.
   - An off-brand JotForm with a 200-country list.
7. **Screens:**
   1. The two paths, with their real team photo.
   2. The pickup flow.
   3. The tour builder and map.
   4. Example days: full day with picnic, 3-hour sightseeing.
   5. Why Cheryl's: licensed, 25 years, family-friendly.
   6. Contact by phone, with day and night numbers.
8. **Look and feel:** warm, bright, local, trustworthy. Their navy and yellow brand colours used with taste. The wow is the route drawing across the island map as you add stops. Their own photos only, at native size.
