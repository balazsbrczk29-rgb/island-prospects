# Majestic Tours: job spec
Current site: majestictoursbahamas.com (custom PHP from 2012, enquiry by email and phone, no booking engine) · Previous concept: https://balazsbrczk29-rgb.github.io/island-prospects/majestic-tours/ (gen 2, sideways "Out of Nassau" journey) · Kind of site: brochure + enquiry (excursion catalogue, airport transfers)

1. **The one job:** send an excursion or transfer enquiry with the trip, date, guests and hotel filled in, or call (242) 677-2600.
2. **The visitor:**
   - **Resort guests** at Atlantis, RIU, Warwick, Margaritaville, Melia and Cable Beach, on a phone, deciding today or tomorrow.
   - **Cruise passengers** with one day in port.
   - **Arriving travellers and travel agents** booking airport transfers, including accessible transport.
   - **What they must decide:** which trip fits our day and our kids, what it costs, where it picks us up, and whether this company is trustworthy.
3. **The product:**
   - **38 excursions** on their tours page, in clear families: sails and dinner cruises (Flying Cloud, Seahorse, Barefoot Sailing), Blue Lagoon Island (dolphins, sea lions, beach day), Rose Island and swimming pigs, Exuma day trips (Fantastic Exuma 38 nautical miles in 55 minutes, Aqua Shores, Born Free), Harbour Island Pink Sands, Stuart Cove's dives and snorkels, deep sea fishing, culture and food (Clifton, Ardastra, food tour, local culture, pirates, Baha Bay).
   - **Real prices where published:** Flying Cloud Evening Dinner $137.50 adult, $68.75 child under 11, pickups Cable Beach 5:00, Downtown 5:15, Paradise Island 5:30; Fantastic Exuma Day Trip from $328.90 adult, $209.00 child, hotel transfer included. Other prices only as their tour pages show them.
   - **Transfers:** shared (3 to 4 hotel stops), private, the only accessible private transport on the island (48 hours ahead); meet and greet at Ground Transportation after Customs.
   - **Trust:** founded June 1959 by Bill and Iris Saunders, "Service is our Profession", five named awards (1991 to 2013), ASTA, CHA, IATA, reps in six languages, desks in hotel lobbies.
4. **The core tool (the hero):** the trip finder on screen 1.
   - Filter the 38 trips by what you want (sail, animals, islands, dive and snorkel, fishing, culture), who is coming (kids, non-swimmers), and time (half day, full day, evening). Results show the photo, duration, pickup and price where published, and where the boat goes on a small real map of the Nassau waters (Nassau Harbour, Blue Lagoon, Rose Island, the southwest reefs, Exuma, Harbour Island).
   - "Ask about this trip" opens the enquiry prefilled (trip, date, guests, hotel); "Call (242) 677-2600" beside it. A second tab, "Airport transfer", takes flight, date, party and hotel for a shared, private or accessible quote request.
5. **Keep:**
   - **From their site:** all 38 tours linked to their own tour pages, the published prices and pickups, the transfer options and rules, the lobby desks, the phones and the working email, the awards and founding story, the six languages, group bookings.
   - **From our previous version:** the "Out of Nassau" stop-by-stop journey (now the browse view below the finder, and the source of the map stops), the Bay Street pastel palette, the excursions and transfers pages, and the prefilled enquiry.
6. **Fix:**
   - The footer says 2012 to 2021; the homepage shows no phone or email.
   - Tour pages send email to a misspelled address (info@majestictourbahamas.com).
   - 38 tours in one unfiltered list.
   - The 1959 founding and the awards are hidden on About.
   - Two pages list different hotel desks.
7. **Screens:**
   1. A Nassau harbour sail photograph, "Out of Nassau since 1959", and the trip finder with its map and Ask about this trip.
   2. The journey: stop by stop out of Nassau (harbour, Blue Lagoon, Rose Island, reefs, Exuma, Harbour Island), each stop listing its real trips.
   3. Airport transfers: the arrival in four steps, shared, private, accessible, the quote request.
   4. Since June 1959: Bill and Iris Saunders, the awards, languages, memberships.
   5. Find us in your hotel lobby: the desks as a list (one confirmed list).
   6. Questions and contact: FAQ, phones, the working email, enquiry.
8. **Look and feel:**
   - **Mood:** gracious, assured, sunlit Nassau heritage.
   - **Type:** a monumental serif for display, a clear sans for the finder and prices.
   - **Palette:** Bay Street pastels (shutter green as the action colour, warm yellow and paper).
   - **Photography:** their own tour photos at or below source size (many are under 400 px, so framed small), Wikimedia Commons photos of the real places (credited), and illustrative photography only where nothing real exists, marked to replace.
   - **Wow:** you tell it "kids, animals, half day" in a lobby and the map of the Nassau waters lights up the three boats that fit, each one tap from a prefilled enquiry.
