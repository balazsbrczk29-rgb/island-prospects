# Hideaways at Palm Bay: job spec
Current site: hideawayspalmbay.com (WordPress + WPBakery + Slider Revolution, Check availability goes to Cloudbeds) · Previous concept: https://balazsbrczk29-rgb.github.io/island-prospects/hideaways-at-palm-bay/ (gen 2, "sand or hill" + sideways room track) · Kind of site: booking (room types, Cloudbeds handoff)

1. **The one job:** find the room type that fits your party and check availability on Cloudbeds, with the right promo code in hand.
2. **The visitor:**
   - **Value-conscious US and Canadian couples and families** comparing George Town options on a phone.
   - **Bahamas residents** using the fly or ferry free offer.
   - **Private pilots** (fee-credit codes) and European and Latin American visitors (air credit offers).
   - **What they must decide:** beach cottage or hillside villa, which room fits us, which offer is mine, and how far the airport is.
3. **The product:**
   - **Eight room types in two halves:** Ocean Side cottages and villas on the sand of Jolly Hall Bay (one bedroom ocean view, one bedroom garden view, two bedroom garden view, villa one bedroom partial ocean view, villa two bedroom ocean view) and the Island Time villas on the hill (studio and up). Each with beds, baths, kitchen and patio.
   - **Rates:** not published; Cloudbeds shows them.
   - **Offers open today (their specials page):**

     | Who | Code | What | Minimum |
     |---|---|---|---|
     | Bahamas residents | FLYFREEBAHAMAS | 1 free air or ferry ticket from Nassau (2 nights), 2 tickets (4+ nights) | 2 nights |
     | Private pilots | FEEFREE | $300 fee credit | 4 nights |
     | Private pilots | ISLANDHOPPING | $150 fee credit | 2 nights |
     | Europe | FLYFREEEUROPE | up to $250 air or ferry credit (book Nov 1 2026 to Jan 31 2027) | 4 nights |
     | Europe | FLYFREEEUROPE | 1 free ticket from Nassau (4 to 6 nights), 2 (7 nights) | 4 nights |
     | Europe and Latin America | FLYFREEEUROPE/LATINAMERICA | 1 or 2 free tickets from Nassau | 4 nights |

     Travel windows run to October 31, 2027; blackouts Nov 26 to 28 2026, Dec 19 2026 to Jan 1 2027, Mar 25 to 28 2027, Jul 1 to 4 2027 (not on the European fly-free offers).
   - **The place:** private white-sand beach on Elizabeth Harbour, pool, free water toys, sunset patios, Splash! Bar & Grill (breakfast 8 to 10, lunch 11 to 5, dinner 6 to 9, live music), 15 minutes from Exuma International.
4. **The core tool (the hero):** "Sand or hill?" plus party and dates, on screen 1.
   - The two real photographs (boardwalk cottage, hillside villas) are the choice. Choosing a half and a party size shows the room types that fit, with photo, beds, baths, kitchen and patio.
   - "I am" (Bahamas resident / pilot / Europe / Latin America / US or Canada) shows the codes open on your dates, with the blackout check against your dates, and a copy button.
   - "Check availability" hands off to Cloudbeds with the dates when the engine accepts them.
5. **Keep:**
   - **From their site:** the Cloudbeds engine, all eight room types with specs, the current specials with codes and dates, Splash and its reservation numbers, the 15-minute airport line, phone +1 242-336-2787 and email.
   - **From our previous version:** the "sand or hill" opening with the two photos, the sideways room track for browsing, the offers finder that hides expired offers, the harbour panorama, the distances list (Jolly Hall 0.29 mi to Mystery Cave 2.03 mi).
6. **Fix:**
   - A COVID-19 banner still on every page in 2026.
   - Expired offers listed beside current ones.
   - Room and activity tiles repeated two or three times on the homepage.
   - No side-by-side room comparison.
   - Three different resort names; the title says "GeorgeTown".
   - A slider-heavy page-builder look.
7. **Screens:**
   1. Sand or hill: the two photographs, the party and dates, the matching rooms, your offer code, Check availability.
   2. The rooms compared: the sideways track (sand then hill) with a table view toggle.
   3. The harbour: the panorama, private beach, pool, water toys.
   4. Which offer is yours: the full finder for anyone who skipped it.
   5. Splash! Bar & Grill on an evening field: hours, cocktails, live music, reservation numbers.
   6. Around the harbour and getting here: distances, Stocking Island, 15 minutes from the airport, the airlines.
   7. Contact: address, phone, email, Check availability.
8. **Look and feel:**
   - **Mood:** easygoing, sunny, sociable, blue water.
   - **Type:** a soft monumental serif for display, a readable text serif or sans.
   - **Palette:** pale sand ground, Exuma shallow-flats aqua as a field, a deep harbour green for the evening section.
   - **Photography:** their own photos at or below source size (room photos are 580x440, so framed, never full-bleed); full-resolution files to be asked for at launch.
   - **Wow:** two photographs of the same bay ask one question, and the answer is your room, your code and your booking button on the same screen.
