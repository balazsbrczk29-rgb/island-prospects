# Donny's Boat Rentals & Marina: job spec
Current site: donnysboatrentalsgtc.com (Wix brochure, 2020, mailto "Book Now" only) · Previous concept: https://balazsbrczk29-rgb.github.io/island-prospects/donny-s-boat-rentals-marina/ · Kind of site: booking enquiry (boat rental) + marina (dockage)

1. **The one job:** reserve a rental boat for your days on Green Turtle Cay. The second job is to check whether your own boat fits Donny's dock and request a slip.
2. **The visitor:**
   - Families and couples staying on Green Turtle Cay, deciding in the week before arrival on a phone.
   - US East Coast cruisers crossing to the Abacos.
   - They need: which boat fits our group, what a week costs, the rules (cash only, 3-day minimum), and whether Black Sound is deep enough.
3. **The product:**
   - Four 4-stroke boats with published specs and prices: 23 ft Angler $185/day or $1,200/7 days; 20 ft Century $170/day or $1,075/7 days; 19 ft and 17 ft Whalers.
   - Terms: pay in full, cash, $100 deposit, fuel, hours, Abaco Sound only.
   - Dockage up to 50 ft LOA and 7 ft draft, with published nightly, weekly and monthly rates; moorings.
   - Where to go by boat: No Name Cay pigs, Manjack Cay turtles.
4. **The core tool (the hero):** a rental planner on screen 1.
   - Pick a boat, pick your dates (3-day minimum), see the estimated cost from their published day and week prices, and the boats side by side.
   - It sends a prefilled WhatsApp to Taylor (242 815 9323) or an email.
   - A short map shows Black Sound and the day trips (No Name Cay, Manjack Cay).
   - A second tab, "Does my boat fit?", takes LOA and draft and returns the published dockage rate.
5. **Keep:**
   - From their site: all fleet specs and prices, every term, the dockage and mooring rates, the Black Sound entry note, VHF 16, the phone and email, the activities, their own aerial and boat photos.
   - From our previous version: the cost planner and the marina rates with the slip check.

7. **Screens:**
   1. Their marina aerial with the planner.
   2. The four boats compared.
   3. Where to go: the map of Abaco Sound day trips.
   4. The rules, short and clear.
   5. The marina for cruisers: rates, depth, amenities, slip check.
   6. Contact: WhatsApp, phone, US number, VHF, position.
8. **Look and feel:** sunny, practical, family-run. Their own aerials at native size, clear numbers in tabular type. The wow: the planner's route lines draw from Black Sound to No Name Cay and Manjack Cay on a real chart-like map.
