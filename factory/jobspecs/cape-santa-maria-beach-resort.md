# Cape Santa Maria Beach Resort: job spec
Current site: capesantamaria.com (Tambourine; booking on bookonthenet.net) · Previous concept: https://balazsbrczk29-rgb.github.io/island-prospects/cape-santa-maria-beach-resort/ (travel-magazine opening whose standfirst tells you how you arrive from your gateway) · Kind of site: booking

1. **The one job:** check availability for a beachfront room on their booking engine, once the guest believes getting there is easy.
2. **The visitor:** US and Canadian couples, families and private pilots looking for a remote, quiet beach; planning months ahead on phone and laptop; comparing with Exuma and Eleuthera. They must decide: how do I get there from where I start, and which of the three room types.
3. **The product:**
   - Three beachfront room types: Beachfront Bungalow (one bedroom, 400 sq ft, king, veranda for sunsets), Two-Bedroom Beachfront Bungalow (580 sq ft), Beachfront Villa (two bedrooms, 1,800 sq ft, full kitchen, washer/dryer).
   - Routes: Makers Air nonstop Fort Lauderdale Executive to Stella Maris (Sun/Tue/Thu); Bahamasair and Stella Maris Air Service from Nassau; Island Wings charters via George Town, Exuma; Sheep Runner ferry Mon/Wed/Fri; Seaplane Safaris from Nassau to the resort's beach; private aircraft.
   - Beach House restaurant and bar; scuba, snorkelling, fishing, kayaking, paddleboarding; weddings.
   - +1 800-926-9704, +1 242-338-5273, resort@capesantamaria.com.
4. **The core tool (the hero):** a route finder on screen 1: "Where do you start?" (Fort Lauderdale, Nassau, George Town, your own plane, Nassau by seaplane). The answer draws the route on a real map of the Bahamas to the north tip of Long Island and states it in one sentence (carrier, days, last leg to the resort), then the room picker and "Check availability" hand off to bookonthenet.
5. **Keep:** from their site: the booking engine, the three room types with their photos, every route and carrier they list, the Beach House, activities, weddings, phones and email, their aerial of the bungalows on the beach. From our previous version: the live "how you arrive" sentence and the magazine voice.
6. **Fix:**
   - The Getting Here page carries noindex, nofollow.
   - Placeholder "Subheadline" on the homepage.
   - The FareHarbor flight widget fails to load.
   - Conflicting taxi times and airport codes on one page.
   - Machine-generated image descriptions.
7. **Screens:**
   1. Their beach aerial, the route finder with the drawn route, and Check availability.
   2. The three rooms compared, each with Check availability.
   3. The beach and the Beach House.
   4. Days here: the water activities, with their photos.
   5. Getting here in detail (every carrier, days, contacts).
   6. Contact.
8. **Look and feel:** remote, sunlit, barefoot, a little aviation. A travel-magazine Didone with a clean sans, sand ground and flats aqua. Their own aerial and beach photos large. The wow: a route drawing across the islands from your own gateway down to the white sand at Cape Santa Maria.
