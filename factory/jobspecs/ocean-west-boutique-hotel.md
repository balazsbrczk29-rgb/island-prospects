# Ocean West Boutique Hotel: job spec
Current site: oceanwestbahamas.com (static HTML image tables; books on innRoad at oceanwestbahamas.client.innroad.com) · Previous concept: https://balazsbrczk29-rgb.github.io/island-prospects/ocean-west-boutique-hotel/ (the room register: ten gem-named rooms in an index column, the room appears beside it, Check dates to innRoad) · Kind of site: booking

1. **The one job:** choose one of the ten rooms and check dates on their own innRoad engine (instead of an OTA).
2. **The visitor:** US couples and business travellers, many flying into Nassau and wanting to be near the airport; families (children under 12 free). Planning on a phone, comparing with Cable Beach resorts and Airbnb. They must decide: which room (bed, floor, view, balcony), what it costs, how close the airport is.
3. **The product:**
   - Ten rooms named for stones: Diamond (king, balcony, ocean, 3rd floor), Sapphire (king, lake and partial ocean), Ruby (queen, lake and partial ocean), Topaz (2 singles, balcony, ocean and pool), Emerald (queen, balcony, ocean and pool), Tanzanite (king, garden), Onyx, Ivory, Pearl, Coral (details from their accommodations page).
   - $215 to $600 a night, single or double; children under 12 free; check-in 3pm, check-out 11am, late check-out $30 to 2pm; no pets; non-smoking; daily maid service; 24-hour desk.
   - On a ridge 70 ft above sea level between Lake Killarney and the Atlantic, on West Bay Street, 5 minutes from Lynden Pindling International.
   - Pool, rooftop gym with ocean view, massage suite, indoor and outdoor bars, breakfast a la carte, Sapodilla restaurant, free Wi-Fi and parking.
4. **The core tool (the hero):** a room finder on screen 1: the ten gem names as a register with quick filters (balcony, king bed, ocean view); tap one and its own photo, bed, floor and view fill the stage, with "Check dates for Emerald" opening innRoad (and "Ask about this room" as a prefilled email). Price range and the airport line sit beside it.
5. **Keep:** from their site: innRoad booking, the ten rooms with their photos, rates range and policies, amenities, directions from the airport, phones (242) 698-6744 / 6745 and info@oceanwestbahamas.com, the Tripadvisor Travelers' Choice 2023 and Clean and Pristine marks they display. From our previous version: the gem register as the front door, the room comparison list, the conch-pink colonial palette.
6. **Fix:**
   - The same keyword-stuffed title on every page.
   - Headings are images inside tables, unreadable to Google and phones.
   - No address or phone on the homepage.
   - Ten rooms shown as tiny thumbnails with no way to compare.
   - The ridge between lake and ocean is one sentence.
7. **Screens:**
   1. The room finder with the selected room's photo and Check dates.
   2. The ridge: lake to the south, the Atlantic to the north, five minutes from the airport.
   3. All ten rooms compared (bed, floor, view, balcony), each with Check dates.
   4. Days here: pool, rooftop gym, bars, breakfast, Sapodilla.
   5. Rates and house rules.
   6. Getting here and contact.
8. **Look and feel:** elevated, intimate, jewel-like, breezy. A Didone for the gem names like a jeweller's engraving, a clean sans for facts. Conch pink and shell white from the house itself, one deep-pink accent for Check dates. Their own photos never above source size (room photos are 800x600). The wow: touching a stone's name and the room appearing, like opening a jewel case.
