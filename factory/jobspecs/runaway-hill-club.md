# Runaway Hill Inn (listed as Runaway Hill Club): job spec
Current site: runawayhill.com (WordPress; Book Your Stay goes to direct-book.com, rates in a pop-up table, a property map PDF) · Previous concept: https://balazsbrczk29-rgb.github.io/island-prospects/runaway-hill-club/ (gen 2, three pages: descent from the hill to the beach, a named-room register with an after-tax estimator, a dated room enquiry, island and getting-here page) · Kind of site: booking (direct-book engine) + room enquiry by email

1. **The one job:** choose your room by name and book it for your dates (direct-book engine), or send a dated enquiry to Reservations@runawayhill.com.
2. **The visitor:** US East Coast couples and small family groups planning Harbour Island 2 to 6 months ahead, on a phone, comparing bigger resorts and OTAs. They must decide which of twelve rooms (where it sits, bed, view, rate), what the stay will really cost with tax and gratuity, and how to get there.
3. **The product:**
   - **The house:** a converted 1946 English manor high on the hill above Pink Sands Beach ("11 acres" on the rooms page, "10 Acres" in the homepage meta), pool and sun deck stepping down from the Main House, a staircase to the beach (175 ft of beachfront per Elite Traveler), restaurant and bar with ocean views, events team, full concierge.
   - **Twelve rooms with names and published nightly rates (with 30% tax and gratuity):** Main House 1 Jacqueline Jean King 595 (773.50), 2 Alice Emily King + single 690 (897), 3 Sophia Jean King 640 (832), 4 Rachel Alice Queen 580 (754), 5 Kathleen Jane Queen 580 (754); Courtyard 6 Matteo Douglas Queen 495 (643.50), 7 Mark Umberto King 500 (published as 550.00), 8 Luke Francesco King 555 (721.50); Villas 9 Douglas Paul King 690 (897), 10 Tyler Frank King 690 (897), 11 Lyon Holland (king, two twin trundles, two baths, kitchen, deck with bay views) 1,425 (1,852.50); Infinity House, five-bedroom oceanfront home, weekly 21,750 (28,275), daily 3,107.14 (4,039.28).
   - **Terms:** 10% room gratuity, 10% VAT, 10% resort levy; 3-night minimum; $150 for a cot or extra person over age 10.
   - **Proof:** three guest quotes published on their own site (Caroline C., Kathy S., Ty), attributed, with their TripAdvisor link.
   - **Contact:** Reservations@runawayhill.com, local (242) 333-2150, US and Canada (843) 278-1724, Love Lane and Colebrooke Streets, Harbour Island.
4. **The core tool (the hero):** a room chooser on screen 1, on the photo of the house on the hill.
   - Dates (arrival, nights, guests) at the top; the 3-night minimum enforced in the picker.
   - The twelve rooms as one register grouped by building (Main House, Courtyard, Villas, Infinity House), filterable by bed, sleeps and view, each with its photo, where it sits and the nightly rate.
   - Picking a room shows the stay total: nights x rate, plus 30% tax and gratuity, plus cots or extra persons, in tabular figures (Room 7 computed at 30%, with a quiet "to confirm" note).
   - "Book this room" opens the direct-book engine; "Ask about this room" opens a prefilled email (room, dates, guests, estimate). Call numbers beside them.
5. **Keep:**
   - **From their site:** the direct-book engine, the property map PDF, every room name, photo and rate, the tax and minimum-stay terms, the guest quotes as published and attributed, special events, concierge, all contact details, their own photography (hero, restaurant, pool, patio, beach, yoga, wedding, room thumbnails). Not their stock "Barbuda" image.
   - **From our previous version:** the descent from hill to beach, the named-room register, the after-tax estimator, the dated enquiry, the plane, taxi, boat, hill getting-here sequence.
6. **Fix:**
   - The homepage shows no phone, address or email.
   - Rates live in a pop-up table, and Room 7 shows $500 with "$550.00" after tax, which does not match the 30% on every other room.
   - The Activities page uses a stock photo captioned "Barbuda, Antigua & Barbuda".
   - The name is split (Runaway Hill Inn on the site, Runaway Hill Club on listings and TripAdvisor) and the acreage reads 10 or 11.
   - Twelve room pages with no comparison, and nothing on how to get there.
7. **Screens:**
   1. The house on the hill above the beach, with the dates bar and the room chooser.
   2. The descent: house, pool, staircase, beach, told in their photographs.
   3. The full register: all twelve rooms with where they sit, bed, view and rate; the Infinity House as its own spread.
   4. Dinner and the bar on the hill; weddings and events.
   5. Getting here: North Eleuthera, taxi, water taxi, the hill.
   6. Contact, with the guest quotes and the map.
8. **Look and feel:**
   - **Mood:** personal, salt-washed, old-world, pink, unhurried: a house where every room has a name.
   - **Type:** keep the previous version's pairing; a literary serif for the room names, tabular figures for every rate.
   - **Palette:** limewash white, deep sea green, a faded pink-sand tone (never orange or red).
   - **Photography:** their own images only, at the size they hold up; the room thumbnails framed, not stretched.
   - **Wow:** dragging through the register, the house photo shows where your room sits (Main House, Courtyard, Villas or down by the beach), and the total for your nights updates beside it, itemised and exact.
