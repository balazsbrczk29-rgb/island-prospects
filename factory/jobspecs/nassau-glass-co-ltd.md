# Nassau Glass Co Ltd: job spec
Current site: nassauglass.com (WordPress brochure, contact form) · Previous concept: https://balazsbrczk29-rgb.github.io/island-prospects/nassau-glass-co-ltd/ · Kind of site: brochure + quote request (phone-first)

1. **The one job:** get a glass, mirror or shower-enclosure quote started: you know what you need and its size, then you call or visit Mackey Street.
2. **The visitor:**
   - Who: Nassau homeowners renovating a bathroom or kitchen, landlords replacing broken glass, interior designers and contractors, plus walk-in customers for bulbs, Hunter fans and framing.
   - How: phone first, then a visit.
   - What they need to decide: can they do my size and type, do they install it, and when are they open.
3. **The product:**
   - **Glass:** 1/16 to 3/4 in (clear, gray, bronze).
   - **Mirror:** 1/8 and 1/4 in (clear, bronze, gray, flesh tone, blue).
   - **Laminated safety glass:** for impact windows and boat windshields.
   - **Also cut:** pattern glass, acrylic 1/8 to 1/2 in, polycarbonate.
   - **Departments:** shower enclosures (made in-house), custom framing, Hunter ceiling fans (authorised dealer), the Lightbulb Center, lamps and shades, architectural metals, art and home decor.
   - **Service department:** measures and fits.
   - **The shop:** at #46 Mackey Street since 1959, the Bahamas' first glass fabricator.
   - **Sister company:** Bahamas Aluminum.
4. **The core tool (the hero):** a quote builder on screen 1.
   - Choose what it is (window glass, mirror, table top, shower enclosure, laminated or impact), then thickness and tint from their real stock list.
   - Enter width and height in inches, plus quantity, and whether you need fitting.
   - It shows the square footage and a ready-to-read summary, with "Call (242) 603-5521" and "Copy summary".
   - It also says whether the shop is open now (Mon to Fri 9 to 4:30, Sat 9 to 1).
5. **Keep:**
   - From their site: all departments, both phones, the international line and fax, hours, Mackey Street directions, the enquiry form, and the Bahamas Aluminum link.
   - From our previous version: the stock picker and quote summary, and the laminated glass explained (keep the 3D only if it serves the picker).
6. **Fix:**
   - "SASTIFIED CLIENTS" is misspelled.
   - Stock photos stand in for their own work.
   - The stock list is buried in one paragraph.
   - There is no search description.
   - The carousel repeats the departments.
   - The for-sale notice sits beside the phone numbers.
7. **Screens:**
   1. The quote builder, with the sheet you are configuring.
   2. The stock list as a clear table.
   3. Shower enclosures, made here.
   4. The walk-in departments: bulbs, Hunter fans, framing, lamps.
   5. Since 1959 at the top of Mackey Street, with the Bahamas Aluminum link.
   6. Visit and call: map link, hours, open now.
8. **Look and feel:** clear, precise, luminous and established, with no stock photos. The wow is the sheet itself: it changes thickness and tint as you choose, and its edge is visible. Real-time 3D is used only for that object. Photos come from their Facebook if sourceable; otherwise none.
