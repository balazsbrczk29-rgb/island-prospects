# Swain's Cay Lodge: job spec
Current site: swainscaylodge.com (WordPress, rebuilt Sept 2026 by Hotel Traffic Builders; a Check-In / Check-Out "Book Now" engine, a fishing page with 2026-2027 package terms and a price PDF, a live Google and TripAdvisor review feed) · Previous concept: https://balazsbrczk29-rgb.github.io/island-prospects/swain-s-cay-lodge/ (gen 2, three pages: a "where you'll fish" chart of the bights, a trip planner that works out boats and gratuities and composes the enquiry, rooms, Reefside and getting here) · Kind of site: booking (their engine for rooms) + fishing-week enquiry

1. **The one job:** plan and request your fishing week (anglers, non-anglers, dates, which waters) to fish@swainscaylodge.com. Booking a room on their engine is the second job.
2. **The visitor:** serious fly anglers from the US, 40 to 70, often repeat groups of two to six friends, father and son trips, couples with a non-angling partner. Laptop at home while planning, phone later. They compare other Andros lodges and must decide: where will we fish, who guides, what is included, what will the week really cost, how do we get there.
3. **The product:**
   - **The water:** Mangrove Cay, placed for the Middle Bight and the South Bight of Andros; a wadeable bonefish flat right off the beach; the Andros Barrier Reef (140 miles long, under a mile from shore in most places) for yellowtail, grouper, triggerfish, barracuda; outside the reef the depth drops to 6,000 ft for wahoo, dolphin fish, tuna and billfish; spearfishing tours and lessons; handpicked local independent guides.
   - **2026-2027 season (March 2026 to April 2027):** day trip $975 per boat (one or two anglers, lunch and transport, lodging not included); half day a la carte; non-angler rate $450. Package includes guide and boat (up to two per boat), lodging, all meals, water, airport and dock transfers (Middle or South Bight), Wi-Fi, levies. Not included: airfare to and from Nassau, alcohol and sodas, gratuities, gear and flies, 10% VAT outside packages, fishing licences. 50% deposit within 21 days, balance 90 days before arrival. Recommended gratuities $125 per day per boat per guide, $35 per night per guest for lodge staff. 12 units, up to 26 guests. Orvis Endorsed.
   - **The lodge:** Duplex Apartment (three double bedrooms, kitchenette, terrace), Ocean View Room, Ocean View Suite, Beach Front Suite; Reefside Restaurant & Bar on the beach (breakfast and dinner, Bahamian home cooking); free bicycles, kayaks and snorkel gear.
   - **Offers (dated, as published):** Bahamas residents fly or cruise free from Nassau; private pilots $300 fee credit for 4+ nights, $150 for 2 nights; air and ferry credits for US, Canada, Europe and Latin America residents; blackout dates listed.
   - **Proof:** the live review feed on their own site (Google and TripAdvisor, named reviewers, dated).
   - **Contact:** +1 (242) 422-5018, +1 (242) 369-0296, fish@swainscaylodge.com, Mangrove Cay Settlement.
4. **The core tool (the hero):** "Plan your week" on screen 1, beside the home flat at dawn.
   - Anglers, non-anglers, nights and guided days; boats needed worked out at two anglers per boat.
   - A real interactive map of Mangrove Cay and the two bights (MapLibre on satellite or open tiles; our previous drawn chart as fallback), with the home flat, the reef line and the drop-off; tap a water to add it to your mix and see method and species.
   - The cost side, from the published figures only: guided days x boats, non-anglers, recommended gratuities, and the "not included" list, with package prices shown as "from the lodge's season sheet" when per-person rates are not published on the page.
   - "Send my week to the lodge" composes the email to fish@; "Book a room" opens their engine; the phone numbers beside them.
5. **Keep:**
   - **From their site:** the booking engine, every season term and figure, the Orvis endorsement, all four room types with photos, Reefside, free bikes, kayaks and snorkel gear, the offers with their dates and blackouts (each shown once), the review feed (real and attributed), their photography (header set, welcome, rooms, dining, fishing gallery; credits Lauren Brown Photography and Ian Davis at Yellow Dog Fly Fishing).
   - **From our previous version:** the trip planner and its gratuity maths, the waters chart (now a real map), the rooms, Reefside and getting-here page.
6. **Fix:**
   - The About page images point to "localhost/swainscaytest", a test server, so they are broken for every visitor.
   - "Download Fishing Guide" links to the 2024-2025 price PDF while the page shows 2026-2027 terms.
   - The homepage headline is "Escape to Paradise: Your Ultimate Resort Getaway", with no bonefishing, Andros or Mangrove Cay.
   - Six offer blocks with near-identical date panels push rooms, fishing and contact down the page; one set of terms names another hotel (The Sandpiper Inn & Cottages).
   - The Orvis endorsement and the home flat, the two strongest angler signals, are a header logo and one sentence; package maths is left to a PDF.
7. **Screens:**
   1. The home flat at dawn, the Orvis mark, and "Plan your week" with the map working.
   2. Where you'll fish: the bights, the flat, the reef, the drop-off, with species and method per water.
   3. The season: what is included, what is not, deposit and balance, gratuities, licences, in one plain table.
   4. The lodge: four room types, Reefside, the beach, and "Book a room".
   5. For the non-angler: kayaks, snorkelling, bikes, the beach.
   6. What guests say: the review feed, as published.
   7. Getting here and offers: via Nassau to Mangrove Cay, the offers each listed once with dates.
   8. Contact.
8. **Look and feel:**
   - **Mood:** dawn, tidal, expert, family-warm, unhurried.
   - **Type:** keep the previous version's Alegreya and Alegreya Sans; tabular figures in the planner and the season table.
   - **Palette:** pale flat-sand ground, mangrove ink, tannin brown accent, deep bight blue for one dark section.
   - **Photography:** their own images, the fishing gallery leading; credits shown.
   - **Wow:** an angler taps the Middle Bight, then the home flat, adds a non-angling partner, and the week (boats, guides, gratuities, what to bring) assembles beside the map, ready to send.
