# BVG Design Services: job spec
Current site: bvgdesign.com (hand-coded HTML, footer "Last Updated on 30 May, 2014", GIF buttons, a dead advertising agency link) · Previous concept: https://balazsbrczk29-rgb.github.io/island-prospects/bvg-design-services/ (gen 2, one page: "Bahamian websites, built and found since 1996", a 1996 to 2026 then-and-now frame, the founding story, offices, how a project goes, a tabbed brief builder that opens a prefilled email) · Kind of site: studio brochure + project enquiry

**Note for Balazs first:** BVG is a web design studio, so it is also a direct competitor. Decide whether to pitch them at all (a partnership or white-label angle may fit better than a rebuild). Approve this spec only if you want the preview.

1. **The one job:** send a website request (what you need, pages, hosting, search) to info@bvgdesign.com, or call the Freeport office.
2. **The visitor:** owners of Bahamian small businesses (a Lucaya guesthouse, an Abaco charter, a Nassau retailer) and overseas owners promoting Bahamian products, found through search or a referral, checking on a phone. They must decide: is this firm current, what will my site cost and how fast, and will a local person answer.
3. **The product:**
   - **Services:** simple web pages and themed multi-page sites; complex sites (JavaScript, dynamic HTML, animation); discounts over 10 pages and larger for hundreds; redesign or takeover of existing sites (hourly for minor work, or full takeover); search optimisation and promotion, with a free initial consultation on key phrases; hosting with user-friendly control panels; domain registration; an advertising agency department (print layout, video advertising).
   - **Speed and payment:** simple pages normally within 24 hours of order; cash, USD or B$ cheques, bank deposits, credit cards via PayPal.
   - **Story:** founded mid-1996 by two entrepreneurs (one graphics, one code); their first site, the Bahamas Vacation Guide, reached the top of the search engines and businesses asked to be included; BVG stands for Bahamas Vacation Guide.
   - **Offices:** Bahamian owned, Freeport headquarters, offices in Nassau and Abaco, US affiliates; P.O. Box F-43652 Freeport, (242) 602-0658, US line (954) 640-8552, info@bvgdesign.com, US mailing address in Fort Lauderdale.
4. **The core tool (the hero):** the brief builder on screen 1, beside the "since 1996" statement.
   - Pick what you need (new site, redesign or takeover, search, hosting and domain, advertising), how many pages (the published discount tiers light up over 10 and in the hundreds), and your business type and island.
   - The answer panel says what BVG does for that request in their own published terms (simple pages usually within 24 hours, free search consultation, payment options), with no invented prices.
   - "Send my request" opens a prefilled email to info@bvgdesign.com; "Call Freeport" and the US line beside it.
5. **Keep:**
   - **From their site:** every service line, the 24-hour simple-page line, the free search consultation, payment options, the three offices and US line, the founding story (told plainly, not as jokes), the Bahamas Vacation Guide link, the logo.
   - **From our previous version:** the 1996 to 2026 then-and-now frame, the brief builder and prefilled email, "how a site with BVG goes", the single page.
6. **Fix:**
   - A web design firm whose own footer says "Last Updated on 30 May, 2014".
   - The advertising agency link is dead, and the page still offers "DVD or CD production" and "Myspace" videos.
   - Text set entirely in bold with GIF buttons and no phone layout.
   - Two phone numbers and two emails in public (602-0658 and info@ on the site, 373-2268 on a directory, mail@bahamasvg.com behind a button).
   - Thirty years and the Bahamas Vacation Guide are buried in a self-mocking paragraph.
7. **Screens:**
   1. "Built and found since 1996" with the brief builder working.
   2. Then and now: the 1996 guide beside a 2026 phone, proof they keep up.
   3. What we do: the services as plain lines with what each includes.
   4. How a project goes: request, consultation, design, live, found; with the 24-hour simple-page line.
   5. The story and the three offices.
   6. Contact, one phone and one email first.
8. **Look and feel:**
   - **Mood:** Bahamian, experienced, plainspoken, practical, sunny.
   - **Type:** keep the previous version's pairing; one confident serif headline, a clean text face.
   - **Palette:** light sand ground, Freeport harbour blue ink, one warm accent that is not orange or red.
   - **Photography:** there is no project photography, so the site's own live UI (the builder, the then-and-now frame) is the visual; one Commons photograph of Freeport or Lucaya, credited, if a place image is needed.
   - **Wow:** the then-and-now frame: the 1996 Bahamas Vacation Guide page rebuilds itself into a 2026 phone layout as you scroll, then hands you the brief builder.
