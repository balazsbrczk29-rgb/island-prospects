# Harding-Lee & Company: job spec
Current site: hardinglee.com (three-page WordPress, last updated July 2022, contact form) · Previous concept: https://balazsbrczk29-rgb.github.io/island-prospects/harding-lee-company/ · Kind of site: brochure + enquiry (professional services)

1. **The one job:** a client describes their matter and contacts the right attorney, Nassau or Long Island.
2. **The visitor:**
   - Bahamian families and business owners.
   - Long Island residents.
   - Overseas owners of Family Island property.
   - Institutions needing incorporation, banking or insurance counsel.
   - Often on desktop at work, or a phone. They need to know: does this firm handle my kind of matter, will a senior lawyer handle it, and which office do I call.
3. **The product:**
   - A boutique general practice since 1996, in all courts of the Commonwealth: commercial, conveyancing, company incorporation, international and domestic transactions, insurance, banking, estate planning, resort development, employment, family law.
   - Two attorneys:
     - Donna M. Harding-Lee, Senior Partner, called 1982, formerly of Cash, Fountain & Company, Bank of The Bahamas director;
     - Nadia Hope Johnson, called 2005, conveyancing, employment, family law.
   - Two offices: Mecam House in Nassau, and Salt Pond on Long Island.
4. **The core tool (the hero):** a matter selector on screen 1 ("What are you dealing with?": buying property, a company, a will or estate, employment, family, banking or insurance).
   - It routes to the attorney whose published areas match, and the right office.
   - It composes an email to that attorney, or offers a call.
5. **Keep:**
   - From their site: both offices with phones and fax, both attorney bios and emails, the enquiry form, the practice areas.
   - From our previous version: the matter-to-attorney routing and the office switcher.
6. **Fix:**
   - A "Welcome To" hero with one long paragraph.
   - "Front page" titles and ?page_id URLs.
   - The associate's surname differs between pages.
   - The senior partner's record is hidden in bold text.
   - No addresses on the homepage.
   - The Long Island office is under-presented.
7. **Screens:**
   1. The matter selector beside the firm's name and its two islands.
   2. Practice areas in plain language.
   3. The two attorneys.
   4. Two offices: Nassau and Long Island, with map, phones and hours if confirmed.
   5. How a first consultation works, phrased as categories and listed as assumptions.
   6. Contact.
8. **Look and feel:** discreet, senior, exact. Paper tones, serif display, generous space. Real portraits only. The wow: choosing a matter draws the line from the question to the attorney and to the office on a two-island map.
