# Jones Construction Company Ltd.: job spec
Current site: none (directory listings only: Yabsta "construction consultants", Airport Industrial Park) · Previous concept: https://balazsbrczk29-rgb.github.io/island-prospects/jones-construction-company-ltd/ (3D block building assembling, project brief builder) · Kind of site: brochure + project enquiry (phone-led)

1. **The one job:** get a developer or property owner to call with a real project in mind (new home, commercial building, renovation, consulting), already described well enough to quote or meet.
2. **The visitor:**
   - Property owners and developers in New Providence, some abroad, comparing three local contractors by word of mouth.
   - Project managers and procurement officers checking capability before inviting a tender.
   - They research on desktop and call from a phone. They must decide: is this a real, established contractor, do they do my kind of building, and how do I start.
3. **The product (brief, Nov 5):**
   - A Nassau building contractor and construction consultant in concrete block (the Bahamian standard), yard at Airport Industrial Park, P.O. Box N-3409.
   - Phone 242-327-7486. No email, no projects, no photos and no hours found.
   - The Managing Director was quoted as Mervin Jones in 2004 press. Old source: confirm before use.
4. **The core tool (the hero):** a project brief builder on screen 1.
   - Inputs: what you are building (new home, commercial building, addition or renovation, consulting only), the lot status (bought, under offer, looking), drawings (none, sketches, full set by an architect), and the rough floor area as a slider that shows a simple block footprint.
   - Hands off to "Call 242-327-7486" with the brief composed on screen to read out or copy.
5. **Keep:**
   - From their record: the phone, Airport Industrial Park, and "construction consultants".
   - From our previous version: the course-by-course block building in 3D, the brief builder with tabs, the four-stage process (A to D), and the plain-spoken, engineered tone.
6. **Fix:**
   - No website at all.
   - The name collides in search with J.A. Jones (US) and other "Jones Construction" firms.
   - No scope, no process and no way to start except a bare phone number.
7. **Screens:**
   1. The block building assembling beside the project brief builder, with the phone always visible.
   2. What we build: homes, commercial buildings, additions and renovation, consulting (category lines, to confirm).
   3. How a project runs: brief, estimate, build, handover (to confirm), as a scroll stage on the 3D building.
   4. Where we are: Airport Industrial Park on a map link, the phone, a "Discuss a project" close.
8. **Look and feel:** precise, solid, local. A grotesk display with drawing-sheet labels, paper and concrete tones with one drawing-ink accent, procedural 3D (no project photos exist). Wow: the building rises course by course while you describe yours, and the footprint you set appears on the slab.

Research this run: none new (the Nov 5 brief was reused). Be aware of a critical 2014 Tribune article about a 2009 NIB contract: it is not used on the site, but read it before the pitch.
Assumptions to confirm: still trading at Airport Industrial Park; service lines and process stages; whether the MD's name may be shown; email or WhatsApp for the brief.
