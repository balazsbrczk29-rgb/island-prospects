# Digitech Bahamas: job spec
Current site: digitechbahamas.com (WordPress/Elementor template, WooCommerce installed but no shop) · Previous concept: https://balazsbrczk29-rgb.github.io/island-prospects/digitech-bahamas/ (gen 2, built) · Kind of site: brochure + quote enquiry

1. **The one job:** request a quote for a Razor POS install or IT and network work.
2. **The visitor:** owners and managers of Nassau restaurants, bars, retail shops and small offices (5 to 50 staff), on a phone or desktop. They compare Digitech with bigger Nassau IT firms and with doing it themselves. They need to see:
   - what they would get;
   - that it is installed and supported locally;
   - how to start.
3. **The product:**
   - **Point of sale:** Razor POS dealer for retail and food and beverage (hardware, software, training, post-installation support).
   - **Managed IT:** project management, system design, virtual server hosting.
   - **Networks:** cabling and Wi-Fi.
   - **Backup:** cloud backup.
   - **Clients** named by their own logos: Hana, Naughty Johnny's, Blu Sail, Beauty Shack, Little Caesars (confirm permission).
4. **The core tool (the hero):** a "build your setup" quote composer on screen 1.
   - Choose the business type (restaurant or bar, retail, office), the number of tills or stations, and Wi-Fi and backup needs.
   - The setup builds up visually (terminals, printer, network, backup) with a plain-words list of what Digitech installs and supports.
   - "Send for a quote" emails sales@ with the list. Calling is the second option.
5. **Keep:**
   - From their site: the services, sales@digitechbahamas.com, (242) 676-2033 and 676-3022, and #33 East Avenue, Centreville.
   - From our previous version: anything the side-by-side critic scores as working.
6. **Fix:**
   - Every CTA links to "#".
   - Theme stock photos.
   - Counters reading "0+".
   - A conflicting directory address.
   - Letter-split headings and the typo "gound".
7. **Screens:**
   1. The quote composer with the setup building up.
   2. POS for restaurants and retail: what Razor does for them.
   3. Network, Wi-Fi and backup.
   4. Support after install: who answers and how.
   5. Clients (logos with permission).
   6. Contact and quote.
8. **Look and feel:**
   - **Mood:** dependable and local, product-led.
   - **Visuals:** studio-lit 3D of the POS hardware is justified here, because it explains the product. No stock photos.
   - **Type:** a clean grotesk with a technical mono only for specs.
   - **Wow:** the setup assembling as you answer.
