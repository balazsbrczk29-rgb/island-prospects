# Kelly's House & Home (Kelly's Home Centre Ltd): job spec
Current site: kellysbahamas.com (online shop with departments, gift registry via eagleregistry.net, in-store pick up) · Previous concept: https://balazsbrczk29-rgb.github.io/island-prospects/kelly-s-home-centre/ · Kind of site: department store webshop front + Family Island ordering, handing off to their real store

1. **The one job:** get a household (in Nassau or on a Family Island) to shop a department on Kelly's online store, or start a gift registry, with clear delivery to their island.
2. **The visitor:** Nassau households, couples and parents-to-be building registries, DIY homeowners and contractors, and Family Island residents ordering for the mail boat on slow phone connections. They need: is it in stock here, how does it reach my island, what does delivery cost.
3. **The product (their site, Nov 5):**
   - **Departments:** Auto, Crystal, Hardware, Home Decor, Houseware, Electrical, Lawn, Outdoor, Paint, Plumbing, Sports, Stationery, Toys, Pet, Baby, Building Supplies, Hand & Power Tools, Home Textiles, Seasonal, Office & School; "Nassau's largest department store with 16 departments ranging from fine china to hardware, water tanks to bath towels"; 40,000 sq ft; best toy selection in Nassau; household name since 1927.
   - **Services:** gift registry (weddings, showers, birthdays, anniversaries); Contractor Cards; in-store pick up; Kelly's Lumber sister store.
   - **Delivery:** mail boat delivery to Potters Cay Dock $25 + VAT, customer arranges freight, Mon to Fri with cut-off, needs receiver's name, boat, settlement and island; local delivery New Providence and Paradise Island Mon to Sat.
   - **Contact:** The Mall at Marathon, Robinson Road, P.O. Box N-865, Nassau; (242) 393-4002; mail@kellysbahamas.com.
4. **The core tool (the hero):** the department wall with the island switch on screen 1: pick a department (their real category photos) or a life moment from their own copy (getting married, expecting a baby, painting your house, planting a garden), and choose your island; New Providence shows local delivery or pick up, a Family Island shows the mail boat steps with the $25 + VAT dock fee and a filled-in delivery note to copy. Every item and department links to their store.
5. **Keep:**
   - **From their site:** all departments and category photos, the shop and checkout, registry, Contractor Cards, delivery rules, contact, since 1927.
   - **From our previous version:** the drifting department wall of real category photos, the life-moments selector, the mail boat instruction builder, Young Serif + Nunito Sans, bougainvillea and sea palette.
6. **Fix:**
   - The homepage leads with seasonal banners; heritage and "largest department store" are hidden on About.
   - Store hours and mail boat instructions are images only; an April 2025 flyer still loads on every page.
   - Registry, Contractor Cards and pick up are scattered.
7. **Screens:**
   1. "Fine china to water tanks, since 1927" with the department wall and island switch.
   2. Departments index with photos, each into their store.
   3. Ordering from a Family Island: mail boat steps as text, the note builder.
   4. Gift registry and life moments.
   5. Contractors: Contractor Cards, building supplies, Kelly's Lumber.
   6. Visit The Mall at Marathon: hours (to confirm), map, phone.
8. **Look and feel:** warm, generous, familiar, festive, dependable. Keep the previous type and palette; one accent for Shop. Photography: their category images at native size (hotlinked from kellysbahamas.com) and the store front; one consistent warm grade. Wow: switch the island to Exuma and the wall turns into the trip to Potters Cay: the department you picked travels to a mail boat tag with your settlement written on it.

Research this run (Nov 22): none (facts from the Nov 5 brief).
Assumptions to confirm: store hours (only an image today); Contractor Cards terms; mail boat cut-off time; current departments count (16 vs 20 listed).
