# Bahamas Business Solutions Limited (BBSL): job spec
Current site: bbsl.com (WordPress; Home, About, Sales, Service, Copy Service, Vendors, a "Get a quote" form and a separate "Xerox quote" form; every page still carries `noindex, nofollow`, re-checked Nov 21) · Previous concept: https://balazsbrczk29-rgb.github.io/island-prospects/bahamas-business-solutions-limited/ (two pages: print-proof hero with crop marks and a 3D fanned stack of six documents, product-line index, service-plan coverage chooser, tabbed quote router to sales, service and copy inboxes, Nassau and Freeport office switcher, Document Centre job-ticket builder) · Kind of site: B2B brochure + three enquiry routes (equipment quote, service call, print job)

1. **The one job:** route each business visitor to the right BBSL desk with a usable request: an equipment or managed-print quote (sales@), a service call on a machine that is down (service@ or 242-605-6006), or a print job for the Document Centre (copyservice@).
2. **The visitor:**
   - Office managers, procurement and IT leads at government agencies, banks, utilities, clinics, hotels and mid-sized firms in Nassau and Freeport, at a desk, comparing a local dealer with ordering from Florida.
   - The person whose copier, printer or ATM just stopped, on a phone, wanting the right number now.
   - Walk-in print customers (architects, contractors, event organisers) with a file and a deadline.
   They must decide: is this the authorised dealer, will an engineer actually come on my island, which plan covers what, and who do I call.
3. **The product (bbsl.com, read Nov 21 and Nov 5):**
   - **Sales:** exclusive local Xerox distributor (multifunction printers, stand-alone printers, production devices, high-resolution scanners, local warranty); authorised NCR dealer (ATMs, payment solutions, POS including mobile, table and guest management); OnBase by Hyland local representative; Lenovo and HP PCs, servers and networking; Neopost sorter, folder and inserter; security (video surveillance, access control, theft prevention); vendor list Xerox, NCR Atleos, Riso, Lenovo, HP. Sales (242) 605-6000, sales@bbsl.com.
   - **Service:** services everything it sells; factory-trained, industry-certified field service engineers, some with 25+ years; helpdesk 8:30 am to 5:00 pm Monday to Friday; carry-in service centre. Plans: time and materials, full-service maintenance (parts and labour), managed print (everything except paper), or something in between. Service options listed: Xerox authorized distributor and service center, NCR authorized distributor and service center, ATM and financial equipment repair, computer repair, printer and fax repair, network installation and support, structured cabling, IT consulting services. Service (242) 605-6006, service@bbsl.com.
   - **Document Centre / Copy Service:** digital on-demand printing, architectural and engineering, wide-format colour, banners and posters, custom printing, scanning and imaging, binding, graphic design, booklets, conference services; remote submission; powered by Xerox. (242) 605-6027, copyservice@bbsl.com.
   - **Company:** 100% Bahamian owned; started as Xerox (Bahamas) Ltd in Nassau in 1966, Freeport office two years later; 1987 management buyout (Bahamas Copier & Office Products), NCR rights later in 1987; merged into Bahamas Business Solutions Limited in August 2002; 80+ full-time employees; 3,000+ customers across The Bahamas and Turks and Caicos; clients by sector: government, commercial and private banks, utilities, healthcare, hotels and hospitality.
   - **Offices:** Nassau (242) 605-6000, Collins Avenue and 7th Terrace (directory); Freeport (242) 688-8100, Queens Highway (LinkedIn snippet, to confirm); info@bbsl.com; tagline "Global Technology. Delivered Locally."
4. **The core tool (the hero):** a "What do you need today?" router on screen 1, beside the 1966 Xerox heritage line.
   - Three doors: **My machine is down**, **I need equipment or a print contract**, **I have a print job**. One tap opens a short, specific form in place.
   - **Down:** island (Nassau or Freeport), brand and machine type (Xerox, NCR ATM or POS, PC or network, other), error or symptom, location and contact; shows the helpdesk hours, whether the helpdesk is open now, the direct service number as a call button, and the carry-in option. Sends a structured ticket to service@.
   - **Equipment / contract:** product line from their list, rough volume (pages per month or number of sites), plan preference with a plain coverage table (time and materials vs full service vs managed print: what each covers, as on their Service page). Sends to sales@ (or the Xerox quote route).
   - **Print job:** job type from their list, size, quantity, deadline, file upload or "I'll bring it in", island; sends to copyservice@.
   - Every route also shows the right phone number for that desk and island. No prices.
5. **Keep:**
   - **From their site:** every product line, vendor and service option as listed, the three plan types, the helpdesk hours and carry-in centre, the Document Centre service list and remote submission, all five numbers and four email addresses, both quote forms (Get a quote, Xerox quote), the tagline, the 1966 history.
   - **From our previous version:** the print-proof visual language (crop marks, colour bar, slug line), the 3D fanned stack of the documents BBSL actually produces (kept as the screen 2 showpiece, not the hero), the product-line index with detail panel, the coverage chooser, the tabbed quote router (now the hero), the office switcher with map, the Document Centre job ticket, Instrument Serif with Geist.
6. **Fix:**
   - `noindex, nofollow` on every page: Google is told not to list the company. This is the opening line of the pitch.
   - The homepage is a slogan and five logos; nothing says what they do, on which islands, or who to call.
   - One "Get a quote" form for everything; a broken-machine customer has to find the Service page for the number.
   - No street address for either office on the site; Freeport is barely mentioned although it is half the business's footprint.
   - Leftover WooCommerce "added to the cart" message on every page; generic page titles.
   - Previous concept: the 3D document stack was the hero and the router sat further down; the person with a dead machine needs the router first.
7. **Screens:**
   1. The router (down / equipment / print job) with Nassau and Freeport numbers, the 1966 Xerox line, the authorised-dealer marks.
   2. What BBSL handles: the 3D stack of real document types, each sheet opening its product line (Xerox, NCR, OnBase, Lenovo and HP, Neopost, security).
   3. Service that comes to you: engineers, helpdesk hours, carry-in centre, the three plans compared, the service options list.
   4. The Document Centre: services, remote submission, the job ticket, its own page with the full list.
   5. Since 1966: the history line from Xerox (Bahamas) Ltd to BBSL, 3,000+ customers, sectors served, 100% Bahamian.
   6. Two offices: Nassau and Freeport switcher with map, numbers, email, hours; Turks and Caicos mention.
8. **Look and feel:**
   - **Mood:** precise, dependable, heritage, calm; a document company that knows paper and machines.
   - **Type:** keep Instrument Serif (display) with Geist and Geist Mono for labels and ticket fields; tabular figures for numbers and hours.
   - **Palette:** keep paper, sheet, ink, graphite, process cyan accent; toner yellow only as small labels on dark.
   - **Photography:** they have none usable (vendor logos and one banner); keep the procedural 3D paper and print-production typography; ask the owner for showroom, Document Centre and engineer photos and list it in the assumptions. No stock.
   - **Wow:** pick "My machine is down" and the page turns into a service ticket being typed out on a proof sheet, with the helpdesk shown open or closed right now and the Freeport or Nassau engineer line one tap away.
