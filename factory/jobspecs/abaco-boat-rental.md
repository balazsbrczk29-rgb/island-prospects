# Abaco Boat Rental: job spec
Current site: abacoboatrental.com (Squarespace + Booqable live booking) · Previous concept: the gen-1 version with the Sea of Abaco delivery chart (restored, live now) · Kind of site: booking

1. **The one job:** plan and book the boat for your stay. Hand off to their Booqable booking, or send a prefilled WhatsApp quote.
2. **The visitor:** families and friends staying on Elbow Cay, Hope Town, Guana Cay, Marsh Harbour or Man-O-War Cay, mostly American, planning a week on a phone. They need to know where the boat can take them, whether it gets delivered to their cay, and the true total.
3. **The product:**
   - **The boat:** one boat type, a 24 ft Key West center console, seats 8, 250 hp, Garmin GPS.
   - **Rates:** $475 for 1 day, $450 a day for 2, $330 a day for 3 to 6, $303 a day for 7 or more (VAT excluded, 24-hour rentals).
   - **Delivery:** free at Man-O-War Cay; $100 per direction to Elbow Cay, Hope Town, Guana Cay, Scotland Cay and Marsh Harbour; $300 to Treasure Cay and Little Harbour.
   - **Add-ons:** captain $50 or $100 an hour, snorkel sets.
   - **Season and hours:** open 9 to 5 daily, November 1 to August 15.
4. **The core tool (the hero):** an immersive Sea of Abaco map planner on screen 1.
   - It is a real-feeling map of the cays (a 3D or richly lit map is allowed and wanted). Tap your cay and the delivery route draws from Man-O-War, with its fee.
   - Pick your days: the day rate rolls down. The map shows the places a day can take you (Hope Town lighthouse, Great Guana, the cays), within the rental boundary.
   - The total updates live, then "Book on Booqable" or "Send this quote on WhatsApp".
   - It shows how far your money takes you. The previous version's chart and cost builder are the starting point: elevate them, don't drop them.
5. **Keep:** from their site, Booqable live booking, rates, delivery fees, add-ons, FAQ, WhatsApp and email, and the Salted Lime link. From our previous version, the delivery chart linked to the cost builder, and the spec-sheet hero idea.
6. **Fix:**

7. **Screens:**
   1. The map planner with the boat and the live total.
   2. The boat: their photos, specs and what's on board.
   3. Rates and delivery, set out clearly.
   4. What a day looks like: their own photos of the turtles and the shallows.
   5. FAQ and the boundary.
   6. Contact.
8. **Look and feel:** sunny, capable, salty, clear. The map is the wow: water you can almost touch, routes that draw, the money counting down as days go up. Use their real boat photo at native size. Pick one bold display face and one clean text face.
