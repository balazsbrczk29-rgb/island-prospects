# Bluff House Beach Resort & Marina: job spec
Current site: bluffhouse.com (Wix; rooms book on Cloudbeds, slips on Dockwa) · Previous concept: https://balazsbrczk29-rgb.github.io/island-prospects/bluff-house-beach-resort-marina/ (a magazine feature about the Out Islands' first resort in which you book the balcony you just read about, by season) · Kind of site: booking (rooms + slips)

1. **The one job:** book a stay (suite or cottage) on Cloudbeds, or reserve a slip on Dockwa.
2. **The visitor:** US couples and families planning months ahead, returning guests, boaters crossing to the Abacos from Florida and the Carolinas, wedding couples. On phones. They must decide: suite or cottage, what it costs in their season, whether their boat fits White Sound's 7 ft channel, and that the place is still the one they remember.
3. **The product:**
   - Eight air-conditioned hotel suites (upper and lower) with balconies over the Sea of Abaco; cottages and villas (Summerset, La Conchita, Turtle Bluff Villa, Pineapple House Villa, Sweet Guava Villa, Blue Bay Cottage, Dove Cottage, Coral Haven, Sunset Cottage 4), 1 to 4 bedrooms.
   - Season rates from their rates page (e.g. Christmas, Winter, Spring, Summer, End of season; closed Sept 3 to Oct 31), shown as published with a confirm note.
   - Marina: 40 slips, 7 ft MLW channel into White Sound, finger piers with power, water and Wi-Fi, 50/100 amp, showers and laundry, dockmaster assist; last-published per-foot rates by season, +VAT.
   - The Boathouse restaurant, Tranquil Turtle Beach Bar (Caribbean Journal Top 25), Low Tide gift shop, weddings; 12 acres from White Sound to the Sea of Abaco, the only private beach on Green Turtle Cay.
   - The first and now oldest Out Island resort, since the early 1950s; new owners VIP Marinas Group from December 2025.
4. **The core tool (the hero):** a "stay or dock" planner on screen 1: pick your arrival month and whether you come by plane or by boat. By plane: suite (upper or lower) or cottage with that season's published rate and its own photo, then Book on Cloudbeds. By boat: enter LOA and draft, see the fit against 7 ft MLW and the season's per-foot estimate, then Reserve on Dockwa.
5. **Keep:** from their site: Cloudbeds booking, Dockwa slip booking, suites and cottages with their photos and 3D tours, season rates, marina specs and rates, dining, beach bar, weddings, history, phones (+1 954 284 7341, +1 242 824 9550 WhatsApp) and frontdesk@bluffhouse.com. From our previous version: the magazine voice and history, booking the balcony you just read about.

7. **Screens:**
   1. Their bluff and balcony photograph with the stay-or-dock planner.
   2. The suites and cottages compared, each with photo, beds, view, season rate and Book.
   3. The marina: specs, fit, rates, Dockwa.
   4. Eat and drink: the Boathouse and the Tranquil Turtle; weddings.
   5. Seventy-odd years on the bluff (the history, told short).
   6. Getting here and contact.
8. **Look and feel:** storied, breezy, hilltop, unhurried, nautical. A literary serif for headlines and a calm sans; paper ground with Abaco blue. Their own photographs large. The wow: the planner switching from a balcony at sunset to a slip in White Sound as you choose plane or boat.
