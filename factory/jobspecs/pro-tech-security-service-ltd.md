# Pro Tech Security Service Ltd: job spec
Current site: none (directory listings, YouTube channel) · Previous concept: https://balazsbrczk29-rgb.github.io/island-prospects/pro-tech-security-service-ltd/ · Kind of site: brochure + call / site-survey request

1. **The one job:** get Pro Tech to call you back about protecting your home or business: an alarm and CCTV install with monitoring, or guards.
2. **The visitor:**
   - Who: Nassau homeowners and small business owners, often after a break-in nearby or an insurance prompt, plus property managers.
   - How: on a phone, comparing them with the big firms and with DIY cameras.
   - What they need to decide: are they licensed and local, what do they actually do, and will someone answer.
3. **The product:**
   - **Divisions:** security guard services, CCTV and alarm sales and service, 24/7 central station monitoring, security supplies, consulting and investigations, collections.
   - **Credentials:** 100% Bahamian owned since 2004; licensed by the Ministry of National Security; officers trained in-house.
   - **Contact:** #2 Farrington Rd & Gregory St; 242-328-0653 (plus 242-325-3335 on older listings); international 1-954-237-1017; office Mon to Fri 9 to 5.
4. **The core tool (the hero):** a "what do you need covered" planner on screen 1.
   - Choose home, shop or office, or warehouse or yard.
   - Choose what you want: alarm, cameras, monitoring, guards, or "not sure".
   - Choose your area of New Providence.
   - It returns the matching divisions in plain words and a summary to read on the call, with Call 242-328-0653 as the main button and WhatsApp/email only if sourced.
   - It also shows whether the office is open now; the central station runs 24/7.
5. **Keep:**
   - From their listings: every division, both addresses (flag the older one), all phones, the hours, and the licence statement.
   - From our previous version: the real-time Nassau hour (office open or closed, monitoring always on). It is folded into the planner rather than being the whole page.
6. **Fix:**
   - There is no website.
   - The name is spelled four ways.
   - The listings show two addresses and three phones.
   - There are no photos of their work and no way to ask online.
7. **Screens:**
   1. The planner, with the open-now line and the call button.
   2. The six divisions explained, one line each, with what you get.
   3. Monitoring 24/7: what happens when an alarm goes off (as a process, no invented response times).
   4. Licensed, Bahamian owned, since 2004.
   5. Contact, with the address and directions.
8. **Look and feel:** calm, solid and local, not military. Light page with one night section for monitoring. Photography is illustrative (Lovable), clearly labelled; use their own install photos if sourced from YouTube thumbnails. The wow is the planner's live read-out.
