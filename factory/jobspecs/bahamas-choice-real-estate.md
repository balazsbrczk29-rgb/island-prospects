# Bahamas Choice Real Estate: job spec
Current site: bahamaschoicerealestate.com (WordPress, AgentImage template with an IDX feed of about 50 listings) · Previous concept: https://balazsbrczk29-rgb.github.io/island-prospects/bahamas-choice-real-estate/ (gen 2, built) · Kind of site: listings

1. **The one job:** find a property (to buy or to rent) and ask the right agent for a viewing.
2. **The visitor:**
   - **Who:** Bahamian first-time buyers and families, and international investors and second-home buyers.
   - **How:** browsing on a phone, often from abroad.
   - **What they must decide:** which island, buy or rent, the budget, and which listing to view; and whether this small brokerage will handle it end to end.
3. **The product:**
   - **Listings:** the live IDX listings (homes, land, commercial, rentals) across the islands they present (New Providence and Paradise Island, Abaco, Exuma, Eleuthera, Andros, Long Island and more), plus the developments they present (Palm Cay, Goldwynn, Ocean Club Residences and others).
   - **The team:** Lana Munnings-Basalyga (Principal Broker, BREA licence #480), Greg Munnings, Sherry Morris and Erica Pinder.
   - **Help beyond the sale:** banking, legal, government requirements and relocation.
4. **The core tool (the hero):** a property finder on screen 1.
   - Set buy or rent, the island (a map of the islands they serve, tap to choose), the type and the price band.
   - The matching listings appear as photo cards with price, beds and baths.
   - Each card has "Ask for a viewing", which goes to the named agent by phone, WhatsApp or email with the listing reference filled in.
5. **Keep:**
   - From their site: the IDX listings and their detail pages, the islands and developments, the team with their direct lines, the client words they publish (quoted, no stars), and sales@ plus 242-677-3868.
   - From our previous version: anything the side-by-side critic scores as working.
6. **Fix:**
   - Generic template feel.
   - The search sits apart from the story.
   - The end-to-end promise ("listening and end-to-end service") is buried on Meet the Team.
   - No clear "who do I call for this listing".
7. **Screens:**
   1. A Harbour Island or Nassau photo of theirs and the finder with live results.
   2. The results grid and list, with filters kept.
   3. Islands and developments.
   4. End to end: what happens from viewing to keys, including the banking, legal and relocation help.
   5. The team, each with a direct line.
   6. Client words, then contact.
8. **Look and feel:**
   - **Mood:** confident, warm, local; a family brokerage, not a portal.
   - **Photography:** listing photos are the star, at their native size.
   - **Type:** a refined serif with a clear sans.
   - **Wow:** the island map that filters the listings.
