# BHM (Bahamas Hot Mix): job spec
Current site: bhmgroup.com (WordPress Divi 4.27, about 25 pages, a filterable portfolio of about 60 projects) · Previous concept: https://balazsbrczk29-rgb.github.io/island-prospects/bahamas-hot-mix-co/ (gen 2, built) · Kind of site: brochure + project portfolio + quote enquiry

1. **The one job:** get a procurement or project manager to request a quote or tender conversation, routed to the right office.
2. **The visitor:**
   - **Who:** procurement and project managers at ministries, the airport authority, resort developers and engineering consultants, plus homeowners who need a driveway paved.
   - **How:** mostly desktop.
   - **What they must decide:** whether BHM can deliver on their island, with certified quality, on time.
3. **The product:**
   - **Capabilities:** roads, airports and runways, ports, reverse osmosis water, buildings, coastal works.
   - **Supply:** hot mix asphalt (three plants, two fully mobile) and ready mix concrete (Grand Bahama).
   - **Quality:** ISO 9001, 14001 and 45001 (Bureau Veritas), with ISO 17025 accredited labs.
   - **Projects:** about 60 named projects (LPIA runway 10-28, Marsh Harbour, Hog Cay and others).
   - **Offices:** Nassau, Grand Bahama and Abaco, all on +1 (242) 601 6350.
4. **The core tool (the hero):** a project atlas on screen 1.
   - A map of The Bahamas with every project pinned by category.
   - Filter by category (runways, roads, ports, water), and the pins and list respond.
   - Tap a project for its photo and scope.
   - Beside the atlas, "Request a quote" asks the island and the type of work, then routes to the nearest office.
5. **Keep:**
   - From their site: the filterable portfolio, the nine expertise areas, the ISO certifications, the three offices and phones, asphalt and ready mix supply, and careers.
   - From our previous version: the approach-to-land runway idea and the project atlas.
6. **Fix:**
   - Slider arrows link to "#".
   - Expertise icons have no words.
   - "Bahamas Hot Mix" is never named.
   - A reCAPTCHA error shows in every footer.
   - Drone photos are shown as 400 px thumbnails.
   - An unsourced "#1" claim.
7. **Screens:**
   1. The atlas and the quote router.
   2. Runways (their strongest proof).
   3. Roads, ports and water.
   4. Materials: the mobile asphalt plants and ready mix, with labs and ISO.
   5. The full project list (filterable).
   6. Offices and careers.
8. **Look and feel:**
   - **Mood:** engineered, certain, large-scale.
   - **Photography:** their drone photography at native size.
   - **Wow:** a real interactive map (and real-time 3D only for the runway approach, because it explains the work).
   - **Type:** a strong grotesk with tabular numbers for project data.
