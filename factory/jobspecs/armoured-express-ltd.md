# Armoured Express (Bahamas) Ltd.: job spec
Current site: none (the company's own text lives in a featured BahamasLocal listing; a near-empty Facebook page; no email published) · Previous concept: https://balazsbrczk29-rgb.github.io/island-prospects/armoured-express-ltd/ (two pages: typographic monument hero with a line-drawn armoured truck arriving, a chain-of-custody stepper with a transit tag that stamps "Signed" at each hand-off, "Request a service proposal" planner ending in a call) · Kind of site: B2B brochure + service proposal request (Track B, phone only)

1. **The one job:** get a bank, retailer or business to request a service proposal for cash in transit, ATM replenishment, Family Island air courier or guarding, with enough detail (island, sites, frequency, service) for the first call to 242-323-4430.
2. **The visitor:** operations, treasury and risk managers at banks, credit unions, retailers, fuel stations, casinos and government revenue points in Nassau and Grand Bahama, plus insurers and auditors checking a provider; mostly at a desk, comparing providers, needing proof of insurance, process and 24-hour reach before they ever call.
3. **The product (their own listing text, re-seen Nov 21):**
   - **Services:** cash in transit with secure armoured vehicles and highly trained, uniformed, armed guards; comprehensive ATM services (replenishment and first-line maintenance; "the first to cater to the demand for ATM services"); air courier solutions for the Family Islands; 24-hour support; a seasoned management team of armoured security experts.
   - **Sister company:** Security Services (Bah.) Ltd. for static guard services and aviation security: "Two companies, one family, one mission."
   - **Proof points they state:** established 1961 (originally Burns Security International), "pioneered the armored security industry in the Bahamas", over 60 years; public liability coverage up to two million dollars; security countermeasures best practice; surveillance-enabled cash in transit; secure locked transit bags; Armoured Services Officers cross-trained in physical security as part of a business continuity plan; 100% Bahamian owned and operated; Nassau and Grand Bahama.
   - **Contact:** Stapledon Gardens, P.O. Box N-4499, Nassau; 242-323-4430 and 242-328-6841; Mon to Fri 9:00 to 5:00 (office), 24-hour support.
4. **The core tool (the hero):** a service proposal planner on screen 1.
   - Pick what you need (cash pickups, ATM replenishment, ATM first-line maintenance, Family Island air courier, static guards, aviation security), the island (New Providence, Grand Bahama, a Family Island for courier), number of sites or ATMs, frequency (daily, set days, on call) and your sector.
   - As you choose, the chain-of-custody line beside it draws the route your consignment takes (your site, locked transit bag, armoured vehicle under surveillance, vault or ATM or aircraft, receipt), stamping each hand-off, with the proof point that covers that step (e.g. two million dollar liability at the vehicle step).
   - The summary hands off as a call button to 242-323-4430 (office hours) and a copyable or printable request summary; when an email is confirmed, a send button. No prices.
5. **Keep:**
   - **From their listing:** every service, the sister company and its line, all proof points in their wording, 1961 and Burns Security International, the two islands, both phone numbers, the address and hours.
   - **From our previous version:** the line-drawn truck arrival (their listing shows a Hino L-series), the chain-of-custody stepper with the stamping transit tag (now joined to the planner instead of a separate section), Libre Franklin, the stone, ink and brass palette, the "Request a service proposal" wording.
6. **Fix:**
   - No website: the only description of the company sits in a paid directory listing, and "cash in transit Bahamas" or "ATM replenishment Nassau" finds nothing of theirs.
   - No email; enquiries go through a directory form.
   - Security Services (Bah.) Ltd. has no home online.
   - The previous concept split the stepper and the planner, so the strongest proof never sat beside the request.
7. **Screens:**
   1. The truck arriving, the 1961 line, and the planner with the custody line.
   2. Services in depth: cash in transit, ATM services, Family Island air courier, 24-hour support.
   3. How a consignment is protected: the full chain of custody, step by step, with each stated safeguard.
   4. The sister company: static guards and aviation security.
   5. Since 1961: from Burns Security International to today, 100% Bahamian, Nassau and Grand Bahama.
   6. Contact: both numbers, hours, address, map, and the proposal summary again.
8. **Look and feel:**
   - **Mood:** sober, exact, accountable; a bank's supplier, not an action film. Quiet confidence over menace.
   - **Type:** keep Libre Franklin (heavy display in sentence case, regular text); tabular figures for times and the custody log.
   - **Palette:** keep stone ground, ink, brass accent and steel; no red, no black-and-yellow hazard look.
   - **Photography:** none usable (directory uploads are not allowed); keep the technical line drawing of the truck and add real-time 3D of the locked transit bag and seal only if it reads as a real studio-lit object; ask the owner for crew, vehicle and vault photos (listed in assumptions).
   - **Wow:** tick "ATM replenishment, 6 ATMs, Grand Bahama, daily" and the custody line redraws for that exact run, each hand-off stamping "Signed" with its safeguard, so a risk manager sees the process they would be buying before they pick up the phone.
