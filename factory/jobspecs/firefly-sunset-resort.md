# Firefly Sunset Resort: job spec
Current site: fireflysunsetresort.com (Duda, Request to book per villa on Guesty, dinner on Tablein) · Previous concept: https://balazsbrczk29-rgb.github.io/island-prospects/firefly-sunset-resort/ (gen 2, live sunset countdown) · Kind of site: booking (six villas, request-to-book handoff)

1. **The one job:** choose a villa and your dates, see the full price with VAT, and send the request to book (their Guesty page for that villa, or the email they ask for). Secondary: reserve dinner at the Firefly Bar & Grill on Tablein.
2. **The visitor:**
   - **US families and couples** (Southeast and Northeast), honeymooners, and groups pricing the whole resort, on a phone, comparing Hope Town rentals and Hope Town Inn & Marina.
   - **Boaters** renting a boat for the week.
   - **Locals and Hope Town visitors** looking for the Bar & Grill hours.
   - **What they must decide:** which villa fits, what a week really costs with VAT, and how to get to Elbow Cay.
3. **The product:**
   - **Six villas** (each with its own Guesty Request to book link):

     | Villa | Beds / baths | In season (Feb to Aug) | Off season (Sep to Jan) |
     |---|---|---|---|
     | Java Rum Plantation | 3 / 2 | $1,500 day, $10,200 week | $1,350 day, $9,180 week |
     | Mint To Be (plunge pool) | 2 / 2 | $1,125 day, $7,650 week | $1,000 day, $6,800 week |
     | Firefly | 2 / 2 | $800 day, $5,440 week | $665 day, $4,525 week |
     | Dragonfly | 2 / 2 | $800 day, $5,440 week | $665 day, $4,525 week |
     | Butterfly | 2 / 2 | $800 day, $5,440 week | $665 day, $4,525 week |
     | Irvin House | 3 / 2.5 | not published (ask) | not published (ask) |

   - **Rules:** rates exclude 10% VAT, 3-night minimum in season; off season excludes Thanksgiving, Christmas and New Year.
   - **Whole resort:** $49,258 a week or $21,731 for 3 days, VAT included.
   - **On site:** private beach, two pools (upper pool for villa guests), dock slips, an artificial reef for snorkelling, free kayaks, boutique, pet friendly, concierge (golf carts, boats, charters, provisions).
   - **Firefly Bar & Grill:** seven days, breakfast 8 to 11, lunch 11:30 to 4, dinner 5:30 to 9, Tablein reservations, parties of 12+ call 1 (242) 366-0145.
   - **Getting here:** fly to Marsh Harbour, 15 minutes by taxi to the ferry dock, 20 minutes by ferry to Hope Town, resort shuttle from the harbour.
4. **The core tool (the hero):** the villa and price check on screen 1, beside their sunset photograph.
   - Pick a villa (photo, beds, baths, one line), arrival date and nights. The tool reads the season from the date, enforces the 3-night minimum, and shows nights x rate (or the weekly rate when cheaper), the 10% VAT and the total.
   - "Request to book" opens that villa's own Guesty page; "Email the resort" composes their exact ask (villa, dates, guests).
   - A "Whole resort" switch shows the two VAT-inclusive group prices. Irvin House shows "Rates on request".
   - The live "tonight's sunset over the Sea of Abaco" line from the previous version stays as a small line in the hero.
5. **Keep:**
   - **From their site:** all six Guesty links, the full rate table with VAT and minimums, the whole-resort rate, Bar & Grill hours and Tablein, the ferry directions, weddings and events, the concierge list.
   - **From our previous version:** the live sunset time computed for Hope Town, the conch-pink and shutter-green palette, the quiet one-photo-one-sentence screens after the tool, and the villas page rate table.
6. **Fix:**
   - Browser tabs and Google show "<title> Villas".
   - The homepage slider shows "Slide title" and "Button" placeholders over stock photos.
   - No phone number anywhere on the site.
   - Rates and the booking rule are buried at the bottom of the villas page.
   - The menu lives at /empty-page.
7. **Screens:**
   1. The sunset over the plunge pool, the live sunset line, and the villa and price check with Request to book.
   2. The six villas side by side: photos, beds, baths, view, rate from, each with Request to book.
   3. The resort: beach, pools, reef, dock, kayaks, in a few large photos with one sentence each.
   4. Firefly Bar & Grill: hours, Reserve dinner (Tablein), to-go orders.
   5. Getting to Elbow Cay: plane, taxi, ferry, shuttle as one line of four steps.
   6. Concierge and weddings: golf carts, boats, charters, provisions; the whole resort for groups.
   7. Contact: email first, the Bar & Grill phone, Request to book.
8. **Look and feel:**
   - **Mood:** quiet luxury at sunset, warm, unhurried.
   - **Type:** one warm display serif, one humanist sans with tabular figures for prices.
   - **Palette:** conch pink (the sunset on their deck) with shutter green as the one action colour.
   - **Photography:** their own professional 1920 px photos, full-bleed; no stock.
   - **Wow:** the price of your exact week, VAT and all, appears under tonight's sunset time, and one tap sends the request.
