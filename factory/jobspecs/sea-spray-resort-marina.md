# Sea Spray Resort & Marina: job spec
Current site: seasprayresort.com (Webflow template, 2026; stays on Guesty, food on Revel online ordering, a "Book a slip" page) · Previous concept: https://balazsbrczk29-rgb.github.io/island-prospects/sea-spray-resort-marina/ (gen 2, marina-first single page, fit and cost check on screen 2 sending the slip request on WhatsApp, a day at Sea Spray in their photographs) · Kind of site: booking (slip request, Guesty stays, Revel ordering) + residences enquiry

1. **The one job:** request a slip for your boat and dates in White Sound. Booking a stay (Guesty) and ordering food (Revel) are the second and third jobs; homes for sale are an enquiry.
2. **The visitor:**
   - **US East Coast owners and captains** (sportfish and cruisers, Florida, the Carolinas, the owners' Charleston network) planning an Abaco run on a phone.
   - **Villa guests** booking a stay.
   - **Boaters already on Elbow Cay** ordering food, checking Thursday's entertainment or fuel.
   - **Second-home buyers** in the $3M to $6M bracket.
   - **What they must decide:** does she fit, what will it cost for my nights and season, how do I reach the dock, what is on this week.
3. **The product:**
   - **Marina:** 62 slips (their own site), boats 20 to 130 ft, drafts to 7 ft; gas and diesel, ice; 30, 50 and 100 amp power with daily minimums; water $0.40/gal; VHF 16.
   - **Published slip rates per ft per night:** high season (1 March to 31 August) nightly $5.65, 30 days $4.45, 90 days $3.75; low season (1 September to 28 February) nightly $3.65, 30 days $2.75; annual $1.25.
   - **Stay:** villas booked on Guesty.
   - **Eat and drink:** marina bar and restaurant over White Sound, Order Online on Revel.
   - **Events:** Happy Hour, Thursday Night Entertainment (live music, DJ, Junkanoo), Game Night, Karaoke; private events; fishing tournaments.
   - **Residences:** oceanfront homes, 6 bed, 6.5 bath, 3,879 sq ft, private pool, from $5,999,500; Marina View Cottage Plan A, 3 bed, 3.5 bath, 2,264 sq ft, from $3,050,000 (oceanfront from $4,050,000); Plan B, 3 bed (4-bed option), 3.5 bath, 2,492 sq ft, from $3,300,000 (oceanfront from $4,250,000); Sales Package 2026 PDF.
   - **Story:** the Albury family built "Sea Spray" boats here from the 1980s; destroyed by Dorian; brought back from 2022 by four Charleston friends and their families.
   - **Contact:** +1 242 577 4696 (also WhatsApp), VHF 16, White Sound, Elbow Cay; Instagram @seasprayabaco.
4. **The core tool (the hero):** "Will she fit, and what will it cost?" on screen 1, beside the marina at sunset.
   - Length (slider and number), draft, month of arrival and nights. The fit answer is live against 20 to 130 ft and 7 ft.
   - The estimate picks the season from the month and the tier from the nights (nightly, 30 days, 90 days) and shows the rate and the total, with power minimums and water as extras.
   - "Request this slip" sends the boat, dates and estimate on WhatsApp (+1 242 577 4696, country code included) or to their Book a slip page; "Call" and "VHF 16" beside it.
   - Two quiet secondary buttons in the same panel: "Book your stay" (Guesty) and "Order food" (Revel).
5. **Keep:**
   - **From their site:** Book a slip, Book your stay (Guesty), Order online (Revel), the published rates and specs, the events, private events and fishing tournaments, the residences with every published figure and the Sales Package, gallery photographs, Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp.
   - **From our previous version:** the fit and cost check (moved up to screen 1), the WhatsApp slip request, the day at Sea Spray told in their own photographs, the single marina-first page.

8. **Look and feel:**
   - **Mood:** salt-bright, exact, convivial: a marina run by people who boat.
   - **Type:** keep the previous version's pairing; big tabular numbers in the tool and the rates.
   - **Palette:** bright white-sand ground, deep sound navy, one shallow-water field, a sunset accent only if it is not orange or red.
   - **Photography:** their own Webflow originals (marina piers at sunset, sportfisher underway, mahi on the dock, tiki bar, open-air bar, fireworks, underwater), below source size.
   - **Wow:** a captain drags a length slider on a phone and watches White Sound answer: she fits, this is the week's cost, send it on WhatsApp.
