# Maximum Security & Patrol Services Ltd: job spec
Current site: none (maximumsecurityservice.com no longer resolves; directories only) · Previous concept: https://balazsbrczk29-rgb.github.io/island-prospects/maximum-security-patrol-services-ltd/ · Kind of site: brochure + request a proposal (call)

1. **The one job:** a facilities, procurement or property manager requests a security proposal by phone.
2. **The visitor:** managers at banks, retailers, offices and government agencies in Nassau, plus property managers and private clients. Often on a desktop at work, comparing 2 to 4 firms, then calling. They need to know: what you cover, that you have been doing it since 1993, and how fast they can get a proposal.
3. **The product:**
   - The services in their own words: trained security officers for diverse and special applications, patrols, investigations, consultancy, alarm monitoring, and customised services for special responses.
   - The clients they name: business and financial, government, retail, private and public.
   - Founded in 1993 by Byron C. Rodgers. #15 Carib Road (Carib House), Nassau. Licensed on the URCA register.
4. **The core tool (the hero):** a proposal builder on screen 1.
   - Choose the premises type (category-standard and listed as an assumption) and the services needed (officers, patrols, alarm monitoring, investigations, consultancy).
   - Add the hours to cover and the location.
   - It reads back a one-paragraph summary to say on the call, with Call 242-394-5425 / 242-393-7206.
5. **Keep:**
   - From the listings: both phones, the fax, the address and P.O. Box, the services list, the 1993 founding story, the sectors served.
   - From our previous version: the proposal builder and the summary to read out.
6. **Fix:**
   - The lost domain still printed in listings.
   - No email and no way to request a proposal.
   - The company story lives only on a directory.
   - Nothing a procurement manager can forward.
7. **Screens:**
   1. The proposal builder with a plain headline naming the service.
   2. Services as clear lanes, each with one action.
   3. Sectors served.
   4. Since 1993: the founding story.
   5. How a proposal works: site visit, plan, officers on post. Phrased as categories and listed as assumptions.
   6. Call Carib Road: phones, fax, address and map.
8. **Look and feel:** calm, exact, institutional. One strong brand colour, a large grotesk, technical plan drawings. No own photos exist, so any illustrative photography is labelled. The wow: the builder draws coverage on a plan of the chosen premises as services are added.
