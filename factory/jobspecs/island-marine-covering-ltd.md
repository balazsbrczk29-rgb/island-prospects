# Island Marine Covering Ltd (Cover Up): job spec
Current site: coverupbahamas.com (broken WordPress brochure on Bluehost, no booking) · Previous concept: https://balazsbrczk29-rgb.github.io/island-prospects/island-marine-covering-ltd/ · Kind of site: brochure + enquiry (get a measure)

1. **The one job:** get a homeowner, hotel or boat owner to request a measure for an awning, weather screen, hurricane shutter or marine canvas job.
2. **The visitor:** Nassau homeowners with verandahs and pool decks, restaurant and hotel managers, yacht owners and captains, property managers before hurricane season. Mostly phone, often comparing with ordering from Florida. They need to know: do they make my thing, will it last the sun and storms, and how do I get someone to measure.
3. **The product:** made-to-measure canvas work sewn in Nassau, house and boat:
   - house: awnings and retractable awnings, weather screens and curtains, hurricane shutters, sun shades;
   - boat: Bimini tops (even on a 10 ft inflatable), isinglass enclosures (up to mega yachts), boat and ferry upholstery, bow rails.
   - The workshop on Raphia Close, Airport Industrial Park, and the family trade since 1992 (Ricky Nixon, son of Henry Nixon of Nixon's Upholstery, who upholstered Nassau's grand hotels).
4. **The core tool (the hero):** a "what needs covering?" selector on screen 1: pick House or Boat, then the job (awning, weather screen, shutters, Bimini, enclosure, upholstery).
   - It shows their own project photo for that job and the questions a measure needs: rough size, location, timing.
   - It hands off to WhatsApp (242) 359-0179 with a prefilled message, or a call.
5. **Keep:**
   - From their site: every product, their project and workshop photos, the 1992 family story, the WhatsApp number for an immediate response, the visit-the-workshop invitation, the email from the contact page.
   - From our previous version: the house/boat catalogue split and the WhatsApp enquiry.
6. **Fix:**
   - A Bluehost login box and maths puzzle show on the homepage.
   - "View Our Products" opens a WordPress sample page.
   - Template text is visible.
   - Social links are placeholders.
   - No phone or address on the homepage.
   - Product videos are download-only.
7. **Screens:**
   1. The selector with their best project photo, and WhatsApp in reach.
   2. House work, by job, as their photographs at native size.
   3. Boat work, by job.
   4. How a job runs: measure, sew in Nassau, fit. Storm-season note for shutters, phrased as category only.
   5. The family story since 1992 and the workshop.
   6. Visit or message: address and map, WhatsApp, phone, email.
8. **Look and feel:** workshop-honest and sunlit. Canvas, stitch and shade, not luxury gloss. Their own photos only. The wow: as you choose a job, the selected photo reveals like an awning unrolling over it.
