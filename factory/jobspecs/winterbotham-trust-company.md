# The Winterbotham Trust Company Limited (Winterbotham Group): job spec
Current site: winterbotham.com (custom site, (c) 2023; client e-banking at ebankingwinterbotham.com) · Previous concept: https://balazsbrczk29-rgb.github.io/island-prospects/winterbotham-trust-company/ · Kind of site: financial group brochure + consultation enquiry (phone) + client login

1. **The one job:** get a family, company, fund manager or their adviser to arrange a consultation with the Nassau desk for the right service (trust, funds, corporate, bank, brokerage, custody), and let existing clients reach e-banking in one click.
2. **The visitor:** international families, entrepreneurs, fund managers and institutions (Latin America, North America, Asia) and their lawyers doing due diligence, mostly on desktop. They need: regulator, certifications, governance, a real head office and a named desk.
3. **The product (their site, Nov 5):**
   - **Services:** Trust (trustee and administration, Private Trust Companies, corporate trust: escrow agent, loan trustee, dissolution trusts); Funds (licensing, incorporation, accounting, NAV, RTA, compliance, administration, reporting, investor relations, domiciliary); Corporate (formation, administration, accounting, compliance, governance, physical presence); Bank (wires, fiduciary placements, FX, multi-currency accounts, execution-only brokerage, Global Custody Platform); Brokerage (trading desk in Nassau); Global Custody; Corporate Finance and Sports Management via SeventyTwo.
   - **Proof:** regulated by the Central Bank of The Bahamas and the Securities Commission; ISO 9001 and ISO 27001; values Trust, Commitment, Excellence; "trust is something that is built over time"; the Winterbotham family history.
   - **Offices:** Nassau head office, Winterbotham Place, Marlborough & Queen Streets, +1 242 356 5454; Montevideo, Cayman Islands, Hong Kong, Brisbane (SeventyTwo Capital), San Juan, each with its phone.
4. **The core tool (the hero):** the consultation desk on screen 1: choose what you are structuring (a family trust, a fund, a company, banking and custody) and where you are, see the services and the office that serves it with its local time (live office clocks), then a summary to read and "Call Nassau +1 242 356 5454"; a quiet "Client e-banking" link beside it.
5. **Keep:**
   - **From their site:** every service line, the regulators, both ISO certificates, the values line, the history, all six offices and phones, e-banking.
   - **From our previous version:** typography and hairlines, live office clocks, booking tabs composing a summary to read out, "Arrange a consultation".
6. **Fix:**

   - (c) 2023 footer; six service icons all labelled "Trust"; the history portrait mislabelled "Geoffrey Hooper".
   - No meta description, generic title; no email, only a reCAPTCHA form.
7. **Screens:**
   1. "A Nassau trust house: trust is built over time" with the consultation desk.
   2. Services in depth, as a calm index that expands.
   3. Governance: regulators, ISO 9001 and 27001, group entities.
   4. The family history.
   5. Offices with live clocks, and the consultation summary again.
8. **Look and feel:** composed, lineal, exact, discreet, international (pictet.com restraint). One serif + one sans, ivory or stone ground, one deep accent for the consultation action.
