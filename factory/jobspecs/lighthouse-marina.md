# Lighthouse Marina: job spec
Current site: htlighthousemarina.com (Wix brochure, Gmail contact, no booking) · Previous concept: https://balazsbrczk29-rgb.github.io/island-prospects/lighthouse-marina/ (gen 2, lighthouse panorama + fit check + boatyard estimator) · Kind of site: brochure + enquiry (fuel, slips, boatyard, store)

1. **The one job:** get the boat in: request a slip or a haul-out and service, with a price you can trust before you call. Calling or VHF 16 is the alternative.
2. **The visitor:**
   - **Cruising captains and owners** arriving in Hope Town Harbour who need fuel, a slip or a mechanic, on a phone, often on the water.
   - **Abaco and second-home owners** comparing haul-out and storage prices for hurricane season.
   - **Renters and day boaters** looking for fuel, bait, ice and the store hours.
   - **What they must decide:** does my boat fit, what will the yard cost, when are you open, how do I reach you.
3. **The product:**
   - **Fuel:** 3 stations (2 gas, 1 diesel), the only marine fuel on Elbow Cay; docks and fuel station rebuilt.
   - **Slips:** 6 protected slips, to 60 ft LOA and 6 ft draft at low water, 30 and 50 amp, laundry and hot showers, 60 kW standby generator. Dockage rates not published.
   - **Boatyard** (Mon to Fri 8 to 4, rates from November 2024, plus 10% VAT): haul and dry storage to 10 tons; single hull haul $5.50/ft, launch $5.50/ft, blocking $50; twin hull $7.00/ft; storage single hull to 25 ft $200/month, 25 to 30 ft $8.50/ft/month, over 30 ft $9.00/ft/month; mechanic $110/hr, fiberglass $150/hr, bottom painting $50/hr, DIY $50/day, and the rest of the sheet.
   - **Service:** factory-trained technicians, Mercury Outboard sales and service.
   - **Ship's store:** tackle, bait, ice, clothing, snacks; Lighthouse Marina Liquors "reopening soon".
   - **Contact:** 1 (242) 366-0154, 1 (242) 577-0283, 1 (305) 735-8550, VHF 16, lighthousemarinaht@gmail.com.
4. **The core tool (the hero):** "Will she fit, and what will it cost?" on screen 1, beside the lighthouse and the fuel dock.
   - Enter LOA, draft and hull type (single or twin). The slip answer is live (fits 60 ft / 6 ft at low water, or talk to the dockmaster).
   - Below it, the yard estimate from the published rate sheet: haul, launch, blocking and months of storage, with 10% VAT and a total.
   - "Request this slip" or "Request this haul-out" sends a prefilled email with the boat and the estimate; "Call" and "VHF 16" sit beside it.
5. **Keep:**
   - **From their site:** every rate on the November 2024 sheet, the slip specs, fuel facts, store and boatyard hours, all three phones, VHF 16, the email, the rental cottages note.
   - **From our previous version:** the panorama of Hope Town Harbour from the lighthouse, the fit check, the haul-out and storage estimator, the full rate table on boatyard.html, and the calling-card contact block.
6. **Fix:**
   - A 2023 footer, a Gmail address, the default Wix favicon and title.
   - The "Dockage" button leads to boatyard rates; no dockage rates at all.
   - DIY is $40/day on one page and $50/day on another.
   - The liquor store is "reopening soon" in one place and listed as open in another.
   - Small blurred thumbnails and an all-caps headline.
7. **Screens:**
   1. The fuel dock under the lighthouse, "The only marine fuel on Elbow Cay", and the fit and cost tool with Request, Call and VHF 16.
   2. Fuel and the dock: stations, rebuilt docks, bait and ice, hours.
   3. The boatyard: haul-out, storage, service, Mercury, with the full estimator.
   4. The harbour from the lighthouse: the panorama as the place screen.
   5. The ship's store: what you can buy, hours, liquors when they reopen.
   6. Contact: the calling card with phones, VHF, email, enquiry by type (slip, fuel, haul and storage, mechanic, store), and all hours.
8. **Look and feel:**
   - **Mood:** a working harbour: precise, salty, trustworthy.
   - **Type:** one Swiss grotesk throughout, large numbers, tabular figures in the rates.
   - **Palette:** hull-paper ground, navy fields, brass accent.
   - **Photography:** their own dock and lighthouse photos plus Wikimedia Commons photos of Hope Town Harbour and the Elbow Reef Lighthouse (credited), never larger than the source.
   - **Wow:** a captain types three numbers on a phone and sees, under the lighthouse, whether the boat fits and exactly what the yard will cost.
