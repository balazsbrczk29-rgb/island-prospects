## Curated per niche
### Marina, yachts
- https://www.feadship.nl : one huge photograph per screen, tiny confident type
- https://www.wallyyachts.com : restraint, precise grid, product as hero
- https://www.yachtclubdemonaco.com : quiet luxury, generous space
### Hotel, resort, inn, lodge
- https://www.aman.com : photography first, almost no interface
- https://www.belmond.com : editorial storytelling, warm grade
- https://www.sixsenses.com : place-led, calm typography
- https://www.kinfolk.com : editorial rhythm for small inns
### Tours, dive, boats
- https://www.patagonia.com/stories : real place, real people, documentary photos
- https://www.visitfaroeislands.com : landscape-led destination storytelling
### Real estate
- https://www.theagencyre.com : big listings photography, clean search
- https://www.sothebysrealty.com : trust and listing detail pages
### Professional, finance, legal
- https://www.pictet.com : calm authority, architecture photography
- https://www.lombardodier.com : restrained type, lots of space
### Industrial, construction, trades
- https://www.arup.com : project photography at scale
- https://www.fosterandpartners.com : architecture photos, quiet grid
- https://www.rivian.com : product as hero, part-by-part storytelling (object study)
### Retail, shops
- https://www.aesop.com : product stills on one backdrop, refined catalogue
- https://www.hay.com : catalogue that feels like a magazine
- https://www.muji.com : calm, practical retail
### Food, events, weddings
- https://www.ottolenghi.co.uk : food photography as hero
- https://www.laduree.com : crafted, celebratory product pages
### Institutions
- https://www.gov.uk : task-first clarity
- https://www.visitfinland.com : warm, photo-led public information
