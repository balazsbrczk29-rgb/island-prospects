## Audit
Old 2015 photos over http; repetitive slider; prices hidden inside package pages; no email/WhatsApp; cruise purser rule buried; 20+ years hidden on About.

## Buyer portrait
US, Canadian and international couples (30-45, mid to upper-middle income) planning a Nassau wedding on a phone months ahead, many arriving by cruise ship for a single port day. They compare resort in-house coordinators and other Nassau planners. Fear: licence paperwork going wrong, a planner who stops answering, hidden costs. Feel safe with clear prices, a named experienced planner and a checklist.

## Strategy
Pages: index, packages. Primary CTA "Check your wedding date" (summary + call; no email sourced). Secondary "Call Anna". SEO primary "Nassau beach wedding planner"; secondary "Bahamas cruise wedding package", "wedding planner Nassau Bahamas", "Bahamas marriage licence requirements".
Positioning: For couples marrying in Nassau, Anna's is the independent planner with two decades of beach and cruise-day weddings and published prices from USD 950.

## Brand truth / feel
One experienced Bahamian planner, published prices, the paperwork handled. Feel: warm, romantic, assured, sunlit, personal.

## Reference sites
belmond.com and aman.com weddings pages (from the hospitality list, known structure): split editorial heroes with a single art-directed photo, serif display, generous white space, calm enquiry. Not scraped (budget).

## Design spec
Split-screen editorial hero, photo bleeding right with a 200 px arched corner, headline overlapping the image boundary. Gloock + Figtree. Palette paper #fbf8f5, blush #f5e6e1, ink #1f2a2e, rose #b44c6e (hibiscus, not orange/red), lagoon #8cc9c2, muted #625b58, line #eadfd8. Roman-numeral indexing. Interactions: package finder (arrival mode + guest slider), licence checklist with progress ring, tabbed date planner with read-out summary, package tabs on packages page.

### Big idea
The offer is the index: Anna's five kinds of wedding (beach, cruise day, cultural, deluxe, renewal) sit in a permanent left index like a bridal atelier's catalogue; touch one and her own photograph of that wedding fills the page, and the package finder turns "how do you arrive and who comes" into one published price.

### Direction
A02 Catalogue index + P02 Shallow flats + T09 Didone fashion (Bodoni Moda + Jost, self-hosted). Why: the index makes a buyer's real question (which package, what price) the structure of the page; Nassau's shallow water and sand are the colours of every photo she owns; a Didone is the type of bridal magazines, refined where Gloock was chunky. P03 Conch pink was refused because Bahamas Bridal Affairs (direct competitor) has it.
