## Audit
Old 2015 photos over http; repetitive slider; prices hidden inside package pages; no email/WhatsApp; cruise purser rule buried; 20+ years hidden on About.

## Buyer portrait
US, Canadian and international couples (30-45, mid to upper-middle income) planning a Nassau wedding on a phone months ahead, many arriving by cruise ship for a single port day. They compare resort in-house coordinators and other Nassau planners. Fear: licence paperwork going wrong, a planner who stops answering, hidden costs. Feel safe with clear prices, a named experienced planner and a checklist.

## Strategy
Pages: index, packages. Primary CTA "Check your wedding date" (summary + call; no email sourced). Secondary "Call Anna". SEO primary "Nassau beach wedding planner"; secondary "Bahamas cruise wedding package", "wedding planner Nassau Bahamas", "Bahamas marriage licence requirements".
Positioning: For couples marrying in Nassau, Anna's is the independent planner with two decades of beach and cruise-day weddings and published prices from USD 950.

## Brand truth / feel
One experienced Bahamian planner, published prices, the paperwork handled. Feel: warm, romantic, assured, sunlit, personal.

## Reference sites
