## Current site audit
1. A "COVID-19 Resort Information" banner still sits at the top of every page in 2026.
2. Expired offers are still on the site (a $100 pilot credit "by April 30, 2025", a "January 13, 2025" air credit), next to current ones, so guests can't tell what applies.
3. The homepage lists every room type twice and repeats the activity tiles three times.

5. The homepage title reads "GeorgeTown" and the resort goes by three different names across the site.
6. Splash menus are 2022 PDFs.

## Audience, buyer
Value-conscious US/Canadian couples and families, Bahamian residents on fly-free offers, private pilots, Europeans; on a phone comparing George Town options. Fear: dated rooms, confusing offers, getting from the airport. Choose well when they see which room fits, which deal applies to them, and that the airport is 15 minutes away.
