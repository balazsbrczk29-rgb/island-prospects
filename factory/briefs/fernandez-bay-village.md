## Audience and buyer portrait
US couples 40-70 from Florida and the Northeast, plus families and groups of friends renting the Manor House or Beach House, and private pilots (5,000 ft runway five minutes away, 15% pilot discount). Upper-middle income, want barefoot, quiet, no crowds; compare with Exuma and Eleuthera small resorts. Fear: hard to get to, nothing to eat nearby. Feel they chose well when the flight is simple, the cottage faces the sea and dinner is handled.

For couples, families and pilots who want a quiet Out Island beach, Fernandez Bay Village is the family-run cottage resort on a mile of white sand in Fernandez Bay, Cat Island, five minutes from New Bight airport, where every room has a beach view and dinner ends at a bonfire.
