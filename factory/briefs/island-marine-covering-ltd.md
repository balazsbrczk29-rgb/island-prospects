## Buyer portrait
Homeowners in western and eastern New Providence with verandahs and pool decks (upper-middle to high income), restaurant and hotel managers who need shade and weather screens, yacht and boat owners and their captains (Bimini tops, isinglass, upholstery), property managers before hurricane season. They compare with ordering from Florida. They fear: fabric that fades or tears in a season, shutters that fail in a storm, waiting months.
