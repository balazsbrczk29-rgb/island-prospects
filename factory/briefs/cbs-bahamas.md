## Audience / buyer portrait
Nassau homeowners and renovators (middle income, own their home, compare CBS with other Nassau hardware stores and with ordering from Florida), contractors buying in bulk, and Family Island homeowners (Exuma, Abaco, Eleuthera, Andros) who cannot drive to Carmichael Road. They fear: wasted trips, items out of stock, freight surprises, damage on the boat. They feel they chose well when: they know it is in stock, the price is matched, and they get photos and a signed proof at the boat.

## Primary conversion
"Shop CBS online" (hands off to their store). Secondary: Call the Home Center (tel), and the "Ask the store" email composer.

## Design spec
- Type: Bricolage Grotesque 700/500 (display) + Figtree 400/500/600 (body). Type scale contrast: hero 16vw monument, 12px tracked labels.
- Palette: --paper #eeefe9 (limestone), --paper-2 #e3e5dc, --ink #17201d, --pine #245a41 (louvred shutter green), --pine-deep #173d2c, --tape #e2b23a (tape-measure yellow, under 5%), --sea #d4e4df (chart water), --muted #56605b, --line #cfd3c8. Ink on paper 15:1; muted on paper 6:1.
- Hero: typographic monument "Build beautiful." (their own #buildbeautiful), with a louvred hurricane shutter inset whose slats open on load. Key-info band in pine.
- Signature visuals: "This shows a Bahamian house elevation because the brief says CBS stocks every part of a home: doors and windows, roofing, lighting and fans, bath, lawn and garden." "This shows an island chart with mailboat lines from Potter's Cay because the brief says CBS drops every order at the mailboat for free."
- Interactions: (1) Shop the house: tap a part of the elevation, see the department and go to that shop category. (2) Mailboat planner: choose your island, the route draws, the real 5 steps fill with your island. (3) Ask-the-store tabs composing an email.
- Motion: line-split hero, shutter slats, SVG line-draw, Lenis, hover lifts, scroll progress line.
