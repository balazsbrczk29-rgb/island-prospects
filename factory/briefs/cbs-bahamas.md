## Audience / buyer portrait
Nassau homeowners and renovators (middle income, own their home, compare CBS with other Nassau hardware stores and with ordering from Florida), contractors buying in bulk, and Family Island homeowners (Exuma, Abaco, Eleuthera, Andros) who cannot drive to Carmichael Road. They fear: wasted trips, items out of stock, freight surprises, damage on the boat. They feel they chose well when: they know it is in stock, the price is matched, and they get photos and a signed proof at the boat.

## Primary conversion
"Shop CBS online" (hands off to their store). Secondary: Call the Home Center (tel), and the "Ask the store" email composer.

For homeowners and builders across The Bahamas who need the right part without the wasted trip, CBS Bahamas is the Bahamian-owned home improvement store on Carmichael Road that has stocked Nassau since 1973 and ships to every Family Island by mailboat for free.

A Bahamian-owned store on Carmichael Road that has built Nassau since 1973 and now sends every order to the boat for free. Feel: practical, generous, precise, island-wide, proud.

A fixed left index column lists seven photographed departments (Roofing, Doors and windows, Building materials, Paint and walls, Flooring, Fans and lighting, Lawn and garden) plus "also on the shelves" text links; hovering or tapping a department swaps the photo grid on the right (own photos, product cut-outs on zinc plates, generated trade photos) with plain spec text under each item (name, use, size).
