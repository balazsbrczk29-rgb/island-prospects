# CBS Bahamas (Commonwealth Building Supplies) — brief

## Facts
- Founded March 1973 by the late building contractor Carl G. Treco with six staff and two major distributorships. https://www.lbmjournal.com/industry-news/news/15784639/commonwealth-building-supplies-ltd-announces-key-leadership-promotions
- Began as "a little store on Robinson Road"; moved to the flagship 30,000 sq ft Home Center, Southwest Plaza, Carmichael Road. lbmjournal + https://www.tribune242.com/news/2014/oct/16/cbs-bahamas-expands-with-brand-showroom + https://www.bahamaslocal.com/showlisting/17519/CBS_Bahamas.html
- Group: CBS Bahamas, CBS PROstore (bulk building materials for contractors), Aluminum Fabricators Ltd. lbmjournal
- Kohler showroom opened Oct 2014 at the Home Centre, official Kohler distributor (Kohler and Sterling); special project rates for designers, renovators, builders. tribune242
- Brands: PGT, JELD-WEN, Kawneer, Huttig, Kohler, U.S. Cabinet (Depot), Valspar, Mohawk (bahamaslocal); Milwaukee Tools, Boise Cascade, USG, Choice Flooring (lbmjournal)
- Departments: Lawn & Garden, Cleaning Supplies, Housewares, Electrical, Plumbing, Windows & Doors, Tools; categories incl. hurricane shutters, impact doors, water heaters, cabinets. bahamaslocal
- BHTA: "Leading suppliers of doors, PTG windows, flooring, locks, tools and other building supplies". https://www.bhahotels.com/members-directory/commonwealth-building-supplies-ltd/
- Phone corporate (242) 677-2100 (cbsbahamas.com/contact-us--6 via WebFetch + bahamaslocal). Alt 242-325-2505 (BHTA).
- P.O. Box SS-6268, Nassau. bahamaslocal
- Socials: facebook.com/BuildBeautiful, instagram.com/cbsbahamas, youtube.com/user/CBSBahamas, twitter.com/CBSBahamas. bahamaslocal
- Hours conflict (bahamaslocal Mon-Sat 8-8, Sun 9-5; 2014 article different). Omitted; "call to confirm".

## Current site audit
1. The contact page tells customers to "email us at" but the email address is missing.
2. Directories list two different main numbers (677-2100 and 325-2505); customers do not know which to call.
3. Store hours differ between listings; nothing says clearly when the Home Center is open.
4. No quick way to ask "do you have it in stock?" from a phone (no WhatsApp, no stock question path).
5. The 50+ year story (Robinson Road, 1973) and the PROstore for contractors are nearly invisible.

## Audience
Nassau homeowners and renovators, contractors (PROstore), designers; phones; often "do they have it / are they open / where".

## Brand truth
A building contractor's little Robinson Road store from 1973 that grew into the 30,000 sq ft Home Center on Carmichael Road, built around storm-ready windows and doors.
Feel: solid, practical, storm-ready, Bahamian, generous.

## Design
Bricolage Grotesque 600/700 + Figtree 400/500. Palette: --paper #f4f4f0, --ink #1c2422, --accent #2f5d3a (louvred shutter green), --accent-2 #e3b23c (safety yellow, tiny), --muted #555e5a, --line #d4d6cf, --stone #e4e1d6.
Hero: typographic-only (huge "Building Nassau since 1973." + spec strip). Signature: a hurricane shutter of louvres that closes as you scroll into the "storm-ready" section. Motion: rise-in hero, shutter scrub, counter-real (years since 1973).
