## Facts (all https://www.grandbahamavacations.com/ and inner pages, Nov 2026)
- Grand Bahama Island is "just 55 miles east of Florida". Home page.
- Getting here: by air ("on the beach by noon"), cruise from (West) Palm Beach (overnight cruise), Fast Ferry from Fort Lauderdale, cruise port of call, private boat or charter. /getting-here/
- Things to do categories: Beaches, Water Activities, Nightlife, Nature & Eco-tours, Dining & Culinary, Couples/Spa & Wellness, Festivals & Events, Golf, Shopping, Fishing, Tennis. /things-to-do/
- Top 5 traveller favourites: Lucayan National Park (Lucayan Caverns, over six miles of caves and charted tunnels, one of the longest underwater cave systems in the world; Gold Rock Beach); UNEXSO (dolphin sanctuary at Sanctuary Bay, swim with dolphins in a natural lagoon); Garden of the Groves (native/exotic plants, waterfalls, replica of Freeport's first chapel built by founder Mr. Groves, the only labyrinth in The Bahamas, Garden Cafe); West End Ecology Tours (Stingray, Coral Reef, Reef Fishing experiences; West End history of 1930s bootleggers and sport fishermen); Smith's Point Fish Fry (fried snapper, lobster, potato bread, conch salad, rum punch, Bahamian music). /things-to-do/
- Hot deals page, destination guide download (/our-guide/), e-club newsletter, Balearia ferry + hotel deals link.

## Audit
1. The homepage still links to "COVID-19 travel entry requirements".
2. The "Book Now" panel lists departure airports from Anchorage to Honolulu but only two resorts; the sign-up box shows "Operation timed out".
3. A homepage link reads "Learn more about" with nothing after it; a tile reads "Grand Bahama Island Open".
4. The core pages were last edited in 2022; three "CLICK HERE TO READ" flip-books lead to an outside site.
5. The 55-miles-from-Florida advantage is a line of body copy instead of the headline.

## Buyer portrait
Florida and US South-East residents (South Florida weekenders, families, divers), plus cruise and ferry passengers, planning on a phone; they compare with Nassau, Bimini, Keys; fear: hassle getting there, nothing to do; delighted by proximity, nature and authenticity.

## Primary conversion
"Plan your trip": trip planner (how you are coming + favourites) -> links to Getting here, Hot deals, destination guide.

## Positioning
For Floridians who want an island without the long trip, Grand Bahama is the Bahamas island 55 miles away, with caves, dolphins, eco-tours and a fish fry you cannot get at home.
