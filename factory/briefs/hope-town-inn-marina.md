## Audit
Out-of-service main number still first everywhere; 30+ menu links; no party-size filter across 9 stays; 120 vs 125 ft conflict; 2021/2022 Covid badges; generic copy ("Welcome to", "Paradise on earth", "Nestled").

## Buyer portrait
US East Coast couples and families (upper-middle to affluent, 35 to 65) planning an Abaco trip on a phone months ahead and comparing with OTAs; yacht and sportfish owners and captains crossing from Florida checking slip limits and power. They fear a hard-to-reach out-island property and a dead phone line; they feel they chose well when they see the exact harbour, the lighthouse, the room that fits their party, and a working booking path.

## Strategy
Pages: index, stay, marina. Primary CTA "Check room rates" (booking engine); secondary "Book a slip" (Dockwa). SEO primary "Hope Town hotel and marina"; secondary "Elbow Cay hotel", "Hope Town marina slips", "Abaco resort with marina", "where to stay Hope Town Abaco".
