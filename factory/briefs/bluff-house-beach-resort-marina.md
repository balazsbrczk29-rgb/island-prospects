# Bluff House Beach Resort & Marina (White Sound, Green Turtle Cay, Abaco) : Track A (bluffhouse.com, Wix)

## Facts (bluffhouse.com home, /marina-rates, /a-little-history)
- 12 tropical acres of palms and pines from White Sound to the Sea of Abaco, own white-sand beach; "the first and now the oldest resort in the Bahamas' Out Islands".
- 1-4 bedroom accommodations; eight air-conditioned hotel suites, each with a balcony and sunset/moonrise views of the Sea of Abaco; "the only private beach on Green Turtle Cay". Cottages and villas: Summerset, La Conchita, Turtle Bluff Villa, Pineapple House Villa, Sweet Guava Villa, Blue Bay Cottage, Dove Cottage, Coral Haven, Sunset Cottage 4.
- Marina: 40 slips; channel from the Sea of Abaco into White Sound 7' MLW (dredged); each boat its own dock space and finger pier with power, water and free Wi-Fi; 12,000+ sq ft of decking; 50/100 amp shore power; showers and laundry; dockmaster to meet and assist; ferry pick-up and drop-off at the dock; golf cart rental assistance. Reserve a slip on Dockwa: https://dockwa.com/explore/destination/wgcegn-bluff-house-resort-and-marina
- Published marina rates (labelled 2022, dated 2024-2025 seasons): $2.00-$3.50/ft/day by season, weekly $1.75-$2.25, slips 6 & 19 and T-head premiums, 30 ft minimum, +10% VAT; closed for maintenance Sept to late Oct. Electricity $.85/kWh, water $.35/gal.
- Dining: The Boathouse Bar & Restaurant (breakfast and dinner six days a week, harborside deck or air-conditioned room); Tranquil Turtle Beach Bar (beach, volleyball, kayaks), "voted #6 of the Top 25 Beach Bars in the Caribbean" (Caribbean Journal); Low Tide Gift Shop; weddings.
- History: early 1950s private home of Pearce and Kitty Coady, who hosted boaters; Davies family 1978-1986; Martin Havill 1986-1995; Roger and Susan Phillips 1995-2011; investors led by Ian Phillips 2011; December 2025 acquired by VIP Marinas Group (Austin and Jenny Cameron, Felecia and Dan Ongley), its first Bahamas property.
- Recognition (own site): TripAdvisor Certificate of Excellence Hall of Fame 2018; GM Molly McIntosh, Caribbean Journal Hotelier of the Year 2018.
- Contact: frontdesk@bluffhouse.com; molly@bluffhouse.com; +1 (954) 284-7341 (US/office); +1 (242) 824-9550 (local, WhatsApp); restaurants +1 (242) 821-7267.
