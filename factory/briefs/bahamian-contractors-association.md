## Facts (source on each line; all https://www.bahamiancontractors.org/ unless noted)
- "The Voice of Bahamian Contractors"; "Uniting Contractors, Raising Standards, Building the Future."
- Established 1959 (own site). ZNS says 1958: https://znsbahamas.com/newly-elected-bahamas-contractors-association-bca-executive-board/ (conflict, own site used).
- Advocacy: official voice of the construction industry; EPA negotiations with the EU, Caricom and others; drove the Construction Contractors Act 2016 and advocates for appointment of the Construction Contractors Board.
- Education: training seminars, certification, Contractor Seminar Series, standardized testing and licensing support, awards.
- Growth: bid invitations (government and private), promotion to international companies, monthly luncheons and events, industry reports.
- Member extras: certificate of membership, Contractor Register listing, trade discounts, free estimate printing, marketing support, social promotion.
- Membership: open to Bahamian citizens or residents with the right to work. Associate $250/yr (suppliers, manufacturers, consultants, PMs, professionals); General $300/yr (general, specialty, subcontractors); Prime $1,000/yr (contractors with annual revenue $5M+); Developer/Corporate $5,000/yr (developers with turnover $10M+).

## Buyer portrait
Bahamian contractors (from one-truck subcontractors to $5M+ firms), suppliers and developers; mostly on phones; also government officials and foreign developers checking who represents the industry. Fear: paying dues for nothing, being shut out of big projects by foreign firms. Chose well when: they see bids, training and real advocacy wins.

## Primary conversion
"Find your membership" (tier finder that writes an email to admin@). Secondary: call the office; RSVP to the next event.
