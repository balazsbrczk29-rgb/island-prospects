## Buyer portrait
Bahamian contractors (from one-truck subcontractors to $5M+ firms), suppliers and developers; mostly on phones; also government officials and foreign developers checking who represents the industry. Fear: paying dues for nothing, being shut out of big projects by foreign firms. Chose well when: they see bids, training and real advocacy wins.

## Primary conversion
"Find your membership" (tier finder that writes an email to admin@). Secondary: call the office; RSVP to the next event.
