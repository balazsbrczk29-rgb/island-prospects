# Orange Creek Inn: brief (Track A)
All facts from https://www.orangecreekinn.com/ (one-page WordPress site with #!/ sections), scraped 2026-11-05.

## Facts
- Orange Creek Inn, Orange Creek, Cat Island, immediately north of Arthur's Town. Tel 1 242 354 4110. info@orangecreekinn.com, orangecreekinn242@gmail.com. Facebook page, TripAdvisor listing.
- Two-story building across the road from a white-sand beach (FAQ "directly across from a beautiful white-sand beach").
- Spacious studio accommodations with stove-top kitchenette, toaster and coffee maker, dining table, TV and internet, AC and ceiling fan, alarm clock/radio, iron; laundry and ice machine; on-site convenience/grocery store; car rental; airport pick-up.
- Activities: saltwater fishing, bonefishing flats nearby, kayaking, bicycling, straw markets, cultural performances, sightseeing, boat rides; nearby Blue Hole, Port Royal Beach, Fernandez Bay; about 6 nearby restaurants of Bahamian food, $12-18 average meal.
- Getting here: Arthur's Town Airport ~3.5 miles, taxi about $15; Western Air from Nassau twice daily (arrives 9:30am and 4pm), about $190 round trip; Makers Air FXE-CAT Mon/Wed/Fri/Sat 6:45am and 12:45pm, CAT-FXE 10am, charters available. Car ~ $80/day, bicycles $10/day.
- Payment Visa and MasterCard, or cash on arrival. Best months Dec-April (their FAQ).
- Specials (Bahama Out Islands Promotion Board programme): 4+ nights $300 fee credit; pre-booked 2 nights $150 fee credit; fly/ferry free from Nassau (1 ticket 2 nights / 2 tickets 4+ nights, booking Jul 1 2026-Jun 30 2027, travel to Oct 31 2027, blackouts); 4-6 nights one free R/T ticket from Nassau, 7 nights two; Florida residents $250 air/ferry credit 3+ nights (book by Nov 9 2026); 7+ nights $500 air credit (book Oct 12-Nov 9 2026). ## Current site audit
1. Everything sits on one long page with #!/ links, so Google sees one page and nobody can link to the rooms or the FAQ.
2. The headline reads "Welcome to Orange Creek Inn" and the copy leans on "nestled", without the one fact that sells you: beach across the road at the top of Cat Island.
3. Eight specials run as a slideshow wall of terms; a guest cannot tell which one fits their trip.
4. Flight times and costs, your most useful content, are buried at the bottom of 25 FAQs.
5. Book Now leaves your site for a third-party engine with no explanation.

## Audience / conversion
US East Coast and Florida couples, families, anglers, eco travellers, Bahamian visitors; budget-aware, planning by phone. Primary: Check dates (bahamago engine). Secondary: call / email.

## Buyer portrait
Value-minded travellers 30-65, anglers and families, often returning Bahamians; compare with Fernandez Bay Village and Airbnbs; fear complicated flights and hidden costs. Chose well when getting there is clear and the deal is obvious.

## Brand truth / feel
A simple, friendly inn at the quiet top of Cat Island, with a grocery downstairs and the beach across the road. Feel: easygoing, sunlit, practical, local, unhurried.

## Positioning
For travellers who want an affordable, self-catering base at the uncrowded north end of Cat Island, Orange Creek Inn is the beach-across-the-road inn with its own store, car rental and airport pickup, 3.5 miles from Arthur's Town Airport.
