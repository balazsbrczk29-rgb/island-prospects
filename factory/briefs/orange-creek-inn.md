# Orange Creek Inn: brief (Track A, generation 2 rebuild, 2026-11-20)
All facts from https://www.orangecreekinn.com/ (one-page WordPress site with #!/ sections), re-scraped 2026-11-20, plus check-in and check-out times from their booking engine page https://www.bahamago.com/hotels/22-Orange-Creek-Inn?ref=oipb.

## Facts
- Orange Creek Inn (spelling on their logo), Orange Creek, Cat Island, immediately north of Arthur's Town. Tel 1 242 354 4110. info@orangecreekinn.com. Facebook page, TripAdvisor listing.
- Two-storey building, directly across the road from a white-sand beach (FAQ). A creek beside it (guest reviews on their site: turtles, stingrays, bonefish).
- Spacious studio accommodations: stove-top kitchenette, toaster and coffee maker, dining table and chairs, television and internet, air conditioning and ceiling fan, alarm clock/radio, iron and ironing board; laundry facilities and ice machine; grocery store on site; car rental; airport pick-up.
- Check-in 2:00 PM, check-out 12:00 PM (BahamaGo page). No rates published anywhere: "Rates on request".
- Activities listed: saltwater fishing, bonefishing flats nearby, kayaking, bicycling, straw markets, cultural performances, sightseeing tours, boat rides; nearby Blue Hole, Port Royal Beach, Fernandez Bay; about 6 nearby restaurants serving Bahamian food, $12 to $18 an average meal.
- Getting here: most guests fly into Nassau, then a domestic flight to Arthur's Town Airport, about 3.5 miles from the inn; taxi about $15; the inn offers airport pick-up. Western Air from Nassau twice a day (arrives 9:30am and 4pm), about $190 round trip. Makers Air from Fort Lauderdale Executive (FXE) Mon/Wed/Fri/Sat, departs 6:45am and 12:45pm, returns 10am; private charters. Car about $80 a day (can be waiting at the airport), bicycles $10 a day.
- Payment: Visa and MasterCard, or cash on arrival. Most popular months December to April.
- Specials as published 2026-11-20 (Bahama Out Islands Promotion Board programme):
  1. 4 nights or longer: $300 fee credit. Book Jul 1 2026 to Jun 30 2027, travel Jul 5 2026 to Oct 31 2027.
  2. Pre-booked 2 nights: $150 fee credit. Same windows.
  3. Fly free from Nassau: one free airline ticket for a pre-booked 2-night stay, two for 4 nights or longer. Same windows.
  4. Air-inclusive 4 to 6 nights: one free round-trip ticket from Nassau; 7 nights: two. Book Jul 1 2026 to Jun 30 2027, travel Jul 2 2026 to Oct 31 2027, no blackouts.
  5. From Florida, air or ferry inclusive, 4 nights or longer: up to $250 air/ferry credit. Book Nov 1 2026 to Jan 31 2027, travel Nov 2 2026 to Oct 31 2027.
  6. Residents of Europe and Latin America (Argentina, Brazil, Colombia, Mexico): 4 to 6 nights one free ticket from Nassau, 7 nights two (double occupancy).
  Blackouts (1, 2, 3, 5): Nov 26 to 28 2026; Dec 19 2026 to Jan 1 2027; Mar 25 to 28 2027; Jul 1 to 4 2027. (6: the first two only.)
- Guest reviews shown on their own site name the hosts as the Cleares and Sharee/Sheree. Not used as a signature (to confirm with the owner); one sourced guest line may be quoted with its source.

## Audit (2026-11-20)
Brochure one-pager with an external Book Now link (BahamaGo). Weaknesses: one page of #!/ anchors; "Welcome to Orange Creek Inn" headline; six specials in a slider of small print; flights and prices under 27 FAQs; no room description beyond icons; photos shown as 520 px square crops.

## Buyer
Value-minded couples, families and anglers from Florida and the US East Coast, and Bahamians visiting the north of Cat Island. Planning on a phone. They compare with Fernandez Bay Village and Airbnb. They fear complicated flights and hidden costs.

## Brand truth
A small family-run inn at the top of Cat Island where the only thing between your door and the beach is the road, with its own grocery store, laundry and cars.

## Big idea (one sentence)
The site is a letter from Orange Creek, written on the inn's own notepaper: you open it on a stack of their photographs that you can deal through like prints from a drawer, and as you read down, each print is laid on the page beside the paragraph it belongs to, ending in a reply card that works out which published offer fits your dates.

## Direction
A07 Letter and stationery + P12 Inagua salt + T08 Warm humanist (Gloock + Nunito Sans). Nav N8 side ribbon tab, motion M6 spring physics on the prints.
Why: a family inn with 900 px photographs should not pretend to be a cinematic resort. Prints at print size on a letter make the small photos a virtue, and nobody else on Cat Island in this repo is a letter (Greenwood and Pigeon Cay are cinematic chapters, Fernandez Bay a horizontal journey). Salt white with flamingo pink picks up the bougainvillea in front of the inn.
No tape, no handwriting, no rotated stickers: prints are white-bordered, lie straight at rest and only tilt while they are being moved.
