Brand truth: Pauline and Antoine's small beachfront resort at the south end of Cat Island, where the diving and kitesurfing start at the beach. Feel: barefoot, remote, salt-washed, personal, unhurried.
Positioning: For divers, kiters and couples who want a remote Out Island beach, Greenwood is the hosted beachfront resort at Port Howe where the water is the main event.
