## Strategy
Pages: index, rooms, getting-here. CTA "Check dates and book". SEO primary "beach resort Cat Island Bahamas"; secondary "Port Howe Cat Island hotel", "Cat Island diving resort", "kitesurfing Cat Island", "how to get to Cat Island".
Brand truth: Pauline and Antoine's small beachfront resort at the south end of Cat Island, where the diving and kitesurfing start at the beach. Feel: barefoot, remote, salt-washed, personal, unhurried.
Positioning: For divers, kiters and couples who want a remote Out Island beach, Greenwood is the hosted beachfront resort at Port Howe where the water is the main event.

## Big idea (rebuild 2026-11-20)
One day at Port Howe, shown as a letterboxed film: the hosts' beach, the kite wind, the deep blue where oceanic whitetips arrive from March to June, the reef and the boat-only bat cave, dinner on the terrace from six, then the room; the end credits are the real route from Fort Lauderdale or Nassau to New Bight.

## Direction
A03 Cinematic chapters + P01 Limestone + T01 Monumental serif (Noto Serif Display 300 + Source Serif 4). Why: the resort's best asset is its photography of the water (Pauline Vaz Branco's whitetip and kite shots), and a film grammar lets each photo own the screen; limestone bars keep every line of type on a light ground (no overlays on bright sand and sky). Two serifs because the buyer reads slowly, months ahead, and the page should feel like a printed film programme, not an app. Differs from any Cat Island competitor (none built with A03/P01).
Ground #EFEAE0, ink #1B1A17, sea glass #2F6F73 (CTA and the whitetip field), sand #B89B6A (hairlines only).
Tier: standard (no real-time 3D): the product is a beach and the water, and their own photographs carry it; no 3D model would explain it better.
