## Audit (for the Commission)
No own website; outdated directory numbers; email domain without website; guidance scattered in Facebook posts; no structured way to submit a complete complaint.

## Audience / buyer portrait
Bahamian consumers (all ages, many on older phones, often upset) who were let down by a shop, repair or service; they fear being ignored or sent between offices. They feel reassured by plain steps, a human channel (WhatsApp), and knowing what happens next. Buyer of the site: the Commission's executive leadership / Ministry comms.

## Strategy
Pages: index.html + file-a-complaint.html. Primary CTA "File a complaint"; secondary WhatsApp us, Call. Journey: Google "file consumer complaint Bahamas" → understand the 3 steps + what CPC does → builder → WhatsApp send.
SEO primary "consumer complaint Bahamas"; secondary "Consumer Protection Commission Bahamas contact", "how to file a complaint against a business Nassau", "consumer rights Bahamas refund", "CPC Bahamas WhatsApp".
Positioning: For Bahamian consumers who were treated unfairly by a business, the Commission is the statutory body that will log, review and take the complaint to the business, and keep them updated.

## Brand truth / feel
The public's referee when a business will not put things right. Feel: calm, fair, plain, warm, official.

## Design spec
Reference: gov.uk (task-first clarity, plain language, big tap targets). Fonts Fraunces 600 + Atkinson Hyperlegible 400/700. Palette paper #f7f6f1, card #ffffff, ink #14181b, muted #50585c, line #d8d4c8, aqua #00707a (from the national aquamarine, darkened for 5.6:1 contrast), aqua-soft #d9ecea, gold #ffc72c (national gold, highlight and WhatsApp button only).
Hero: task-first split, headline with gold marker, aqua panel with the signature receipt.
Signature: "This shows a shop receipt stamped LOGGED, REVIEWED, BUSINESS CONTACTED, UPDATE SENT because the Commission's own post says 'we log it, review it, reach out to the business, and update you along the way'". Sticky scrollytelling re-stamps the receipt as each stage scrolls past.
