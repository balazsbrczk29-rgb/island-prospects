# Firefly Sunset Resort (id 71, batch 11, Tier A, Track A)

## Facts
- Five cottages and villas on the Sea of Abaco, Hope Town, Elbow Cay: Mint To Be, Butterfly, Dragonfly, Firefly, Java Rum Plantation House. Opened quietly late 2010, grand opening 2011. https://www.fireflysunsetresort.com
- Mint To Be: "romantic honeymoon suite with private plunge pool", on the edge of a coral cliff almost suspended over the Sea of Abaco (homepage); villas page lists it as 2B/2B with private back deck facing the sunset. https://www.fireflysunsetresort.com/rooms
- Butterfly, Dragonfly, Firefly: 2B/2B, modern tropical interiors, elevated views over the Sea of Abaco, each bedroom with adjoining full bath. Java Rum Plantation: 3B/2B, expansive wooden decks, French doors, steps above the beach, Brazilian wood, named after their Sea Island Java Rum.
- Hurricane Dorian (Sept 1, 2019, 185 mph): lost two 4b/4b villas (Sweet Tea, Georgia Peach); dock, outside bar and dining deck rebuilt; power restored.
- General Manager Tom Hazel. Email info@fireflysunsetresort.com. https://www.fireflysunsetresort.com
- Firefly Bar & Grill on site, open to locals and visitors 7 days for breakfast, lunch and dinner. Breakfast 8:00am-11:00am, Bahamian breakfast on Sundays, no reservations needed; lunch 11:30am-4:00pm; dinner 5:30pm-9:00pm (kitchen closes 9pm). To-go orders placed in The Boutique by 8pm. https://www.fireflysunsetresort.com , /empty-page
- Dinner reservations: https://firefly-sunset-resort.tablein.com/ ; address Centre Line Rd, Elbow Cay; parties of 12+ must call 1 (242) 366-0145. (Tablein listing, search snippet)
- Amenities: resort boutique, private beach, two pools (upper pool villa guests only), dock slips, on-site artificial reef for snorkelling (stingrays, turtles, sharks seen), corn hole on the beach, complimentary kayaks. Pet friendly. https://www.fireflysunsetresort.com/our-services
- Concierge: golf cart and boat rentals arranged (cart delivered to villa, boats meet you in Marsh Harbour or at the dock), charters and excursions (fishing, paddle board, conch pulling, scuba, sailing, island tours), pre-stocked provisions, gift baskets, florals, massage/manicure visits.
- Rates (rooms page, not incl. 10% VAT): In season Feb-Aug, 3-night minimum: Java Rum $1,500/day, $10,200/week; Mint To Be $1,125/$7,650; Dragonfly, Butterfly, Firefly $800/$5,440. Off season Sep-Jan (excl. Thanksgiving, Christmas, New Year): Java Rum $1,350/$9,180; Mint To Be $1,000/$6,800; the three 2B villas $665/$4,525. Whole resort: week $49,258, 3 days $21,731 (incl. VAT). Booking: find dates on calendar, then email villa name, dates and number of guests.
- Getting here (FAQ): fly to Marsh Harbour, 15 min taxi to Albury's or G&L ferry dock, 20 min ferry to Hope Town, resort shuttle from Hope Town harbour. Hope Town 4 miles off Abaco; Abaco under 180 miles east of Florida. Elbow Reef Lighthouse 1862, 89 ft. Golf carts; drive on the left. Payment: BSD/USD cash, Visa, MasterCard, Amex, Discover. Phone lines can go down with island power: email then. https://www.fireflysunsetresort.com/faqs
- Instagram bio snippet: "closing August 24 & reopening ..." (seasonal closure, dates unconfirmed).

## Audience, buyer
US families and couples (Southeast US, Northeast) 35-65, upper-middle to affluent, boaters who rent a boat for the week, honeymooners, groups booking the whole resort; plus Hope Town visitors and locals for the Bar & Grill. On a phone, comparing with Hope Town Inn & Marina and rentals. Fear: complicated ferry logistics, hidden VAT, villa not right size. Chooses well when price with VAT is clear, the route is simple, the view is real.
