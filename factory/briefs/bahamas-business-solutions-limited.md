## Audience / buyer portrait
Office managers, procurement and IT leads at government agencies, banks, utilities, clinics, hotels and mid-sized firms in Grand Bahama and New Providence; plus walk-in print customers (architects, contractors, event organisers). They compare BBSL with ordering online from Florida or a local reseller without a service team. They fear downtime with no local engineer. They feel they chose well when a known local team with Xerox/NCR authority and a fast helpdesk stands behind the machine. Desktop at work for procurement, phone for service calls.

## Strategy
Pages: index.html + document-centre.html. Primary CTA "Request a quote" (tabbed: equipment → sales@, service call → service@, print job → copyservice@). Secondary: "Book a service call", calls to Freeport/Nassau.
Journey: Google "Xerox dealer Freeport" → hero says Xerox/NCR + both islands + since 1966 → quote tab or call.
SEO primary "Xerox dealer Bahamas"; secondary "copier service Freeport Grand Bahama", "printer repair Nassau", "NCR ATM service Bahamas", "printing services Freeport", "managed print Bahamas".
Positioning: For offices across The Bahamas that cannot afford a dead printer or ATM, BBSL is the 100% Bahamian, Xerox-authorized technology company that sells and services world brands from Nassau and Freeport, since 1966.

## Brand truth and feel
The Bahamas' Xerox company since 1966: sixty years in the document business, with its own engineers on two islands. Feel: precise, dependable, heritage, crafted, calm.

- Fonts: Instrument Serif 400/italic (display) + Geist 400/500/600 (body) + Geist Mono (labels).
- Palette: paper #f2f0ea, paper-2 #e7e3d8, sheet #fbfaf6, ink #121417, graphite #23272c, muted #5b5f63, line #d3cec2, process cyan #0a6d8c (accent), toner yellow #e3b505 (labels on dark only).
- Hero pattern: "print-proof" product/craft close-up: full-viewport paper hero with crop marks, colour bar, slug line; line-split headline (their own tagline) left; procedural R3F fanned stack of six documents bleeding off the right edge.
