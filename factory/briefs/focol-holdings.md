# FOCOL Holdings: brief

Track A. Record said no own site; research found focol.com (rebuilt in 2026 on WordPress/Elementor). Large enterprise, BISX-listed: one-page concept.

## Facts (source)
- Largest integrated energy company in The Bahamas; over 50 years; sole licensed Shell distributor for The Bahamas and Turks and Caicos Islands. https://www.focol.com/ , https://www.focol.com/company/freeport-oil-company/
- Business: importation, distribution and retail of petroleum products (gasoline, diesel, aviation fuels), power generation, energy infrastructure; Bahamas and Turks and Caicos. https://www.focol.com/who-we-are/
- Subsidiaries and roles: Sun Oil Ltd (gasoline, diesel, marine, aviation fuel); Freeport Oil Company (established 1967 by Grand Bahama Port Authority); Bahamas Utilities Company Ltd (utilities, power generation support, New Providence); New Providence Gas Ltd (LNG regas terminal at Clifton Pier); Bahamas Solar & Renewables (utility-scale, C&I, micro-grid, distributed); Convenience Retailing Limited (New Providence stations; ORSL since 2007, CRL incorporated 1 Oct 2021); Grand Sun Investments (Freeport/Grand Bahama stations, convenience stores, commercial fueling). https://www.focol.com/
- NPG: JV of Sun Oil Holdings (60%) and Shell Bahamas Power Company (40%); terminal moving from plans to construction; LNG by marine vessel, Shell supplies LNG from the US. https://www.focol.com/a-new-energy-milestone-for-the-bahamas/ (Sept 7, 2026)
- Contacts: head office Clifton Pier, Nassau, +1 (242) 702-3000, info@focol.com; Freeport Oil +1 (242) 352-8131, Lucayan Harbour Area, Freeport; CRL +1 (242) 362-6673/4, Old Fort Bay; GSI (242) 351-3104, East Mall Drive. https://www.focol.com/contact/

## Current site audit
Slider "Read More" links to "#"; stock Depositphotos hero; "Who We Are?" plus loose value headings and empty "Our Vision"; contact page has conflicting robots tags incl. noindex and the typo "Grand Bahamas"; seven companies but no routing for customers; BISX tile shows "0—".

## Buyer portrait
Commercial fuel buyers (marine, aviation, industrial, contractors), government and utility partners, BISX investors, job seekers, journalists. They compare FOCOL with Rubis and Esso/Sol distributors; they fear calling the wrong entity and wasted time; they feel right when the group looks as large, disciplined and modern as its LNG project.

## Strategy
Single page. Primary CTA "Find the right company" (routing selector), secondary "Call head office". SEO primary "fuel distributor Bahamas"; secondary "Shell distributor Bahamas", "Freeport Oil Company", "LNG Clifton Pier", "solar company Bahamas".
Positioning: For Bahamian businesses, partners and investors who need dependable energy, FOCOL is the one group that runs the whole chain, from tanker to service station.

## Brand truth / feel
Brand truth: one Bahamian group runs every link of the country's energy chain. Feel: precise, engineered, calm, national, modern.

## Reference sites
arup.com (editorial large type, technical restraint, numbered narrative), maersk.com (full-bleed operational imagery, clean corporate grid, strong single blue). Not scraped (budget); studied from known structure.

## Design spec
Hero: typographic monument + full-width technical elevation drawing (ship, tanks, LNG regas, power station, pylon, solar, service station) drawing itself on load. "This shows the energy chain because the brief says FOCOL's subsidiaries cover import, storage, LNG, power, solar and retail." Sticky scrollytelling reuses the drawing and highlights each link. Fonts Schibsted Grotesk 500-700 + Public Sans. Palette: paper #f2f4f3, paper-2 #e6eae9, ink #0c1b2a, blue #1c7fb0 (echoes their #3ABDFF), sky #8fd3f4, muted #4f5d68, line #d3dad9. Interactions: scroll story with clickable steps, company router, island tabs with live maps, tabbed enquiry to info@focol.com.
