## Buyer portrait
Couples in the US and Canada, 28-45, household income comfortably above average, planning 9-18 months ahead on a phone, often with a parent paying. They compare planners on Instagram and The Knot, and with in-house resort coordinators. Fear: a planner they have never met, vendor no-shows, rain, hidden fees. They chose well when: the planner has done it at the exact resort they love, has been published, and answers personally.
