## Audience
Cruising sailors and charter catamaran crews (Navigare, Dream Yacht), US East Coast powerboat owners crossing to Abaco, on a phone, often on the boat. Second: guests flying into Marsh Harbour (3 mi), business travellers, families who want a balcony over the marina. Repeat guests. Locals for Snappa's.

## Buyer portrait
Owner-skipper of a 38-50 ft catamaran or sportfish, 50s-60s, from Florida to the Carolinas, upper-middle to high income, comparing Conch Inn with Marsh Harbour's other marinas and with anchoring out. Fears: no slip, a surprise bill, being far from groceries and fuel. Chooses well when the rate is clear, the dock is walkable to town and the bar is on the water.
