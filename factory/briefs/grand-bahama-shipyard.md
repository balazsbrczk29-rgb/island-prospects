## Buyer portrait
Technical superintendents, fleet managers and newbuild/drydock planners at cruise lines and ship managers (US, Europe, Asia), planning windows 12 to 24 months out, comparing with yards in Europe, the US Gulf/East Coast and the Caribbean. They fear overruns and missed delivery dates (lost voyages) and safety incidents. They feel they chose well with hard specs, class approvals, a named PM and schedule, and a safety record. Desktop at the office, phone on board.
