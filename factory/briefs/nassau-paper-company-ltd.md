## Business analysis
B2B and walk-in supply house for restaurants, takeaways, canteens, offices, schools, cleaning crews, facilities teams, event organisers and retail counters. Found through repeat ordering, Facebook and drive-by on Carib Road. Buyers need: do they stock it, are they open, can I get a quote fast. Comparable US janitorial/paper distributors lead with categories, quote lists and reorder flows.
