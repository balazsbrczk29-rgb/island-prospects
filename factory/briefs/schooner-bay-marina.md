# Schooner Bay Marina, Schooner Bay, Great Abaco

Track A (site loads, marina page updated 2026-06, homepage frozen at a 2020 "we are open" banner). Tier A. Niche: marina.

## Facts
- Harbour is "not a conventional marina, but rather a working/living/recreational harbour and the centerpiece of our community". Published position N 26 09.529', W 077 10.633'. Harbour Master Albert Russel. Open 7:30 am to 4:30 pm daily, monitors VHF 16/77. Phone 242.366.2044, harbourmaster@schoonerbaybahamas.com. https://schoonerbaybahamas.com/community/the-marina/
- Diesel and gas at market rates; call ahead. Vessels up to 80 ft with 8 ft draw at MLW. Slips for owners and transient use. Non-gated village, visitors welcome by land and sea. Same page.
- 30 and 50 amp power, Wi-Fi, restrooms, showers; General Store for ice, marine supplies, snacks, laundry delivery; pet friendly; help with local transport. Same page.
- Transient dockage daily, weekly, monthly; daily rate $3.00 per ft, variable by length of stay. Covered, secured boat storage for residents. Marina handout PDF. Same page.
- Abaco page: protected harbour basin; "closest marina to the famed Hole in the Wall sport fishing grounds"; on the cruising route from the Abaco Cays to Nassau; lists 70 ft / 8 ft MLW (conflicts with 80 ft); fuel, power, internet, showers, laundry, provisions, overnight accommodations, bait and tackle, golf cart rentals; MHH 30-minute ride; Sandy Point Airstrip (MYAS) 20 minutes. https://schoonerbaybahamas.com/abaco-bahamas/
- Contact page: main line 1 (242) 699-3470; Sales June Russell 1 (242) 577-5577; Blackfly Lodge in the village. https://schoonerbaybahamas.com/contact/
- Home: Discovery Stay, 3 nights / 4 days hosted tour for prospective buyers; three steps to buying. https://schoonerbaybahamas.com
- Waterway Guide: 40 slips (5 transient), working channel 77, approach and dockside 8 ft, grocery, restaurant, marine store onsite; beach 0.5 mi. https://www.waterwayguide.com/marina/schooner-bay (third party, used only for slip count note in status, not on site)

## Current site audit
1. The homepage still leads with a 2020 "We are open" banner and a real-estate pitch; a boater sees nothing about slips, fuel or VHF.
2. The site contradicts itself: 80 ft on the Marina page, 70 ft on the Abaco page.
3. The Harbour Master's number (366-2044) differs from the main contact page (699-3470), with no explanation.
4. Specs are buried in paragraphs and a 2017 PDF, unreadable on a phone at sea.
5. No way to request a slip or fuel online; boaters must call during office hours.
6. Hole in the Wall, the strongest reason for sportfishermen to come, is one sentence on a secondary page.

## Buyer portrait
Sportfish and cruising-yacht owners (US Southeast, HNW, 40-70), their captains, running between the Abaco Cays and Nassau or heading to Hole in the Wall; plus second-home buyers who arrive by boat. Phone in hand, patchy signal. They fear an unknown entrance, no fuel, or no slip at day's end. They feel they chose well when specs, fuel and the Harbour Master's channel are clear before they leave the dock.

## Primary conversion
"Request a slip": email to the Harbour Master prefilled (boat, LOA, draft, dates, power, fuel). Secondary: call the Harbour Master, VHF 16.

## Brand truth / feel
A working harbour cut into Great Abaco's south-east shore, the closest marina to Hole in the Wall, with a village around the water. Feel: precise, salt-washed, sporting, sheltered, neighbourly.

## Positioning
For sportfishermen and cruisers running south Abaco, Schooner Bay is the protected harbour with fuel and a Harbour Master on 16, closest to Hole in the Wall, with a village and lodge ashore.
