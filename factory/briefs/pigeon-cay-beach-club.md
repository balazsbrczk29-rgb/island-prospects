## Big idea
A beach club run on the honor system: the first screen says "Pour your own drink. Take a kayak. Pay when you leave." and the page lets you work out your own tab, which always ends "Due today: $0".

## Direction
A09 Colour poster + P11 Straw market + T11 Neo-grotesk (Bricolage Grotesque + Figtree). Why: the photographs are too small to carry full screens, so type and colour fields carry the page and photographs appear as prints and wide bands at or below real size. It is the opposite of Greenwood (letterboxed film, Noto Serif Display, limestone) and of Fernandez Bay (horizontal journey).
