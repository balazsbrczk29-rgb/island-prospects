# Lighthouse Marina (id 81, batch 11, Tier A, Track A)

## Facts (htlighthousemarina.com)
- At the base of the historic Elbow Reef Lighthouse in Hope Town Harbour, Abaco. Established 1980. https://www.htlighthousemarina.com/
- 6 protected slips, vessels up to 60 ft LOA, up to 6 ft draft at low water. Deep water dockage.
- 3 fueling stations (2 gas, 1 diesel); "the only marine fuel station on Elbow Cay"; newly rebuilt fuel dock and docks; renovated gift shop.
- 30 and 50 amp service; full laundry (DIY, 7 days 8am-6pm, tokens at fuel dock and gift store) and hot showers; 60kW standby generator.
- Hauling and dry storage up to 10 tons; factory-trained technicians; engine servicing, bottom painting, fiberglass repair, brite work, detailing; Mercury Outboard sales and service; mechanic Monday-Friday.
- Ship's store: largest selection of rods and fishing tackle on island; bait (ballyhoo, squid, shrimp, minnows); souvenirs and clothing; snacks and soft drinks; ice; cold beer, wines, spirits, cigars (Lighthouse Marina Liquors, "reopening soon" on homepage).
- Boatyard Mon-Fri 8:00am-4:00pm, closed weekends and holidays. ## Buyer, conversion, positioning
Buyer: boaters in Hope Town (renters and owners of 20-40 ft centre consoles and twin-engine boats, cruisers to 60 ft) who need fuel, a slip, a mechanic, or a summer/hurricane-season dry storage for their boat; plus second-home owners on Elbow Cay. Primary conversion: Request a slip or service (email/phone, VHF 16). Positioning: the small, family marina at the foot of the Hope Town lighthouse with the only marine fuel on Elbow Cay, a boatyard for haul-out and storage, and a store stocked for fishing.

## Brand truth and feel
Brand truth: since 1980, the fuel dock and boatyard in the shadow of the candy-striped lighthouse, the one stop every Elbow Cay boat makes.
Feel: practical, salty, trusted, compact, nautical.
