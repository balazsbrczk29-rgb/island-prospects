## Current site audit
1. The homepage shows no phone, address or email; the contact details sit on a separate page.
2. Rates live in a pop-up table with a typo: Room 7 shows $500 with "$550.00" after tax, which does not match the 30% shown for every other room.
3. The Activities page uses a stock photo captioned "Barbuda, Antigua & Barbuda", not Harbour Island.
4. The name is split: the site says Runaway Hill Inn, listings still say Runaway Hill Club; the homepage says 10 acres, the rooms page 11.
5. Room pages do not compare rooms (where in the property, bed, view, rate), so guests open twelve pages to choose.
6. Nothing on the site explains how to get there (North Eleuthera airport, water taxi).

## Audience / buyer portrait
US East Coast couples and small family groups, household income $250k+, booking 2 to 6 months ahead on a phone, comparing against Rosewood Briland-style resorts and OTAs. They fear a tired inn that looks better in photos than in life. They feel they chose well when they can see exactly which room, which view, which rate, and talk to a person. Infinity House: multi-family groups and celebrations. Events: destination wedding couples.

Room enquiry by email (prefilled) to Reservations@runawayhill.com, plus a direct link to their booking engine. Secondary: call local or US number.

## Brand truth and feel
A 1946 English manor on Harbour Island's high ground, above Pink Sands Beach, where every room carries a name. Feel: personal, salt-washed, old-world, pink, unhurried.

## Positioning
For couples and families who want Harbour Island without a big resort, Runaway Hill Inn is the oceanfront manor house on the hill where you know your room by name and walk down the staircase to Pink Sands Beach.

## Design spec
- References studied: aman.com (Amanyara: full-bleed aerial, warm paper #f3eee7, tiny tracked serif labels, long quiet sections), belmond.com (editorial split rooms, italic serif accents, horizontal room carousels). Takeaway: patience, one photo per screen, rates and rooms as calm lists.
- Type: Castoro 400 + italic (display, English old-style for a 1946 manor) + Be Vietnam Pro 300/400/500 (body). Not used elsewhere in repo.
- Palette: paper #f8efe9 (pink sand), paper-2 #f1e2d9, ink #1f2838 (Cuban tile ink), tile #2b5175 (accent), glass #a9d0c5 (sea glass, Room 1 is the Sea Glass Suite), conch #e6bcae (pink beach), line #e4d2c8, muted #5b6271. Ink on paper 13:1; tile on paper 7.4:1.
- Hero: framed-image passe-partout: their real photo inside a pink-paper margin, name set at 11vw in Castoro across the lower frame edge, a small glass "key tag" card with 11 rooms + Infinity House and CTAs.
- Signature: "Down the hill" sticky scrollytelling: an SVG section of the descent (Main House, pool and sun deck, staircase, Pink Sands Beach) with a marker walking down as you scroll; each stop swaps a real photo. This shows the inn's descent from house to beach because Elite Traveler and the site say the pool is steps down from the Main House and the beach further down the staircase.
- Interaction 2: the Register: rooms listed like a guest register by name and number, filter by house, selection shows the room's real photo, bed and published rate.
- Interaction 3: stay estimator in the booking tabs (nights x published rate, 30% taxes, min 3 nights) composing the email.
- Motif: Cuban tile pattern drawn in SVG (the Jacqueline Jean tiles pre-date the inn), used once as a divider.
- Motion: hero mask reveal of photo + line-split name; one scroll idea per section; Lenis; reduced motion respected.
