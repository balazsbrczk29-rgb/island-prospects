## Buyer portrait
Procurement and project managers at government ministries, airport authorities (NAD), developers (resorts like Baha Mar), engineering consultants (Golder), homeowners for small paving. They compare BHM with other local contractors and foreign EPCs; they fear delay, weak QA/QC and Family Island logistics; they choose on certification, track record and capacity.
