# DESIGN PLAN (gen 2)

**Big idea (one sentence):** The page is an approach to land: scrolling descends onto BHM's own runway on Hog Cay, a strip of asphalt between two shades of Exuma water, and every chapter after it is the ground a visitor touches next (the runway, the road, the port, the water), each one a project BHM built.

**Direction:** A04 Swiss information grid + P12 Inagua salt + T04 Technical mono (direction.mjs, N1 static masthead, M9 line drawing). Why: procurement buyers trust order and evidence; a precise hairline grid with runway designators (10-28, 06-24) and ISO numbers set as data lets their drone photography be the only spectacle. Salt grey (#EDEDEB ground, #2F3135 ink) is the colour of fresh limestone base course and lets the turquoise in the photos carry colour. Accent petrol #0B5D73 (from the water in their photos), used only for the CTA and active filters. One asphalt field (#1D2023) for the materials section.

**Fonts:** Space Grotesk variable (display, 500-600, tight -0.035em), IBM Plex Sans variable (text), IBM Plex Mono 400/500 for real data only (runway numbers, ISO standards, phone, PO boxes). Self-hosted.
