# Swain's Cay Lodge, Mangrove Cay, Andros

Track A (site rebuilt Sept 2026 by Hotel Traffic Builders). Tier A. Niche: hospitality-stay (fishing lodge).

## Facts
- "Bonefishing Lodge and Island Hotel." White sand beaches, crystal-clear water; complimentary bicycles, kayaks and snorkelling gear. https://www.swainscaylodge.com
- Accommodations: Duplex Apartment (3 double bedrooms, kitchenette, dining area, furnished terrace); Ocean View Room, Ocean View Suite, Beach Front Suite (en-suite, air conditioning, furnished patio, Wi-Fi). Same.
- Reefside Restaurant & Bar, right on the beach, authentic Bahamian cuisine, breakfast or dinner, home-cooked. Same.
- Activities: fishing, watersports (kayak the bights, snorkel), bike rides around Mangrove Cay, eco-tourism. Same.
- Specials: Bahamas residents fly/cruise free from Nassau; private pilots fee credits ($300 for 4+ nights, $150 for 2 nights); air credits for US/Canada/Europe/Florida residents (dated offers). Same.
- Fishing: positioned on Mangrove Cay for the South Bight or the Middle Bight of Andros; handpicked local independent guides; wade-able bonefish flat right off the beach. Deep sea: outside the Andros Barrier Reef the depth drops to 6,000 ft: wahoo, dolphin fish, tuna, billfish. Reef: Andros Barrier Reef, 140 miles long, less than one mile from shore in most places; yellowtail snapper, Nassau grouper, triggerfish, barracuda, reef sharks, porgy. Spearfishing tours and lessons. https://www.swainscaylodge.com/activities/bahamas-fishing/
- 2026-2027 season (March 2026 to April 2027): Day Trip $975 per boat (1 or 2 anglers, lunch and transport, lodging not included); half day a la carte; non-angler rate $450. Package includes guide and boat (up to 2 per boat), accommodations, all meals, water, airport and dock transfers (Middle or South Bight), Wi-Fi, amenities, levies. Not included: airfare to/from Nassau, alcohol and sodas, gratuities, gear and flies, 10% VAT outside packages, fishing licences (license.gooutdoorsbahamas.com). 50% deposit within 21 days; balance 90 days before arrival. Gratuities recommended $125 per day per boat per guide, $35 per night per guest for lodge staff. Check-in after 2 pm; flexible check-out. Capacity 12 units, max 26 guests. Phones +1 (242) 422-5018 / +1 (242) 369-0296, fish@swainscaylodge.com. Same.

## Buyer portrait
Serious fly anglers (US, 40-70, often repeat groups of 2-6 friends, father-son trips, couples with a non-angling partner), comparing with other Andros lodges (Andros South, Mangrove Cay Club, Bair's). They fear bad guides, wasted tides and hidden costs. They choose well when they see where they will fish, who guides, what is included and the full cost picture.
