# Black Sound Marina (Green Turtle Cay, Abaco): SKIPPED

## Why skipped
Cannot confirm the marina still operates.
- BahamasLocal listing marks it "Business and/or Location Closed Down - Permanently: Destroyed in Hurricane Dorian". https://www.bahamaslocal.com/showlisting/16826/Black_Sound_Marina.html
- Its old domain blacksoundmarinagreenturtle.com no longer resolves (DNS failure, 2026-11-05).
- All specs found are old: noonsite listing dated 2004 (15 slips, min depth 2 m, VHF 16, water, electricity, showers, laundry, golf carts, 5 min walk to New Plymouth; phone (242) 365-4531, fax 365-4567, royboles@bellsouth.net). https://www.noonsite.com/business/black-sound-marina/
- The same phone (242) 365-4531 is listed for Leeward Yacht Club in a 2018 guide, so the number may now belong to another business. https://www.sportfishingmag.com/abacos-destination-guide/
- A Facebook page exists ("proudly serving Green Turtle Cay for over 20 years... closest marina to the town of New Plymouth") but could not be read (unsupported by our tools) to check for recent posts. https://www.facebook.com/blacksoundmarina/
- A 2025 real-estate blog still names it among nearby marinas, but in passing, without details. https://www.abacobahamas.com/blog/posts/2025/10/23/abaco-marinas/

## Recommendation for Balazs
If a visit or call confirms the marina reopened after Dorian, re-queue it: it is a strong Track B marina prospect (closest marina to New Plymouth, port-of-entry island).
