## Current site audit
1. Keyword-stuffed browser title on every page ("Bahamas Boutique Hotel, Vacation Rental Bahamas, Bahamian Accommodations").
2. Built from image tables: headings are pictures, so Google and phones cannot read them.
3. No address or phone on the homepage; plain http in the BHA directory.
4. Ten beautifully named gem rooms shown as tiny thumbnails with no way to compare.
5. The 70 ft ridge between lake and ocean, your best story, is one sentence.

## Audience / conversion
US couples, business travellers, airport-adjacent stays, families (kids under 12 free). Primary: Check dates (innRoad engine). Secondary: email about a specific room.

## Strategy
Pages: index, rooms.html (gem selector, full table), stay.html (amenities, policies, getting here). SEO primary "boutique hotel West Bay Street Nassau"; secondary "hotel near Nassau airport", "Lake Killarney hotel", "boutique hotel near Cable Beach".

## Buyer portrait
Couples 30-60 and business travellers from the US East Coast; compare with Cable Beach resorts and Airbnb; fear noise, airport hassle, hidden fees. Chose well when the room is named and seen, the price range is clear, and the airport is 5 minutes.

## Brand truth / feel
Ten gem-named rooms on a ridge 70 ft up, between Lake Killarney and the Atlantic, five minutes from the airport. Feel: elevated, intimate, jewel-like, breezy, composed.

## Positioning
For travellers who want a quiet, personal base in Nassau close to the airport, Ocean West is the ten-room hotel on the ridge above West Bay Street with views of both lake and ocean.
