## Current site audit (it is a modern 2026 site; pitch is precision and conversion)
1. Two marina phone links dial different numbers than the ones printed (786-535-7322 dials 833-717-6499; 242.333.2325 dials 242-333-2500).
2. "40-slip" in Google's description, "40+ slips" on the page; captains want one number.
3. Max LOA (190 ft) is in prose; no draft, beam or channel info and no fit check; power spec "408V" looks like a typo.
4. Contact info for the resort is not visible on the homepage (only reservations line inside the booking block).
5. Homepage leads with a dated pickleball event rather than the two reasons people come: the marina and the rooms.

## Audience / conversion
Yacht owners and captains (US East Coast), Harbour Island couples, villa groups. Primary: Reserve a stay (booking engine) and Reserve a slip; secondary call 833.717.6499.

## Strategy
Pages index, marina.html, stay.html. SEO primary "Harbour Island marina and resort"; secondary "port of entry Harbour Island", "Harbour Island hotel waterfront", "yacht slip Harbour Island 190 ft", "Sip Sip Harbour Island".

## Buyer portrait
Owners of 60-190 ft yachts and their captains, plus affluent couples; compare Valentines and Harbour Island Marina; fear customs hassle, power mismatch, unreachable dockmaster. Chose well with port of entry, exact power, named dockmaster.

## Brand truth / feel
Harbour Island's resort where the yacht and the room share one dock: a port of entry with 17 rooms behind it. Feel: barefoot, polished, pink-sand, nautical, gracious.
