Every Cheryl's tour is private. Add what you like, leave out what you don't, and the day is planned around you. Drinks are included; most historic sites charge $3 to $5 entry.

  • Wed, Fri 11:30am

    History

    The forts

    Wed, Fri 11:30am

    Fort Charlotte, Fort Fincastle and the 66 Steps, Fort Montague: built in the 1700s against Spanish ships and pirates. Wednesday and Friday at 11:30am, a costumed re-enactment ends with the noon cannon.

  • $10 a bowl, shares 4

    Food and rum

    Conch demonstration

    $10 a bowl, shares 4

    Conch 101: out of the shell, cleaned, every part explained, then fresh conch salad.

  • Food and rum

    The Fish Fry

    Lunch with the locals: 15 to 20 restaurants, seafood fried, steamed or grilled.

  • Food and rum

    Rum Cake Factory

    Free tastings: piña colada, amaretto, banana, cinnamon. Over 1,000 cakes baked a day.

  • Food and rum

    Rum distillery

    A history tour of one of Nassau's oldest restaurants, ending at the bar with drinks made from its rum.

  • Food and rum

    Bahamian candy store

    An authentic Bahamian sugar rush. Ask about the candy store tour.

  • History

    The Cloisters

    Built by French monks in the 14th century, now on Paradise Island, with the view back across to the mainland.

  • History

    The Bat Cave

    Lucayan history, pirate legends, fruit bats today, and the famous kissing chair for a photo.

  • 45 min

    History

    Clifton Heritage Park

    45 min

    A 45-minute walk through the history of the slave village, with ATV, beach, lunch or shore snorkel options.

  • junkanoo

    Culture and shopping

    Junkanoo World Museum

    Drums, horns, cowbells and the costumes. Try them on, play the drums, stay for conch fritters and jerk chicken.

  • Culture and shopping

    Straw market and hand craft

    Souvenirs made from conch shell, coconut and straw, with help knowing a fair price.

  • $18 pp, under 4 free

    Culture and shopping

    Ardastra Gardens and Zoo

    $18 pp, under 4 free

    Four acres of gardens, native birds and flowers, and the flamingo show every morning at 10:30.

  • Snorkel $20

    Beach and water

    Time at Love Beach

    Snorkel $20

    A secluded beach away from downtown: music, food, drinks. Day package with lunch for six or more.

  • About 6 hours

    Beach and water

    Snorkel cruise to Rose Island

    About 6 hours

    About six hours: a coral reef, a secluded beach eight miles east of downtown, a BBQ lunch cooked at anchor. Hotel transfers included from most hotels.

  • Beach and water

    Fishing and snorkelling

    Sightseeing by boat, reef fishing, optional snorkelling near a private island, Bahama Mama on board.

  • Beach and water

    Blue Lagoon beach day

    A boat ride to Blue Lagoon Island: grilled lunch, inner tubes, the aqua park, dolphins and sea lions to watch.

  • Beach and water

    Dolphin swim

    Meet the dolphins in an ocean lagoon: the kiss, the hug, the foot push. Sea lion programmes too.

  • $400 adult, $365 age 5-11

    Beach and water

    Pieces of 8: the swimming pigs

    $400 adult, $365 age 5-11

    A 38 ft Fountain powerboat to five Exuma cays in a day: the pigs, rock iguanas, nurse sharks, a sandbar, lunch. Full refund 72 hours ahead.

  • 9am to 5pm

    Beach and water

    Exuma day trip

    9am to 5pm

    9am to 5pm: stingrays, sharks and the pigs. A plane and boat combination for anyone who gets seasick.

  • Beach and water

    ATV adventure and beach

    Off-road through Clifton's trails, then the beach with a cocktail. One rider per ATV, driver's licence needed, helmets and gloves provided.

  • Beach and water

    Parasailing and jet ski

    Up above the harbour, or fast across it.

  • Beach and water

    Glass bottom boat

    The reef below, without getting wet.
