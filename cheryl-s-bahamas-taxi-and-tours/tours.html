pick your stops.

Every Cheryl's tour is private. Add what you like, leave out what you don't, and the day is planned around you. Drinks are included; most historic sites charge $3 to $5 entry.

  • The forts and the 66 Steps

    Fort Charlotte, Fort Fincastle, Fort Montague: built in the 1700s. Re-enactment and the noon cannon on Wednesdays and Fridays.

    Wed, Fri 11:30am

  • The Cloisters

    Built by French monks in the 14th century, now on Paradise Island, looking back across the harbour.

  • The Bat Cave

    Lucayan history, pirate legends, fruit bats today, and the kissing chair for a photo.

  • Clifton Heritage Park

    A 45-minute walk through the history of the slave village, with ATV, beach or snorkel options.

    45 min

  • Conch demonstration

    Out of the shell, cleaned, every part explained, then fresh conch salad.

    $10 a bowl, shares 4

  • The Fish Fry

    Lunch with the locals: 15 to 20 restaurants, seafood fried, steamed or grilled.

  • Rum Cake Factory

    Free tastings: piña colada, amaretto, banana, cinnamon. Over a thousand cakes baked a day.

  • Rum distillery

    A history tour of one of Nassau's oldest restaurants, ending at the bar.

  • Bahamian candy store

    An authentic Bahamian sugar rush. Ask about the candy store tour.

  • Junkanoo World Museum

    Drums, horns, cowbells and the costumes. Try them on, then conch fritters on site.

  • Straw market and hand craft

    Souvenirs of conch shell, coconut and straw, with help knowing a fair price.

  • Ardastra Gardens and Zoo

    Four acres of gardens and native birds; the flamingo show is at 10:30 every morning.

    $18 pp, under 4 free

  • Time at Love Beach

    A secluded beach away from downtown: music, food, drinks. Lunch package for six or more.

    Snorkel $20

  • Snorkel cruise to Rose Island

    A coral reef, a secluded beach, a BBQ lunch cooked at anchor. Hotel transfers included.

    About 6 hours

  • Pieces of 8: the swimming pigs

    Five Exuma cays by 38 ft powerboat: the pigs, iguanas, nurse sharks, a sandbar, lunch.

    $400 adult, $365 age 5-11

  • Exuma day trip

    Stingrays, sharks and the pigs, with a plane and boat option for anyone who gets seasick.

    9am to 5pm

  • Blue Lagoon beach day

    A boat to Blue Lagoon Island: grilled lunch, the aqua park, dolphins and sea lions.

  • Dolphin swim

    In an ocean lagoon: the kiss, the hug, the foot push. Sea lion programmes too.

  • Fishing and snorkelling

    Sightseeing by boat, reef fishing and optional snorkelling near a private island.

  • ATV adventure and beach

    Off-road through Clifton's trails, then the beach. Driver's licence needed; helmets provided.

  • Parasailing and jet ski

    Up above the harbour, or fast across it.

  • Glass bottom boat

    The reef below, without getting wet.
